## 1. Haulers and local businesses: first email (founding-member offer, from 10/21/2026)

AIDA: Attention = subject about the 10 free calls; Interest = a real {City} disposal hassle from the city page plus the hauler's verified local details; Desire = only-you leads and zero risk; Action = reply yes. Keep under about 150 words, no em-dashes, and don't state competitors' prices.

**Subject:** 10 free customer calls for {Org} in {City}

Hi {Name},

I run HowToDumpIt, a free guide helping {City} neighbors dispose of junk responsibly, keep usable items out of the landfill and support local businesses: {city page URL}

{City-specific disposal hassle}, so many readers would rather call {verified local detail}, like yours.

I'd like {Org} to be our {City} Featured partner, the one clearly labeled top spot. Ready-to-hire local customers would reach only you, while lead sites share each lead with several competitors.

Zero risk: as a founding member, your first 10 calls or emails are free, then $2 each, paid only once leads are coming in. No upfront payment, card or contract. You get your own tracking number forwarding to your phone, plus a monthly report: https://howtodumpit.com/become-a-featured-partner/

Reply yes and I'll send the exact wording for your approval.

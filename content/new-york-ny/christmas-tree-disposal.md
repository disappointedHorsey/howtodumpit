# Christmas Tree Pickup and Drop-Off in New York City (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Set a bare real tree at the curb on your weekly recycling and compost day and the Department of Sanitation (DSNY) collects it for free, in any week of the year. If you can't carry it down yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below. NYC Parks has not posted its 2026-27 Mulchfest drop-off dates yet.

*Last verified November 16, 2026, against official New York City pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: DSNY Curbside Collection {#curbside}

- **What:** DSNY collects live Christmas trees and wreaths at the curb as part of Curbside Composting. You do not need an appointment. ([NYC311: Christmas Tree Disposal](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02489))
- **When:** every week on your recycling day; compost goes out the same day. DSNY's December 24, 2025 notice says the old limited tree schedule is gone, so a tree can go out right after Christmas or weeks later. ([DSNY: Special Christmas Tree Collection Is No Longer Special](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/news/25-045/special-christmas-tree-collection-is-no-longer-special))
- **Set-out rules:** take off the stand, lights, ornaments and tinsel, leave the tree unwrapped, and place it next to your other compostable material. A tree with anything still attached can't be composted.
- **Holiday week:** Friday, December 25, 2026, is on the City's 2026 sanitation holiday list, so Friday collection that week may change. ([NYC311: Collection Schedule](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01801)) Check the [DSNY Holiday Schedule](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/collection/residents/holiday-schedule.page) close to the date.
- **The catch:** you carry the tree down and out yourself, needles and all, and it waits for your recycling day.

## Drop It Off Yourself: NYC Parks Mulchfest {#mulchfest}

**2026-27 dates: not published.** On November 16, 2026, the official Mulchfest page still showed last season. ([NYC Parks: Mulchfest](https://www.nyc.gov/mulchfest))

Last season (2025-26), NYC Parks took trees from December 26, 2025, through January 11, 2026, during park hours at sites in all five boroughs. On Chipping Weekend, January 10 and 11, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., chipping sites handed out free bags of mulch. Parks asked people to remove all lights, ornaments and netting first.

**The catch:** you have to get the tree to a park site, and the window lasts about two weeks.

## Artificial Trees {#artificial}

Take an artificial tree apart. The metal base and trunk (pole) go out with metal, glass, plastic and cartons on your recycling day, and the rest goes out as trash on a regular collection day. ([NYC311: Christmas Tree Disposal](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02489))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When is Christmas tree pickup in NYC for 2026-27?

There is no special pickup week. DSNY takes bare trees on your weekly recycling day all year, so the first recycling day after the holidays works, and so does any later one.

### Do I need to book DSNY to collect my tree?

No booking is needed. Put the tree out with your compost on recycling day.

### Can my tree go out in a plastic bag?

No. DSNY only composts trees and wreaths that are unwrapped and have nothing attached.

### When is Mulchfest 2026-27?

NYC Parks had not announced it when we checked on November 16, 2026. Last season ran from December 26 to January 11.

### Who can take a Christmas tree out of my apartment?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

# Christmas Tree Recycling and Pickup in Nashville, TN (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Last season, NDOT and Metro Parks ran free Christmas tree drop-off at 14 sites from December 26, 2025, through February 13, 2026, and NDOT's brush crew also took trees on its regular brush schedule, with no special tree pickups. NDOT had not posted 2026-27 drop-off sites or dates when we checked on November 17, 2026. If your brush date is weeks away, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 17, 2026, against official Metro Nashville (NDOT and Waste Services) pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: NDOT Christmas Tree Recycling Program {#tree-recycling}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 17, 2026, on the [NDOT newsroom](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/news/ndot-announces-christmas-tree-recycling-program-0) and [Brush and Leaf Collection page](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/right-way-maintenance/brush-and-yard-waste-collection)).
- **Last season (2025-26):** NDOT, with Metro Parks, opened 14 drop-off sites from December 26, 2025, through February 13, 2026: Cane Ridge Park, Una Recreation Center, Whitfield Park, Cedar Hill Park, Two Rivers Park, Joelton Community Center, Sevier Park, Richland Park, Elmington Park, Edwin Warner Park, Lakewood City Hall, Frederick Douglass Park, and both Living Earth locations (1511 Elm Hill Pike and 6401 Centennial Blvd.). ([NDOT: Christmas Tree Recycling Program, December 17, 2025](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/news/ndot-announces-christmas-tree-recycling-program-0))
- **Prep (last season):** remove wooden stands, watering bowls, ornaments, lights, wire, string and other decorations. No artificial trees, and nothing else may be dumped at the sites. ([NDOT release](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/news/ndot-announces-christmas-tree-recycling-program-0))
- **The catch:** this season's sites and dates aren't posted yet, the program is drop-off only, and you need a vehicle that fits the tree.

## Free: NDOT Brush Collection {#brush-collection}

- **How it works:** NDOT said its brush collection crew can pick up Christmas trees as part of its regular schedule, and there are no special pickups for trees. ([NDOT release](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/news/ndot-announces-christmas-tree-recycling-program-0))
- **Who gets it:** single-family homes in Metro's Urban and General Services Districts, four times a year along 12 routes. Not on private streets or in satellite cities such as Forest Hills, Berry Hill and Belle Meade. Set brush out before your area's start date where your garbage is collected; a route normally takes one to three weeks. ([NDOT: Brush and Leaf Collection](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/right-way-maintenance/brush-and-yard-waste-collection))
- **No trash cart:** Metro says all yard waste is banned from trash collection and can't go in your trash cart or dumpster. ([Brush and Leaf Collection](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/right-way-maintenance/brush-and-yard-waste-collection))
- **2026-27 brush start dates after Christmas** (published): Area 6, December 29, 2026; Area 7, January 5, 2027; Area 8, January 8; Area 9, January 13; Area 10, January 25; Area 11, January 28; Area 12, February 2; Area 1, February 8; Area 2, February 12, 2027. Areas 3, 4 and 5 start December 7, 11 and 18, 2026, and their next dates are February 25, March 2 and March 10, 2027. Find your area in the Nashville Waste Wizard. ([NDOT: 2026-2027 Brush Collection Schedule (PDF)](https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-2027_BrushFlyer.pdf))
- **The catch:** depending on your area, the next brush pass after Christmas may be late February or March.

## Drop It Off Yourself: Living Earth and Convenience Centers {#drop-off}

- **Living Earth (natural trees as yard waste):** Metro says Davidson County residents can drop off brush and other yard waste free at the Living Earth sites, 1511 Elm Hill Pike and 6401 Centennial Blvd. (trailers are charged a fee). Hours vary through the year; call Living Earth Nashville at 615-730-7238. ([NDOT: Brush and Leaf Collection](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/right-way-maintenance/brush-and-yard-waste-collection)) Both sites were also on last season's tree drop-off list.
- **Artificial trees:** NDOT said artificial trees can go to any of Metro's four convenience centers for disposal. ([NDOT release](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/transportation/news/ndot-announces-christmas-tree-recycling-program-0)) Fees and limits are in Metro's fee table, and all convenience centers are closed on Metro holidays. ([Metro Waste Services: Convenience Centers](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/waste-services/convenience-centers))

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Metro Waste Services won't collect trash or recycling on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, 2026; Friday routes are collected Monday, December 28. Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, is collected as normal. For New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027, Friday routes are collected Monday, January 4, 2027. ([Metro Waste Services: Curbside Trash and Recycling](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/waste-services/trash-and-recycling-services))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where can I recycle my Christmas tree in Nashville?

Last season, NDOT and Metro Parks ran 14 free drop-off sites from December 26, 2025, through February 13, 2026. The 2026-27 sites and dates were not posted as of November 17, 2026.

### Does Nashville pick up Christmas trees at the curb?

Only through regular brush collection. NDOT's brush crew can take a tree on your area's normal brush schedule, and there are no special tree pickups.

### Can I put my Christmas tree in the trash cart in Nashville?

No. Metro bans all yard waste from trash collection, carts and dumpsters.

### Is Nashville trash pickup different during Christmas week 2026?

Only Friday routes change. Friday, December 25, 2026, collections happen Monday, December 28, and Friday, January 1, 2027, collections happen Monday, January 4.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Nashville right now?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

# Junk Disposal in Las Vegas, NV: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Las Vegas, Nevada, item by item. The City of Las Vegas franchises residential trash to **Republic Services**, which picks up **bulk items every other week** at the curb on your regular service day. That service is **included in the City's single-family residential rate**. **Fridges, freezers and window AC units** (anything with freon) **can't go out with bulk pickup**. Take them to a Republic transfer station or landfill (fee charged). **TVs and household electronics** are taken **at no charge** at Republic's Cheyenne and Henderson transfer stations, and paint and chemicals go to Republic's **rotating household hazardous waste drop-off**. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call Republic Services at 702-735-5151 before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Every-other-week curbside bulk pickup; soiled mattresses must be wrapped in plastic |
| Couch / furniture | Every-other-week curbside bulk pickup (items 6 feet long or less, at the curb); donate if it's still usable |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Not taken at curbside bulk pickup; drop off at a Republic transfer station or landfill, for a fee |
| TV / computer | Free drop-off for TVs and household electronics at the Cheyenne or Henderson transfer station (7 a.m. to 3 p.m., daily) |
| Paint / chemicals | Republic household hazardous waste drop-off, Wednesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., rotating between North Las Vegas and Henderson; dried-out latex paint can go in the trash |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Put it out on your **bulk pickup day**, which comes **every other week** on your regular trash day. It must be **at the curb**, and a **soiled mattress must be wrapped in plastic**. ([Republic Services: Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf))

### Step by Step: Every-Other-Week Bulk Pickup

1. **Find your bulk week.** Republic runs bulk pickup on an **"A" or "B"** every-other-week schedule tied to your service day. Download your day's **2026 bulk calendar** from Republic's Southern Nevada page, or enter your address in Republic's schedule lookup (no account needed). ([Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada))
2. **Know what counts.** Bulk items are items that **don't fit inside your cart**. Republic sends a **third truck every other week** to collect them. ([Republic Services: Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf)) City code defines a bulky item as a large household item that doesn't fit in your cart, **including appliances, carpeting and mattresses**, that a two-person crew can handle. ([Las Vegas Municipal Code, Chapter 9.08](https://library.municode.com/nv/las_vegas/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT9HESA_CH9.08COMASOWAREMA))
3. **Set it out right.** Republic's rules:
   - Items must be **no more than 6 feet long**.
   - **No loose trash**; everything must be contained.
   - **No protruding nails or screws** in wood.
   - **Soiled mattresses must be wrapped in plastic.**
   - Tree branches must be **bundled and tied**, **50 pounds or less** per bundle.
   - Items must be **at the curb**. Crews **won't remove items from private property**.
   - **No refrigerators or anything containing Freon.**

   ([Republic Services: Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf))
4. **Smaller loose items:** On bulk day, City code lets you use a container other than your cart if it holds **less than 36 gallons** and weighs **no more than 50 pounds** with its contents. Heavier items go **next to your carts or at the curb**. ([Las Vegas Municipal Code, Chapter 9.08](https://library.municode.com/nv/las_vegas/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT9HESA_CH9.08COMASOWAREMA))

**What it costs:** The City's 2026-2027 rate sheet lists **$20.02 a month** for single-family homes and duplexes (and units with individual service) for **weekly automated trash and recycling plus every-other-week bulky pickup**. ([City of Las Vegas: Republic Services Rates, July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/finance/Finance/Franchise/Republic_Services/Republic-Services-Rates-Effective-July1-2026-June-30-2027-CLV.pdf)) No separate per-item charge for regular bulk pickup appears on the pages we checked.

**Live in an apartment?** Complexes without individual curbside service are billed differently on the City's rate sheet, and the every-other-week bulky pickup on that sheet is for homes and units with individual curbside service. Ask your property manager how bulky items are handled.

**Not in the City of Las Vegas?** Republic also serves Clark County, North Las Vegas, Henderson and Laughlin, and posts 2026 bulk calendars for each. ([Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada)) We didn't find an official mattress recycling or mattress drop-off program for Las Vegas.

*Sources: [Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada), [Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf), [City of Las Vegas rate sheet](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/finance/Finance/Franchise/Republic_Services/Republic-Services-Rates-Effective-July1-2026-June-30-2027-CLV.pdf) and [Las Vegas Municipal Code, Chapter 9.08](https://library.municode.com/nv/las_vegas/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT9HESA_CH9.08COMASOWAREMA). Checked 10/23/2026. Rules change, so confirm on the official page before you go.*

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** Couches, chairs and other furniture go out on your **every-other-week bulk day**, using the same set-out rules as mattresses (at the curb, 6 feet long or less, no protruding nails or screws). ([Republic Services: Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf)) Republic's general bulk guidance asks you to leave items like sofas and chairs **fully assembled**. ([Republic Services: Bulk Waste](https://www.republicservices.com/residents/recycling-and-solid-waste/bulk-waste))

**Too much for the curb?** You can haul it to a Republic facility. Republic asks you to **call for current rates** and accepted materials:

- **Cheyenne Transfer Station:** 315 W. Cheyenne Ave., North Las Vegas, NV 89030. Phone 702-599-5624.
- **Henderson Transfer Station:** 560 Cape Horn Dr., Henderson, NV 89011. Phone 702-599-5768. Open 7 a.m. to 3 p.m., Monday to Sunday.
- **Apex Landfill:** 13550 North US Highway 93, Las Vegas, NV 89165. Phone 702-599-5920. Open 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday to Sunday.

([Republic Services: Southern Nevada Service Information](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada/service-information)) The Cheyenne Transfer Station is open **7 a.m. to 3 p.m., seven days a week**. ([Republic Services: Cheyenne Transfer Station](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Cheyenne-Transfer-Station.pdf)) We didn't find resident drop-off fees on an official page; call before you go.

**Still usable?** The Southern Nevada Health District suggests donating or consigning furniture in good condition, and lists Goodwill Industries, Habitat for Humanity, Las Vegas Rescue Mission, Salvation Army, Vietnam Veterans of America and Donation Town as donation organizations (not an endorsement). It also suggests asking the store to take your old furniture when it delivers the new one. ([Southern Nevada Health District: Household Hazardous Waste Management](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Refrigerators and anything containing freon are not taken at curbside bulk pickup.** ([Republic Services: Bulk Item Setout](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Southern-NV-Bulk-item-setout.pdf))

**Fridges, freezers and window AC units** are accepted **for a fee** at:

- **Henderson ("South Las Vegas") Transfer Station:** 560 Cape Horn Dr., Henderson
- **Cheyenne ("North Las Vegas") Transfer Station:** 315 W. Cheyenne Ave., North Las Vegas
- **Apex Landfill:** 13550 U.S. Highway 93, Las Vegas
- **Laughlin Landfill:** about half a mile south of Highway 163 on Needles Highway, Laughlin

Window units can be **no larger than 2 feet by 3 feet**. ([Republic Services: Refrigerators, Freezers & Window A/C Units](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/Resi-Refrigerators-Freezers-Window-AC.pdf)) Republic doesn't publish the fee amount on these pages. Call the facility or Republic at 702-735-5151 for the current charge. ([Republic Services: Southern Nevada Service Information](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada/service-information))

**Non-freon kitchen appliances:** The **Cheyenne and Henderson transfer stations** accept **ovens, microwave ovens, jet stream ovens and general kitchen appliances at no charge**, 7 days a week, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. ([Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada)) City code lists appliances as bulky items, but Republic's pages don't say which non-freon appliances (washers, dryers) it takes at the curb. Ask Republic before you set one out. If you do, Republic's general bulk guidance says to **remove doors** from appliances like refrigerators and washing machines. ([Republic Services: Bulk Waste](https://www.republicservices.com/residents/recycling-and-solid-waste/bulk-waste))

**Other options:** The Southern Nevada Health District notes there can be **fees for freon removal**, suggests asking the store to haul away your old fridge when it delivers the new one, and suggests donating working appliances. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/)) Nevada's environmental agency says there's **generally a fee** for appliances containing refrigerants. ([NDEP: Where Can I Recycle](https://www.ndep.nv.gov/nevada-recycles/recycle/where-can-i-recycle))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Free drop-off:** Republic's **Cheyenne Transfer Station** (315 W. Cheyenne Ave., North Las Vegas) and **Henderson Transfer Station** (560 Cape Horn Dr., Henderson) accept **TVs and household electronics at no charge**, **7 days a week, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m.** ([Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada))

**Other options from official lists:**

- **Best Buy:** Nevada's Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP) says most Best Buy stores recycle TVs **for a fee**, with a **limit of two TVs per household per day** and size limits (tube TVs under 32 inches; flat-panel LCD, plasma and LED TVs under 50 inches; portable TVs). Check with your store first.
- **Clark County special-waste recyclers listed by NDEP:** Nevada State Recycle (1816 Industrial Road #4, Las Vegas, 702-979-1280), Dunn & Son Electronic Recycler (3050 Westwood Dr., Las Vegas, 702-604-0810) and The Blind Center (1001 N. Bruce St., Las Vegas, 702-998-4901; **no CRT TVs**).

([NDEP: Where Can I Recycle](https://www.ndep.nv.gov/nevada-recycles/recycle/where-can-i-recycle)) The Southern Nevada Health District also lists local drop-off and recycling locations for TVs, monitors, computers and phones (not an endorsement) and advises you to **call first to ask about fees**. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/)) Store and recycler programs and fees change, so call before you go.

Erase personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Republic Services runs a **household hazardous waste (HHW) drop-off** for Southern Nevada households. It **rotates between two sites**, so check the calendar for which site is open that week:

- **North Valley:** 333 W. Gowan Road, North Las Vegas, NV 89032. **Enter on Gowan Rd. through Gate B only.**
- **South Valley:** 560 Cape Horn Drive, Henderson, NV 89011.
- **Hours:** **Wednesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.**, on the dates shown for each site. **Closed all holidays.**
- **Accepted (in structurally sound, sealed containers):** paint, oil, gasoline (**5-gallon limit**), batteries, pesticides and fertilizer, flammable liquids, aerosol cans, drain and oven cleaners, adhesives and pool chemicals.
- **Limits:** **Household waste only**, up to **40 pounds dry or 15 gallons liquid** (measured by container size).

All of the above is from Republic's [2026 Household Hazardous Waste Calendar](https://www.republicservices.com/sites/default/files/legacy_documents/Muni/Southern%20Nevada/2026-HHW-Calendar.pdf). For items not on that list, Republic points you to the Southern Nevada Health District. The calendar doesn't list a fee. Ask Republic at 702-735-5151 if you're unsure whether you're eligible.

**Leftover latex paint:** NDEP says latex paint **can be dried out** (kitty litter speeds it up) and, **once dry, put in the trash**; wet paint goes to an HHW collection. ([NDEP: Where Can I Recycle](https://www.ndep.nv.gov/nevada-recycles/recycle/where-can-i-recycle)) The Southern Nevada Health District gives the same tip: let it dry in the sun with the lid off, or add kitty litter, and once it's solid put it in your residential trash can. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

**Oil-based paint, thinners and solvents** are hazardous (flammable). Take them to the HHW drop-off. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

**Health District rules for any HHW:** Read the label, keep products in their **original containers**, and **never mix chemicals**. **Never** pour them on the ground, down a drain, toilet, gutter or storm drain, and **never** put them in your trash or recycling cart. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

**Medications** aren't on Republic's HHW list. The Health District lists Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department (LVMPD) locations, CVS, Walgreens and local pharmacies as drop-off options. ([Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

Questions? Call Republic Services Southern Nevada at **702-735-5151** (Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Saturday, 8 a.m. to noon) or the Southern Nevada Health District at (702) 759-0600. ([Republic Services Southern Nevada](https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/southern-nevada), [Southern Nevada Health District](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/permits-and-regulations/solid-waste-compliance/household-hazardous-waste-management/))

*Rules, hours, fees and schedules change. Confirm on the linked official page or with Republic Services before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

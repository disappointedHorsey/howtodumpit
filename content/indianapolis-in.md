# Junk Disposal in Indianapolis, IN: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana, item by item. The City picks up **heavy trash** such as mattresses and furniture **once a month**, but you're limited to **two heavy/bulky items per month**. **Appliances with freon** (fridges, freezers, AC units) need a **separate pickup** arranged through the Mayor's Action Center. **Electronics can't go in the trash at all** under Indiana law, and household hazardous waste goes to the City's Saturday **ToxDrop** sites. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622 before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Heavy trash pickup, once a month, next to your trash cart (lay it flat); limit two heavy items per month |
| Couch / furniture | Heavy trash pickup, same two-item monthly limit; or the Citizens' Transfer Station on Saturdays ($2 car, $5 SUV/pickup, cash) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Contact the Mayor's Action Center (317-327-4622 or RequestIndy) to arrange a freon pickup; allow 10 business days |
| TV / computer | Not allowed in the trash or heavy trash (Indiana law). ToxDrop takes TVs 27 inches or less and up to five computer systems per visit; RecycleForce also takes electronics |
| Paint / chemicals | ToxDrop on the first, second and fourth Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. (Marion County residents only; 20 gallons liquid or 75 pounds solid per visit) |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Put it at the curb **next to your trash cart** on your **heavy trash pickup day**, which comes **once a month**. Lay it **flat on the ground**, not leaning on anything. It counts toward your limit of **two heavy/bulky items per month**.

### Step by Step: Heavy Trash Pickup

1. Find your heavy trash day. Enter your address in the City's [Find Your Trash Pickup Day](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-pickup) lookup. The heavy trash day varies by address and comes once a month.
2. Count your items. You can put out **up to two** heavy/bulky items, and you're **limited to two per month**. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))
3. Place **box springs and mattresses flat on the ground**, not leaning on other objects, at the curb **next to your trash cart**. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))
4. If your item isn't acceptable, your service provider will leave a **tag** explaining how to meet the pickup requirements. ([Find Your Trash Pickup Day](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-pickup))
5. On observed holidays, trash, heavy trash and recycling pickup **slide one day later**. Holidays that fall on a weekend aren't observed. ([Find Your Trash Pickup Day](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-pickup))
6. Missed heavy trash pickup? Report it through the [Mayor's Action Center](https://www.indy.gov/activity/report-a-trash-pickup-issue), but not until the **next business day**. Crews have until 6 p.m. to collect. Allow three business days for a missed pickup to be serviced.

**More than two items?** Haul the extra to the **Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station**, 2324 S. Belmont Ave., open **Saturdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.** The fee is **$2 per carload or $5 per SUV or pickup truckload, cash only**, with up to two trips per day. Trailers, moving trucks and loads over 3/4 ton aren't allowed. **South Side Landfill**, 2561 Kentucky Ave., is another option (Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m., Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m.). The City says landfill pricing is based on volume; we couldn't verify the current rates, so check with the landfill before you go. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** dump it. Indiana says it's illegal to open dump or open burn solid waste. ([IDEM: Household Hazardous Wastes](https://www.in.gov/idem/health/household-hazardous-wastes/index))
- **Don't** put out more than two heavy items in a month, and don't stuff bulky items into your cart. Bulky items that don't fit in the cart can't go in it. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

We couldn't find an official City rule for mattresses with bed bugs. Ask the Mayor's Action Center before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** Household furniture (for example a **couch, desk, dresser, table or chairs**) goes out as **heavy trash** on your monthly heavy trash day, next to your trash cart, with the same **two items per month** limit as mattresses. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

Other items the City lists as heavy trash:

- **Appliances without freon**, such as a stove or water heater
- **Lawnmowers**, with the gas and oil drained
- **Swing sets and disassembled bicycles** (assembled bicycles can count as heavy trash, but they must be properly labeled)
- **Yard waste**: bundled limbs 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long count as one heavy trash item

**Construction debris is not accepted** as heavy trash. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

**Drop-off:** Extra household trash and heavy trash can go to the **Citizens' Transfer Station** (2324 S. Belmont Ave., Saturdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., $2 car / $5 SUV or pickup, cash only) or **South Side Landfill** (2561 Kentucky Ave.). See the details in the mattress section above. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Appliances containing freon or refrigerants can't go in your trash cart**, and they aren't part of regular heavy trash. To get rid of one, **contact the Mayor's Action Center to arrange a pickup**. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

- **How to request:** Use the City's [Report a Trash Pickup Issue](https://www.indy.gov/activity/report-a-trash-pickup-issue) service (it covers scheduling a refrigerator, freezer or AC unit pickup), RequestIndy (linked from the [Mayor's Action Center page](https://www.indy.gov/agency/mayors-action-center)) online, or call the Mayor's Action Center at **317-327-4622**. Call center hours: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Thursday, 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. ([Mayor's Action Center](https://www.indy.gov/agency/mayors-action-center))
- **Timing:** **Allow ten business days** for collection of a scheduled freon pickup (refrigerator, freezer, AC unit, etc.). ([Report a Trash Pickup Issue](https://www.indy.gov/activity/report-a-trash-pickup-issue))
- **Cost and prep:** The City pages we checked don't list a fee or prep rules (such as removing doors or food) for freon pickup. Ask the Mayor's Action Center when you schedule.

**Appliances without freon** (for example a stove or water heater) go out as **heavy trash** on your monthly heavy trash day and count toward the two-item limit. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Electronics can't go in the trash in Indianapolis.** Indiana's Electronic Waste law (IC 13-20.5) prohibits households, small businesses and schools from throwing e-waste in the trash, so you **can't** put electronics out with curbside or heavy trash pickup. Haulers **won't collect televisions** with electronic components intact. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

**ToxDrop (Marion County residents only):** The City's Saturday ToxDrop sites take electronics along with household hazardous waste. ([ToxDrop Drop-off Sites](https://www.indy.gov/activity/hazardous-waste-dropoff-sites))

- **Limits:** **Televisions (including flat screens) 27 inches or less**, and **up to five computer systems per visit**.
- **Also accepted:** computers and laptops, monitors, keyboards, mice, printers, scanners, fax machines, hard drives and USB drives, modems, DVD and VHS players, DVR/TiVo and cable or satellite boxes, gaming systems, cameras and camcorders, e-readers, iPods and MP3 players, GPS units, digital photo frames, PC speakers, microphones, circuit boards and UPS batteries.
- **No commercial waste.** See the paint section below for ToxDrop dates and locations.

**RecycleForce:** The City says you can also drop off electronics at [RecycleForce](https://www.recycleforce.org/), a nonprofit. Some items have a requested donation fee. Check RecycleForce's website for drop-off hours and accepted items. Larger TVs (over 27 inches) aren't on the ToxDrop list, so ask RecycleForce whether they take yours. ([Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Household hazardous waste (HHW) **can't go in your trash cart**. Take it to a City **ToxDrop** day. ToxDrop is only for **residents of Marion County** and doesn't accept commercial waste. ([ToxDrop Drop-off Sites](https://www.indy.gov/activity/hazardous-waste-dropoff-sites), [Trash 101](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-101))

- **First Saturday of each month, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.:** Traders Point Collection Facility, 7550 N. Lafayette Rd.
- **Second and fourth Saturday of each month, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.:** Perry Township Government Center, 4925 S. Shelby St.
- **Closed:** third and fifth Saturdays and all major holidays. The City notes the **Perry Township site is closed Saturday, October 24, 2026** for early voting.
- **Never leave waste at a closed site.** The City treats it as illegal dumping, punishable by fines up to $2,500.
- **Limits:** **20 gallons of liquid waste or 75 pounds of solid waste per visit.** Bring it in plastic crates, cardboard boxes, storage bins or other containers that are easy to see into. Waste brought in trash bags may not be accepted.
- **Accepted:** paint, paint stripper and paint thinner, wood stains and varnishes, adhesives and glue, aerosol cans, cleaners (bathroom, oven, carpet), bleach and ammonia, gasoline, lighter fluid, brake fluid, used motor oil, used cooking oil, pesticides, herbicides, insecticides and fertilizer, pool chemicals, propane tanks under 20 pounds, fire extinguishers, AA and AAA batteries, lead acid batteries, fluorescent tubes, CFL and LED bulbs, thermostats, thermometers and elemental mercury, among others.
- **Cost:** The ToxDrop page doesn't list a fee for Marion County residents.

All of the above is from the City's [ToxDrop Drop-off Sites](https://www.indy.gov/activity/hazardous-waste-dropoff-sites) page. The City also occasionally hosts special drive-thru disposal events when resources allow.

**Leftover latex paint:** We didn't find a City of Indianapolis rule for drying out paint for the trash. The state's environmental agency, IDEM, says paint should **never be poured down the drain or put in the trash unless it's completely dry**. Usable paint should be used up, given to theater or community groups, or recycled through a paint exchange. If there's no reuse or disposal program in your area, letting the paint dry out allows it to go out with regular garbage, and adding cheap clay cat litter speeds that up. ([IDEM: Household Hazardous Wastes](https://www.in.gov/idem/health/household-hazardous-wastes/index)) Since ToxDrop takes paint, that's the simplest option for full cans.

For DPW questions, the Department of Public Works lists 317-327-4000 (8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday). ([ToxDrop Drop-off Sites](https://www.indy.gov/activity/hazardous-waste-dropoff-sites))

# Junk Disposal in Albuquerque, NM: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Albuquerque, New Mexico, item by item. The City of Albuquerque Solid Waste Management Department gives its residential customers a **free Large Item Pick-Up** for furniture, mattresses, appliances and TVs. You request it at least 24 hours ahead, and it comes on your next trash day. You can also drive larger loads to one of **three City Convenience Centers** for **$5.00 a load** (plus tax). **Computers and small electronics** go to the Eagle Rock Convenience Center, and **paint and household chemicals** go to the free **Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center** on Girard NE. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call **311 (505-768-2000)** before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Request a free Large Item Pick-Up (at least 24 hours before your trash day), or take up to 3 to a Convenience Center for $5.00 a load |
| Couch / furniture | Free Large Item Pick-Up, a Convenience Center, or donate usable pieces |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Free Large Item Pick-Up (empty it and tape fridge and freezer doors shut), or up to 2 appliances with refrigerant intact at Eagle Rock or Montessa Park |
| TV / computer | TVs go out with a Large Item Pick-Up (never in the blue cart); computers and small electronics go to Eagle Rock for a fee |
| Paint / chemicals | Free at the Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center for City and Bernalillo County residents; dried-out latex paint can go in the trash |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** The City lists mattresses, couches, chairs and bed frames as eligible for its **Large Item Pick-Up**, which is free for residential customers. Request it at least 24 hours before your next trash collection day using the City's online form or by calling 311. Requests made within 24 hours may be handled case by case. ([City of Albuquerque: Large Item Pick-Up](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/large-item-pick-up))

### Step by Step: Large Item Pick-Up

1. **Check that you're a City Solid Waste residential customer.** The City's FAQ says large items are picked up at no charge for Solid Waste Management Department residential customers only. Commercial customers pay **$59.31** for a large item or excess trash pickup. ([City of Albuquerque: Solid Waste FAQ](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/frequently-asked-questions))
2. **Request it at least 24 hours ahead.** Use the online form, call 311 (505-768-2000) or use the ABQ311 app. Pickup happens on your next normal trash collection day.
3. **Set it out the right way.** Place items on the curb five feet away from carts, mailboxes, fire hydrants, parked cars, low-hanging trees and utility poles, so the truck can reach them.
4. **Keep it to one load.** The City collects no more than "a standard pick-up load," and contaminated items won't be collected. ([City of Albuquerque: Large Item Pick-Up](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/large-item-pick-up))

Look up your trash day on the City's [Collection Day Overview](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection) page.

**Bed bugs or soiled mattresses:** We couldn't find an official City rule about wrapping or bagging a mattress. The City only says contaminated items won't be collected, so call 311 before you set one out.

**Outside city limits:** Unincorporated Bernalillo County homes get trash service through Waste Management, which includes bulky item removal once a month. For help with County trash service, call the County's Solid Waste Program at **505-224-1639**. ([Bernalillo County: Residential Trash Service](https://www.bernco.gov/public-works/public-works-services/trash-recycling/residential-trash-service/))

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** Furniture follows the same Large Item Pick-Up rules as mattresses: free for residential customers, requested at least 24 hours before your trash day, one standard pick-up load at a time.

**Convenience Centers:** The City runs three drop-off sites for residents and small commercial haulers. All three are closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day, and no vehicles are admitted after 4:30 p.m.

| Convenience Center | Address | Phone | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eagle Rock | 6301 Eagle Rock NE (north off I-25 and Alameda) | 505-768-3925 | Daily, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Don Reservoir | 117 114th SW (west of 98th off West Central Ave.) | 505-768-3920 | Daily, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Montessa Park | 3512 Los Picaros SE | 505-768-3930 | Fri, Sat and Sun, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |

Only Eagle Rock takes recyclables. There's no trash drop-off at the 4600 Edith NE headquarters.

**Fees:** City residents pay **$5.00 per load** (plus tax) and non-residents pay **$12.00 per load** (plus tax). An unsecured or untarped load costs more: **$10.54** per load for City residents and **$24.00** for non-residents. ([City of Albuquerque: Convenience Center Rule Changes 2026](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/convenience-center-rule-changes-2026)) One load is a 4 x 8 x 2 foot truck bed or trailer. Bring a utility bill, driver's license or valid ID as proof of residency. The centers take cash, check, Mastercard, Visa and American Express.

**Limits:** Up to 3 mattresses per customer, vehicles rated under two tons, and trailer limits of 35 feet (Eagle Rock), 30 feet (Don Reservoir) and 40 feet (Montessa Park). More mattresses than the limit must go to the Cerro Colorado Landfill. Construction and demolition debris (including drywall and carpet) and roofing materials aren't accepted. ([City of Albuquerque: Trash Drop Off](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/trash-drop-off))

**Renovation debris:** The City's FAQ points to other sites for construction debris, including the Southwest Landfill (505-242-2020), and lists Waste Management (505-892-1200) for County customers. Call ahead for rules and fees. ([City of Albuquerque: Solid Waste FAQ](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/frequently-asked-questions))

**Extra bags of trash:** Excess garbage or yard waste that doesn't fit in your cart can be picked up for a **$59.31** fee. Call 311 at least 24 hours ahead (Monday to Saturday). ([City of Albuquerque: Residential Collection and Disposal Rates](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/residential-collection-disposal-rates))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Curbside:** Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, stoves, dishwashers, washers and dryers, and water heaters are all eligible for the free Large Item Pick-Up. Appliances must be empty, and fridge and freezer doors must be taped shut. ([City of Albuquerque: Large Item Pick-Up](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/large-item-pick-up))

**Drop-off:** Eagle Rock and Montessa Park take up to **2 appliances with refrigerant intact** per visit. Don Reservoir takes only one appliance and **can't receive refrigerators**. Remove all food from fridges and freezers first. ([City of Albuquerque: Trash Drop Off](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection/trash-drop-off)) The Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center does not take appliances.

**Freon:** The City's drop-off rules accept appliances with refrigerant intact, and its pickup page doesn't ask you to remove refrigerant first. If you're unsure, call 311 before you set one out. Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**TVs:** Neither flat-screen nor older CRT (boxy) TVs can go in your blue recycling cart. The City says to call 311 for pickup. ([City of Albuquerque: Electronic Waste](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/recycling/copy_of_electronic-waste-e-waste)) Televisions are on the Large Item Pick-Up list.

**Computers and small electronics:** Take them to the **Eagle Rock Convenience Center**, where disposal fees apply. The City doesn't list the fee amount, so ask when you arrive or call 505-768-3925. Accepted items include computers and peripherals, laptops, LCD monitors, printers, scanners, copiers, fax machines, keyboards and mice, DVD and VHS players, stereo equipment, cameras, phones and cellphones (batteries removed when possible), hard drives and thumb drives, cables and cords, and drills and small tools. Small appliances like toasters, microwaves and blenders aren't e-waste and can go in your trash cart if they fit. Delete your personal data first.

**CRT computer monitors and batteries:** These aren't part of the Eagle Rock e-waste program. CRT monitors and batteries of any kind are accepted at the Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center (see below). ([City of Albuquerque: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/hazardous-waste))

**Other options:** The US EPA keeps a list of [manufacturer and retailer electronics donation and recycling programs](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling).

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center (HHWCC):** Residents of the City of Albuquerque and Bernalillo County can drop off household hazardous waste **at no charge**. Since December 1, 2023, the center has been run under contract by Safety-Kleen.

| Site | Address | Hours | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center (Safety-Kleen) | 2720 Girard NE, Albuquerque, NM 87107 | Mon, Wed and Fri, 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.; Sat, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. | 505-884-2277 |

The center's holiday list includes closures on Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Christmas Eve, Christmas and New Year's Day.

- **Accepted:** paints, batteries of any kind (including car batteries), cleaners, fertilizers, pesticides, pool chemicals, used motor oil, propane bottles (up to 10 gallons or 40 pounds), fire extinguishers, medical-grade oxygen cylinders, over-the-counter medications and CRT monitors.
- **Not accepted:** electronics and e-waste, appliances, tires, explosives or ammunition, other compressed gases, radioactive and biomedical waste, smoke detectors, and anything from a business. Businesses, public agencies and nonprofits must use a state-permitted vendor; see the [New Mexico Environment Department vendor list](https://www.env.nm.gov/hazardous-waste/recycling-and-hazardous-waste-management-services/).
- **Limits:** no more than 125 pounds of material per load, and no container bigger than 55 gallons.
- **Free reuse area:** usable materials brought to the center are offered to the public free of charge.

**Latex paint at home:** The City says latex and acrylic paint can go in the trash once you let it evaporate or solidify it with an absorbent like kitty litter. You can also take it to the HHWCC. Oil-based paint, stains, varnishes, paint thinner and turpentine go to the HHWCC. ([City of Albuquerque: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/hazardous-waste))

**Never in your carts:** The City says not to put hazardous waste in your trash or recycling cart, or medications in the trash. ([City of Albuquerque: Collection Day Overview](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/trash-collection))

Questions? Call **311 (505-768-2000)** or the Solid Waste Management Department at **505-761-8100**. ([City of Albuquerque: Solid Waste Contact](https://www.cabq.gov/solidwaste/contact))

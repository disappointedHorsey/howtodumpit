## How every guide works

Putting a mattress or couch on the curb without following your city's rules can get you fined, and the rules, fees, and booking steps differ everywhere. Each city guide lists the options we could verify, with links to the official source:

1. **Free or low-cost city bulk pickup.** Often free, but you usually have to book it first.
2. **Drop-off sites** (convenience centers, transfer stations, recyclers).
3. **Donation**, if the item is clean and usable.
4. **Paid haul-away** when you need it gone today.

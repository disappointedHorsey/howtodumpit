## What Each City Guide Covers

Putting junk on the curb without following your city's rules can get you fined, and the rules, fees and booking steps differ everywhere. For each item, a city guide lists your options in this order, with a link to the official source for every one:

1. **Free or low-cost city bulk pickup.** Often free, but you usually have to book it first.
2. **Drop-off sites** (convenience centers, transfer stations, hazardous-waste facilities).
3. **Donation**, if the item is clean and usable.
4. **Paid junk removal** when you can't wait for the city or can't move the item yourself.

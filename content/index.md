# Get rid of bulky stuff the legal, cheap way

Putting a mattress or couch on the curb without scheduling a pickup can get you fined in many cities, and the rules, fees, and booking steps are different everywhere. Each city guide here lists every option we could verify, with links to the official source:

1. **Free or low-cost city bulk pickup.** Often free, but you usually have to book it first.
2. **Drop-off sites** (convenience centers, transfer stations, recyclers).
3. **Donation pickup**, if the item is clean and usable.
4. **Paid haul-away** when you need it gone today.

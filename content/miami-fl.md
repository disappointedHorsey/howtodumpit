# Junk Disposal in Miami, FL: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in the **City of Miami**, Florida, item by item. The City's **Department of Solid Waste** collects bulky trash **once a week** from homes with City green and blue carts and runs a **free Mini Dump** for City residents. **Miami-Dade County** runs the **Home Chemical Collection Centers** for paint and chemicals. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Call **311** or Solid Waste customer service at **305-960-2801** before you set anything out or drive.

**City of Miami only.** Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Hialeah, Doral and other Miami-Dade cities run their own programs or use the County's service, so their rules differ (see Outside City Limits below).

## Quick Answer by Item

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** City of Miami homes with City carts can put a mattress out with **weekly bulky trash** (the City lists **household furniture**), or drop it at the **Mini Dump**, which names **mattresses** among accepted household furniture. ([City of Miami: Bulky Trash Schedules](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/View-Garbage-Pickup-Recycling-and-Bulky-Trash-Schedules); [About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash))

### Step by Step: Weekly Curbside Bulky Trash

1. **Find your bulky day.** Enter your address in the City's schedule lookup.
2. **Set out on time.** Bulky trash (other than clean yard waste) can go out **the evening before** but **no later than 6 a.m.** on collection day. ([City of Miami: Solid Waste](https://www.miami.gov/My-Government/Departments/Solid-Waste))
3. **Place it right.** Put items in front of your home, **5 feet away from all objects** such as poles, cars and mailboxes, and keep bulky items separate from garbage and recycling carts. ([Let's Get Rid of Illegal Dumping](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Illegal-Dumping/Lets-Get-Rid-of-Illegal-Dumping-Landing-Page))
4. **Bundling note:** The schedule page says items must be tied in bundles no longer than **3 feet** and no heavier than **50 lbs**. It doesn't explain how that applies to a whole mattress or sofa, so call **311** if you're unsure.

### Step by Step: Mini Dump (Self-Haul)

Bring **one proof of City of Miami residency** (Florida driver's license or ID, a recent utility bill, rental agreement or tax bill). One trip per day, no more than a pickup truck load. Commercial-marked vehicles, dump or panel trucks and contractor materials are refused. Mini Dump phone: **305-468-5900** or **311**.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask 311 before you set one out.

**Illegal dumping** (wrong time, wrong place or prohibited items) can mean a **$500 fine or arrest**. Report dumping to **311**.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Homes with City carts:** couches, chairs, tables, dressers and bed frames go out with **weekly bulky trash** or to the **Mini Dump** (same rules as mattresses). Construction and remodeling debris (cabinets, doors, tile, carpet, toilets, tubs) is **not** bulky trash and is refused at the Mini Dump. ([About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash))

**Apartments:** If your building uses a **dumpster**, you **cannot** put bulky trash at the curb. Ask your property manager or landlord to schedule bulky pickup through an **authorized private hauler**, or take items to the free Mini Dump yourself. Properties with **four or more units** use City-approved franchise commercial haulers. ([Let's Get Rid of Illegal Dumping](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Illegal-Dumping/Lets-Get-Rid-of-Illegal-Dumping-Landing-Page); [City of Miami: Solid Waste](https://www.miami.gov/My-Government/Departments/Solid-Waste))

**City districts and neighborhoods:** We found no difference in bulky rules between City of Miami neighborhoods or commission districts. What changes is your **property type** (home with City carts vs. apartment with a dumpster) and your **collection day**.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Miami** only. **Unincorporated Miami-Dade County** and the cities of **Aventura, Cutler Bay, Doral, Miami Gardens, Miami Lakes, Opa-locka, Palmetto Bay, Pinecrest, Sunny Isles Beach and Sweetwater** get County service: **two scheduled bulky pickups a year** of up to **25 cubic yards** each, booked online or through 311 **before** you put anything at the curb, with mattresses **enclosed in a plastic bag**. ([Miami-Dade County: Bulky Waste Pickup](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser148069212998189); [2025 Waste Service Guide (PDF)](https://www.miamidade.gov/resources/solid-waste/documents/brochures/2025-waste-service-guide.pdf)) Other cities, including **Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Hialeah, North Miami and Homestead**, run their own trash service; the County lists each city's phone number on its [Waste Collection page](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1464792744722367). The City Mini Dump is for City of Miami residents only. If you live in a condo, apartment or HOA with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**White goods** such as refrigerators, stoves, microwaves and dishwashers are on the City's accepted list for **weekly bulky trash** and the **Mini Dump**. ([About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash))

**Freon / refrigerants:** The City pages we checked don't say whether refrigerant must be removed or doors taken off before pickup. Call **311** before you set out a fridge, freezer or AC unit. Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

**Not at the County chemical centers:** Miami-Dade's Home Chemical mobile events list **appliances** and **microwave ovens** as not accepted. ([Miami-Dade: Home Chemical Collection Centers](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1464798615648535))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**City options:** **Household electronics** (TVs, computer monitors, printers, laptops, DVD players, cell phones, small kitchen appliances) are accepted at the **Mini Dump** and listed for **weekly bulky trash**. The City also holds electronics recycling opportunities during the year; ask **solidwastecustomerservice@miamigov.com** for dates. ([About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash); [Bulky Trash Schedules](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/View-Garbage-Pickup-Recycling-and-Bulky-Trash-Schedules))

**County option:** Both Miami-Dade **Home Chemical Collection Centers** accept computer monitors, TVs, computers, keyboards, printers, audio and video equipment and cell phones from residents (not businesses). ([Miami-Dade: Home Chemical Collection Centers](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1464798615648535))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you recycle them.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never** put paint or chemicals out with bulky trash. The Mini Dump refuses **paint, paint containers, chemicals, pesticides, propane and oxygen tanks, light bulbs, batteries, tires and auto fluids**. ([About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash))

**Miami-Dade County Home Chemical Collection Centers** (residents only; Wednesday to Sunday, **9 a.m. to 5 p.m.**):

- **West Dade:** 8801 NW 58th Street, Doral, FL 33178
- **South Dade:** 23707 SW 97th Avenue, Gate B, Homestead, FL 33032

They accept **oil-based paint**, **latex paint that is still liquid**, pesticides, solvents, pool chemicals, unbroken fluorescent bulbs, small propane tanks, empty oxygen tanks and small lithium (button) batteries. **Dried-out latex paint** can go in your garbage cart once it is solid (use kitty litter or newspaper). Good paint is better donated. Pack containers upright in sturdy boxes with tight lids and labels. Free leftover products are available through the **Chem Again** program. ([Miami-Dade: Home Chemical Collection Centers](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1464798615648535))

The City of Miami points residents to these County centers for household chemicals. ([City of Miami: Solid Waste](https://www.miami.gov/My-Government/Departments/Solid-Waste)) The County also runs free **Home Chemical Drop-Off Mobile Events** (8 a.m. to 2 p.m.) for all Miami-Dade residents; check the County page for upcoming dates.

Questions? City of Miami Department of Solid Waste, 1290 NW 20th Street: **305-960-2801**, **311**, or **solidwastecustomerservice@miamigov.com**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Miami Department of Solid Waste has not published a dedicated 2026-27 Christmas tree collection page that we could find as of November 10, 2026. Do not assume Miami-Dade County's tree recycling sweep covers City of Miami homes.

City of Miami homes with City carts get weekly bulky trash pickup, which accepts clean yard trash such as tree cuttings, yard clippings, shrubbery, hedge trimmings, and bagged leaves and grass. A whole Christmas tree is not named on the bulky or Mini Dump pages we read. Ask 311 or Solid Waste at 305-960-2801 whether a bare tree is accepted at the curb or at the Mini Dump, 1290 NW 20th Street, before you set one out. ([City of Miami: About Mini Dump and Bulky Trash](https://www.miami.gov/My-Home-Neighborhood/Garbage-Recycling/About-Mini-Dump-and-Bulky-Trash))

**Miami-Dade County (not City service):** The County's Christmas tree recycling page is only for residents who receive waste collection from Miami-Dade County. If you do not, the County says to contact your local service provider. Last season the County listed a final Christmas tree collection sweep from January 21 through February 1, with collection on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays, and trees to be at the curb by Wednesday, January 21. Trees placed as part of a scheduled bulky waste pickup are not recycled into mulch. ([Miami-Dade County: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/solidwaste/christmas-tree-recycling.page))

If you need a tree taken from the house, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

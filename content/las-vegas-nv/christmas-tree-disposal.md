# Christmas Tree Recycling in Las Vegas, NV (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Each year from December 26 to January 15, you can take a bare cut Christmas tree to one of more than 30 drop sites across the Las Vegas Valley to be recycled free of charge. The program is run by the Springs Preserve and the Christmas Tree Recycling Committee, with the City of Las Vegas as a partner. If you can't get the tree to a site, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against the official Springs Preserve Christmas tree recycling pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Valley Christmas Tree Recycling Program {#tree-recycling}

- **2026-27 dates:** the official page gives a standing window of **December 26 to January 15 each year** and does not post separate 2026-27 dates (checked November 25, 2026). ([Springs Preserve: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.springspreserve.org/education-conservation/christmas-tree-recycling.html))
- **Prep:** remove all non-organic objects, such as lights, wire, tinsel, ornaments and nails; they contaminate the mulch and can ruin the chipper. Trees are chipped into mulch for landscaping.
- **Flocked trees can't be recycled.**
- **Questions:** the Christmas Tree Recycling Committee contact is UNLV Rebel Recycling, 702-895-3760.
- **The catch:** there's no City curbside tree pickup listed. You drive the tree to a site, and the Springs Preserve itself won't take trees on Tuesdays or Wednesdays, when it's closed.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where can I recycle my Christmas tree in Las Vegas?

At any of more than 30 valley drop sites, including Sunset Park, Desert Breeze Park, Bruce Trent Park, Sunny Springs Park and the Springs Preserve (not on Tuesdays or Wednesdays).

### When is Christmas tree recycling in Las Vegas?

From December 26 to January 15 each year, according to the Springs Preserve program page.

### Is Christmas tree recycling free in Las Vegas?

Yes. The official program says trees dropped at the valley sites are recycled free of charge.

### Can I recycle a flocked tree in Las Vegas?

No. The program says flocked trees can't be recycled.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Las Vegas?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

# Junk Disposal in Boise, ID: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in **Boise**, item by item. The City of Boise's **Curb It** program includes **up to six free bulky item pickups per customer each year**: you call the City's service provider, Republic Services, to request one. For self-haul, the **Ada County Landfill** takes trash by weight and recycles e-waste and household hazardous waste for free. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call Republic Services at **(208) 345-1266** or the landfill at **208-577-4725**.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** A mattress or box spring counts as one of your **six free bulky items** a year. Call **Republic Services at (208) 345-1266** to request the pickup, then place it at the curb or in the alley. ([City of Boise: Trash and Bulky Items](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/trash/))

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Free Bulky Item Pickup

1. **Count your items.** Each customer gets up to **six bulky items a year**, free of charge.
2. **Call to request.** Phone Republic Services at (208) 345-1266 and ask for a bulky item pickup.
3. **Set it out where trucks can reach it:** at the curb or in the alley.
4. **Empty appliances first.** Appliances must be empty.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask Republic Services when you call.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, chairs, dressers, bed frames and other furniture** are on the City's bulky item list, along with carpet and pad, grills, bicycles, swing sets, lawnmowers, water softeners and swamp coolers. Call Republic Services to book, and set items at the curb or in the alley. ([City of Boise: Trash and Bulky Items](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/trash/))

**Extra bags of trash:** each household gets **five free Overflow Stickers** a year for an extra 20-32-gallon can of trash on your regular day; a sheet of five more costs **$11.69**.

**Self-haul:** the **Ada County Landfill**, 10300 N. Seaman's Gulch Rd., is open **Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.**, and **Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.** From October 1, 2026, the tonnage fee is **$34 per ton** with a **$15 minimum**; hard-to-handle items are $50 per ton. During wet weather the landfill deck can be slippery and may need 4-wheel drive. ([Ada County Landfill](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/))

**Apartments and condos:** if your building has a shared dumpster or a private hauler, ask your property manager or HOA before you leave anything out.

### Outside City Limits?

Boise's Curb It program covers City of Boise customers. **Unincorporated Ada County**, Garden City, Meridian and Eagle each have their own collection, so check that city's or the county's hauler page; the Ada County Landfill lists the links for each. ([Ada County Landfill: Waste Diversion & Recycling](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/waste-types-solutions/waste-diversion-recycling/)) The landfill's household hazardous waste program is open to all Ada County residents. Apartment, condo and HOA communities with a **private hauler** should ask their property manager or HOA.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, dishwashers and water heaters** can go out as free **bulky items**. Empty them first; after pickup, the City says Freon is safely removed and the appliances are recycled. ([City of Boise: Trash and Bulky Items](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/trash/))

**Self-haul:** bring an **empty** fridge, freezer or AC unit to the landfill's **Recovery & Diversion Area**, where technicians remove the refrigerant. Refrigerated appliances cost **$25.75** each; other mostly-metal appliances go in the scrap metal area. ([Ada County Landfill: Waste Diversion & Recycling](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/waste-types-solutions/waste-diversion-recycling/))

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**TVs, computers, printers, microwaves and other electronics** can be dropped off **free** at the landfill's **Recovery and Diversion Area** (past the scales) during regular hours, Monday to Saturday; make an appointment if you have more than ten items. Ada County residents can also bring e-waste to the City's free **HHW mobile collection sites**, which take LED flat-screen TVs of any size, but other TVs and monitors only if smaller than 27 inches. The landfill doesn't wipe data, so clear your devices first. ([Ada County Landfill: Hazardous Waste and E-Waste](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/waste-types-solutions/hazardous-waste/))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Latex and oil-based paint, stains, solvents, pesticides, automotive fluids and rechargeable batteries** go to a City of Boise **HHW mobile collection site** (residents only; up to 25 items or 25 gallons, staff unload your car) or to the **Household Hazardous Waste Facility at the Ada County Landfill**, open **Fridays and Saturdays, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.**, which takes larger loads. Both are free for residents. Don't put hazardous waste in your trash, recycling or compost cart. ([City of Boise: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/hhw/)) Questions: the HHW office at (208) 577-4737, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ([Ada County Landfill: Hazardous Waste](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/waste-types-solutions/hazardous-waste/))

**Tires:** the landfill takes up to 12 tires at **$5.25** each; call ahead for more.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** If you have a City compost cart, remove all decorations and lights and **cut the live tree into pieces that fit inside the cart** with the lid closed; whole trees left beside the cart may not be collected. Flocked or artificial-snow trees and wreaths go in the trash. ([City of Boise: Christmas Trees](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/curb-it-sorting-guide/compost/christmas-trees/))

**Landfill drop-off:** last season, Boise residents could take a whole tree to the **Ada County Landfill** for free from **December 26, 2025, to January 10, 2026**. Ada County says residential holiday trees are taken free each season in the wood waste area and that dates will be posted in December; the 2026-27 dates were **not posted** as of November 25, 2026. Remove lights, ornaments, tinsel and the stand. ([Ada County Landfill: Waste Diversion & Recycling](https://adacounty.id.gov/landfill/waste-types-solutions/waste-diversion-recycling/))

**Holiday collection:** the City says trash collection is delayed by one day when Christmas or New Year's Day falls on a weekday. Both fall on a Friday this season (December 25, 2026, and January 1, 2027). ([City of Boise: Trash](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/public-works/curb-it/trash/))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Does Boise have bulk trash pickup?

Yes. City of Boise customers get up to six free bulky item pickups a year. Call Republic Services at (208) 345-1266 to request one.

### How much does the Ada County Landfill cost?

From October 1, 2026, $34 per ton with a $15 minimum. Refrigerated appliances are $25.75 each, and e-waste and household hazardous waste are free.

### Where do I take an old TV in Boise?

To the Ada County Landfill's Recovery and Diversion Area for free, or to a City HHW mobile site if it's an LED flat screen or under 27 inches.

### Where can I get rid of paint in Boise?

At a City HHW mobile collection site or the landfill's HHW facility on Fridays and Saturdays. Both take latex and oil-based paint free for residents.

### Who can haul away junk in Boise today?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page for a quote. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

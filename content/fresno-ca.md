# Junk Disposal in Fresno, CA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Fresno, California, item by item. If you pay for City of Fresno residential trash service, you get a **free One-Time Bulky Item Pickup** once a year, a yearly **Operation Clean Up** curbside collection for your neighborhood, and **Free Dump Events** at the City's transfer station. **TVs, computers, paint and household chemicals** go to the **Fresno County Environmental Compliance Center**, which is free for residents. **Refrigerators and freezers** aren't accepted at the City's curbside bulky collections. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call the City's 311 Center (dial 3-1-1 or **559-621-2489**) before you set anything out.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Mattresses and box springs are on the accepted list for the City's **One-Time Bulky Item Pickup**, which is free once a year for City residential solid waste customers whose account is in good standing. Call 311, call 559-621-2489, or use the FresGO app to ask for a pickup. ([City of Fresno: One-Time Bulky Item Pickup](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/residential-services/one-time-bulky-item-pickup/))

<!--LISTINGS-->

**Operation Clean Up and Free Dump Events:** Mattresses and box springs are also accepted at your neighborhood's yearly Operation Clean Up and at the City's Free Dump Events. Both are explained in the furniture section below.

**Buying a new mattress?** Fresno County says California retailers have up to 30 days to pick up your old mattress, but a retailer may refuse one that poses a health or safety risk. The County also points to the state's Bye Bye Mattress program for drop-off sites. ([Fresno County: Special Handling](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/special-handling/))

**Bed bugs:** We couldn't find an official City of Fresno rule about wrapping or bagging a mattress with bed bugs. Ask Solid Waste when you book your pickup.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**One-Time Bulky Item Pickup:** Once a year, at no extra cost, the City will pick up a single bulky item that's too large for your carts. Accepted items include couches and sofas, recliners, chairs, tables, desks, dressers, nightstands, bookcases, shelving, entertainment centers, filing cabinets, headboards, carpet, mattresses and box springs, stoves and ovens, and drained water heaters. Solid Waste will call you within 7 business days to confirm a date. ([City of Fresno: One-Time Bulky Item Pickup](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/residential-services/one-time-bulky-item-pickup/))

1. **Ask for your pickup.** Call 311 or 559-621-2489, or use the FresGO app. Your neighbor's request doesn't cover you: piles from addresses that didn't ask are treated as illegal dumping.
2. **Set it out on time.** Put the item out no more than 36 hours before your date, and by 6 a.m. on the day.
3. **Place it right.** Set it 1 to 1.5 feet from the curb in front of your home, where your carts are serviced (alley homes use the front curb), clear of vehicles, sewer vents, water meters, mailboxes and fire hydrants, and 4 to 5 feet from speed bumps.

A second pickup within a year counts as a **Special Haul**, which has a fee set in the City's Master Fee Schedule. To ask about a Special Haul, call Solid Waste at 559-621-6888.

**Operation Clean Up:** Once a year, City crews collect bulky items at the curb in each neighborhood. It's only for residences that pay for City trash service on their utility bill. Find your date with the lookup tool on the City's page or by calling 311, and check back as the date nears because dates can change. ([City of Fresno: Operation Clean Up](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/operation-clean-up/))

- **Timing:** Put items out no earlier than 7 days before your pickup day and no later than 6 a.m. that day.
- **Two piles:** One pile for yard clippings (tree limbs, lumber, grass, leaves and brush) and one pile for everything else.
- **Limit:** 10 cubic yards, about 2 piles of 8 by 4 by 4 feet (2 pickup truck beds loaded to the top of the cab).
- **Accepted:** furniture and appliances (but no refrigerators or freezers), mattresses and box springs, drained water heaters, sinks and toilets, disassembled cabinets, and taped glass doors.
- **Not accepted:** tires, paint, batteries, chemicals, propane tanks, motor oil, broken TV screens or computer monitors, and contractor or major remodeling debris.
- **No alleys:** Debris in alleys won't be picked up.

**Free Dump Events:** City residential solid waste customers can drop off bulky items at the Cedar Avenue Recycling and Transfer Station (CARTS), 3457 S. Cedar Ave., at no additional cost. The remaining fall 2026 dates are **November 14 and 15, 2026**, from 7 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. The limit is one full-size pickup truck load per household (about 8 by 4 by 4 feet). Bring a City of Fresno utility bill or a Proof of Eligibility Form, plus a state-issued ID. Furniture, mattresses, appliances, carpet and scrap metal are accepted; electronic waste, household hazardous waste, tires and household garbage aren't. ([City of Fresno: Residential Services](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/residential-services/))

### Outside City Limits?

These City programs are only for homes that pay for City of Fresno residential trash service. Inside the city, **apartment buildings with 4 or more units** aren't eligible for Operation Clean Up, and multi-family properties are served by a private franchise hauler that depends on which side of **Ashlan Avenue** you're on: Republic Services (Allied Waste) north of Ashlan, 559-275-1551, and Mid Valley Disposal south of Ashlan, 559-237-9425. ([City of Fresno: Multi-Family and Commercial Services](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/multi-family-commercial-services/)) If you live in **unincorporated Fresno County**, your trash hauler depends on your zone; find it on the County's hauler and zone map. ([Fresno County: Cart Rates and Hauler Map](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/cart-rates/)) The County also runs a free drop-off coupon program for unincorporated residents through their local hauler. ([Fresno County: Community Cleanup Coupon](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/community-cleanup-coupon/)) Clovis is a separate city with its own services, so check the City of Clovis. If you live in an HOA or gated community, check with your HOA or property manager first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Not curbside:** Refrigerators and freezers aren't accepted at the One-Time Bulky Item Pickup or at Operation Clean Up. Stoves and ovens and drained water heaters are accepted at the One-Time Bulky Item Pickup. ([City of Fresno: Bulky Item Accepted List](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/residential-services/one-time-bulky-item-pickup/))

**Free Dump Events:** "Appliances" are on the accepted list. The City page doesn't say whether refrigerators, freezers or AC units are included, so call 311 before you bring one.

**Certified Appliance Recyclers:** Fresno County says state law bars landfills and scrap dealers from accepting discarded appliances without a "Certificate of Recycling" showing that hazardous materials were removed by a Certified Appliance Recycler. Find one on the state's DTSC map, or ask your retailer about removal when you buy a new appliance. The County's drop-off list shows SA Recycling, 3489 S. Chestnut Ave., taking large appliances **with or without freon** (household quantities, 559-237-6677). Several other listed sites take appliances **without freon** only. ([Fresno County: Special Handling Programs](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/special-handling/)) ([California DTSC: Certified Appliance Recycler Program](https://dtsc.ca.gov/certified-appliance-recycler-car-program/))

**Freon:** Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Never in a cart:** Electronics and e-waste aren't allowed in any City cart. They also aren't accepted at the One-Time Bulky Item Pickup or at Free Dump Events. ([City of Fresno: What Goes Where?](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/what-goes-where-green-gray-blue/))

**Environmental Compliance Center (ECC):** Fresno County's ECC takes cell phones, laptops, tablets, printers, TVs and computer monitors for free from residents. Small appliances are accepted if they weigh under 125 pounds and don't contain freon. ([Fresno County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/hhw/)) The County's drop-off list also shows other sites for e-waste and computer equipment, including CARTS, Mid Valley Disposal and Best Buy stores. Call any site before you go. ([Fresno County: Drop-Off Locations](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/drop-off-locations/))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Environmental Compliance Center (ECC):** Free for household waste from residents, no appointment needed.

| Site | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Fresno County Environmental Compliance Center | 1327 W. Dan Ronquillo Dr., Fresno | Thu, Fri and Sat, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. (except holidays) |

- **Limit:** household items and small quantities only, up to **125 pounds or 15 gallons per visit** or vehicle. You can make more than one trip while it's open. Call 559-600-4259 about larger loads or unusual items.
- **Accepted:** paint, stain and varnish, paint stripper, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, cleaners, pool chemicals, pesticides, fertilizer, propane tanks, motor oil, antifreeze, gasoline, treated wood, roofing tar, solar panels and cooking oil.
- **Reuse Center:** The ECC gives away usable paint and other products collected there to County residents at no cost.

([Fresno County: Drop-Off Locations](https://cleanupfresnocounty.com/drop-off-locations/))

**Paint at home:** The City says leftover latex or water-based paint should be soaked up with kitty litter and put in your **gray cart**, and a completely empty, dried-out can goes in your **blue cart**. Oil-based paint must go to a household hazardous waste facility, event or approved drop-off site. You can also take leftover paint to a participating PaintCare retailer. ([City of Fresno: Hazardous Waste and Sharps Disposal](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/hazardous-waste-and-sharps-disposal/))

**Never in your carts:** Don't put household hazardous waste in the trash, on the ground, down the sewer or into the gutter. Batteries, chemicals, aerosol cans, light bulbs, motor oil and pesticides aren't allowed in any cart.

Questions? Call the City of Fresno Recycling Hotline at **559-621-1111**, Solid Waste at **559-621-6888**, or **311**. For hazardous waste, call Fresno County at **559-600-4259**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Fresno tells single-family customers to recycle a live Christmas tree or wreath in the green organics cart, not in the landfill.

Prepare the tree by removing all ornaments, lights, tinsel and the stand. Wood and prunings must be less than 4 inches in diameter and cut to 3-foot lengths or less. Place the pieces in the green waste cart and make sure the lid closes completely. Artificial Christmas trees can be donated or placed in the gray cart for disposal. Do not burn a tree in the fireplace, and do not dump trees in vacant lots (the City says there are heavy fines for littering). ([City of Fresno: What Goes Where](https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/trash-disposal-recycling/what-goes-where-green-gray-blue/); [Holiday Trees Go in the Green Cart (PDF)](https://www.fresno.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2512December-Insert_web-10w1151.pdf))

Apartment and commercial customers north of Ashlan Avenue should contact Allied Waste Services of Fresno, 559-275-1551. Customers south of Ashlan Avenue should contact Mid Valley Disposal, 559-237-9425. For more information, call the Recycling Hotline at 559-621-1111 or 311.

If the tree will not fit in the cart, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

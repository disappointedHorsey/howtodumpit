# Junk Disposal in Washington, DC: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Washington, DC, item by item. Every option links to the official District page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call 311.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | DPW bulk appointment (wrap in plastic) or Fort Totten drop-off |
| Couch / furniture | DPW bulk appointment (max 7 items) or Fort Totten |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | DPW bulk (drain AC fluids; fridge doors removed) or Fort Totten |
| TV / computer | Banned from trash; DPW Special Waste events or eCYCLE DC options |
| Paint / chemicals | PaintCare drop-off sites; other HHW at DPW Special Waste events |
| Christmas tree | Last season: DPW curbside January 2-February 28, 2026, for DPW-serviced homes; 2026-27 dates not yet published |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** If you get **DPW trash collection** (single-family or buildings with **three or fewer** units), book a **bulk trash appointment** through **311** or [311.dc.gov](https://311.dc.gov), **wrap the mattress or box spring in plastic**, and set it out only in the appointment window. Or self-haul to **Fort Totten Transfer Station**, where mattresses do **not** need plastic wrap for drop-off.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: DPW Bulk Pickup

1. Confirm you get **DPW trash collection**. DPW serves single-family homes and residential buildings with **three or fewer** living units. Buildings with **four or more** units, condominiums, co-ops and commercial properties must use a **private licensed hauler**.
2. Call **311** or use **311.dc.gov** and request **residential bulk trash collection**. Book **before** putting anything in the public space.
3. **Wrap** mattresses and box springs in plastic. Max **seven items** per appointment.
4. Place items where your trash and recycling are collected **between 6:30 p.m. the night before and 6:00 a.m. on appointment day**. Items left out at other times can draw a sanitation ticket.

### Self-Haul: Fort Totten Transfer Station

District residents can take acceptable bulk, trash and recycling to **Fort Totten**, 4900 John McCormack Rd NE, without an appointment. Resident drop-off hours listed on the DPW bulk page are **Tue–Fri 10 a.m.–2 p.m.** and **Sat 7 a.m.–2 p.m.** Bring proof you live in DC. Vehicle limits apply (passenger cars, SUVs, pickups and vans under 15 ft; commercial tags and many larger vehicles are denied). Mattresses at the transfer station do **not** have to be wrapped in plastic.

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** put a mattress out without an appointment (ticket risk).
- **Don't** set bulk out outside the 6:30 p.m.–6:00 a.m. window.
- **Don't** put electronics or hazardous liquids out with bulk. Electronics and HHW are **not** acceptable bulk items.
- Renovation debris (bricks, concrete, drywall, dirt, etc.) is **not** accepted on residential bulk routes.

### Live in a 4+ Unit Building, Condo, or Co-op?

DPW bulk does not apply. Use your building's private hauler, or ask the property manager. Self-haul to Fort Totten may still be an option if you meet resident ID and vehicle rules. Confirm on the DPW page or with 311.

### Need It Gone Today?

Self-haul to Fort Totten during resident hours, or use a paid junk removal service.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Household furniture** is on DPW's acceptable bulk list. Same rules as mattresses: DPW-served homes book through **311** (max **seven items** per appointment), set out only in the appointment window, or self-haul to **Fort Totten**. Buildings with four or more units use a private hauler. ([DPW Bulk Trash Collection](https://dpw.dc.gov/node/1652581))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Major appliances are accepted on DPW bulk routes and at Fort Totten. Official guidance: **refrigerators with doors removed**; **air conditioners with water and fluids drained**. Book bulk through 311, or drop off at Fort Totten during resident hours with proof of DC residency. Appliances are **not** accepted at Special Waste Collection electronics tables (those events send large appliances back to bulk / Fort Totten). ([DPW Bulk Trash Collection](https://dpw.dc.gov/node/1652581); [DPW Special Waste / HHW](https://dpw.dc.gov/service/household-hazardous-waste-hhw-e-cycling-document-shredding))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

Covered electronic equipment is **banned from DC trash** (Sustainable Solid Waste Management Amendment Act). So TVs can't go out with bulk either.

Verified options:

1. **DPW Special Waste Collection Events** (free for DC residents; **register in advance** on Eventbrite; bring proof of residency). Accepts TVs, computers, monitors, phones, printers, cables and more. Check upcoming dates and register via the [DPW Special Waste page](https://dpw.dc.gov/service/household-hazardous-waste-hhw-e-cycling-document-shredding) (registration link: tinyurl.com/hhw-register).
2. **eCYCLE DC** manufacturer mailback and permanent drop-off options listed by DOEE for covered electronics. See [eCYCLE DC for residents](https://doee.dc.gov/service/eCYCLE-Residents).

For usable items, DOEE points residents to donation/repair via [Reuse DC](https://reuse.dc.gov).

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Paint:** The most convenient path is **PaintCare** year-round retail drop-off sites in the District (house paint, primers, stains, sealers, varnish in sealed original containers; limits and product rules apply). Find sites at [PaintCare DC](https://www.paintcare.org/states/district-of-columbia/) or call (855) 724-6809. Paint is also accepted at DPW Special Waste events.

**Other HHW** (solvents, pesticides, auto fluids, propane tanks up to 20 lbs, fluorescent lamps, many household chemicals): take to **DPW Special Waste Collection Events**. Free for DC residents with registration and proof of residency. Full accepted/prohibited lists are on the [DPW Special Waste page](https://dpw.dc.gov/service/household-hazardous-waste-hhw-e-cycling-document-shredding). Do **not** put HHW in trash, recycling, or bulk set-out.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** DC Department of Public Works (DPW) collects holiday trees and greenery curbside from DPW-serviced households (single-family homes and apartment buildings with three or fewer units). 2026-27 dates were not published as of November 10, 2026.

**Last season (2025-26):** Collection ran January 2 through February 28, 2026, from the front of the residence at the curb or in the tree-box area. Remove all ornaments and lights, and do not bag the trees or greenery. Do not put trees in DC food-waste drop-off bins. The collection schedule mirrored leaf collection: Monday Section A, Tuesday Section B, Wednesday Section C and Thursday Section D in Wards 1-8, with Friday for service requests and missed collections. If a tree was not picked up within 7 days, residents could call 311 starting January 9 and make a "Christmas Tree Removal - Seasonal" request. After February 28, 2026, trees and greenery were to be placed at the normal trash and recycling collection point and collected as space permitted. Inclement weather may delay collection. Households not serviced by DPW (buildings with more than three units) should ask the property owner. Trees collected through February 28 were used for mulch. ([DC DPW: Holiday Trees and Greenery Collection](https://dpw.dc.gov/page/holiday-trees-greenery-collection))

**Last-season weekday drop-off (January 2-February 28, 2026):** Bryant Street Sweep Shop, 201 Bryant Street NW, 8 a.m.-3 p.m.; Guy Mason Recreation Center, 3600 Calvert Street NW, 8 a.m.-4 p.m.; and the DPW Salt Storage Facility, 2700 South Capitol Street SE, 8 a.m.-2:30 p.m.

If you missed the DPW window, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

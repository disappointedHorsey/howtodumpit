# Junk Disposal in Mesa, AZ: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Mesa, Arizona, item by item. If you are a **Mesa Solid Waste customer**, you can schedule **Bulk Item Pick-up** for furniture, mattresses and TVs (paid, by appointment) and **Appliance Recycling** for fridges, washers and similar items. **Paint, chemicals, electronics and many appliances** go free to Mesa's **Household Hazardous Materials (HHM)** facility for residents who pay the Green & Clean fee. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call **480-644-6789** (bulk and appliance scheduling) or **480-644-2221** (general trash and recycling) before you set anything out.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Mattresses, bed frames and box springs are on the City's **Bulk Item Pick-up** accepted list. The service is for current Mesa Solid Waste customers, must be scheduled in advance, and starts at **$31.00 per load** (about 4'x8'x4'; maximum 4 loads per scheduled pickup). Non-Mesa customers start at **$39.59 per load**. Book online or call **480-644-6789**. ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

<!--LISTINGS-->

**Bed bugs:** The City asks every customer whether items have come in contact with bed bugs. There is **no extra charge**, but you must mark each affected item: write **"BB"** on a sheet of paper so crews can see it, and tape that paper to **each** item. ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

**Not at HHM:** The Household Hazardous Materials facility does **not** take mattresses, box springs, couches or exercise equipment; it points those items to Bulk Item Pick-up or reuse. ([City of Mesa: Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials))

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Bulk Item Pick-up:** Furniture (tables, chairs, couches and similar) is accepted on a scheduled curbside pickup for Mesa Solid Waste customers. Charges vary by load size; a load is about 4'x8'x4', with a maximum of 4 loads per scheduled pickup. Mesa customers start at **$31.00 per load**. Book online or call **480-644-6789** during business hours (Monday–Thursday 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., Friday 7 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Any past-due utility balance must be paid before you can request service. ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

### Step by Step

1. **Schedule first.** Use the online Bulk Item Pick-up Request or call 480-644-6789. Nothing is collected without a confirmed appointment.
2. **Place it right.** Put items at the **end of the driveway** (don't block sidewalks). Items not at the end of the driveway may not be collected.
3. **Set it out on time.** Place items anytime after **6 p.m. the night before**, and no later than **6 a.m.** on your scheduled day. A **$31.00 trip charge** applies if items aren't at the curb when the crew arrives, or if you cancel or reschedule with less than one business day's notice.
4. **Contain small pieces.** Bundle, box or bag smaller items. Bundle branches and carpet no more than 24" in diameter, 48" long, or 60 pounds. Put cacti in small cardboard boxes. Overweight bundles or single items over 60 pounds may be refused.
5. **Keep prohibited items out.** Mixing in appliances, construction debris, hazardous waste, liquids or tires can leave the whole load behind with a trip charge.

**Not accepted on bulk:** appliances (including microwaves and air compressors), construction and remodeling materials, hazardous waste (oil, paint, batteries, light bulbs, propane tanks), liquids, overweight items, and tires (tires go to the HHM facility or Maricopa County Waste Resources and Recycling at **602-506-4006**). ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

**HOAs and multi-unit homes:** Residents in an HOA or multi-unit community may need to contact their property manager for trash and recycling services. City resident programs cover single-family homes and similar dwellings where the occupant is the Mesa trash account holder. ([City of Mesa: Resident Trash & Recycling Programs](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs))

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers **Mesa city limits** and Mesa Solid Waste customers. **Maricopa County does not provide** solid waste, bulk trash, household hazardous waste or recycling collection: incorporated cities run their own programs, and **unincorporated** areas use **private haulers**. ([Maricopa County: Refuse Collection Services](https://www.maricopa.gov/2977/Refuse-Collection-Services)) Neighboring cities such as **Tempe**, **Gilbert** and **Chandler** run their own bulk programs, so check your own city's page ([Tempe bulk trash](https://www.tempe.gov/government/public-works/solid-waste-and-recycling/bulk-uncontained-garbage-and-green-organics); [Gilbert Bulk Trash](https://www.gilbertaz.gov/departments/public-works/solid-waste/bulk-trash); [Chandler Bulk Pickup](https://www.chandleraz.gov/residents/recycling-and-trash/curbside-bulk-collection)). Mesa's HHM facility is for Mesa residents who pay the Green & Clean fee; **Maricopa County island residents** who don't pay for Mesa trash at home are **not eligible**. ([City of Mesa: Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials)) If you live in an HOA or apartment with a private hauler, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Not on bulk trash:** Appliances are **prohibited** on Bulk Item Pick-up. Use the City's **Appliance Recycling** program instead. ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

**Appliance Recycling:** Curbside collection (scheduled in advance) covers air compressors, refrigerators, dishwashers, stoves, washers and dryers, water heaters, microwaves, evaporative coolers and more. Fees are added to your Mesa utility bill. For Mesa trash and recycling customers: **$19.00** per appliance without refrigerant, or **$19.00 plus an $11.00 Freon removal fee** for appliances with refrigerant. Non-Mesa customers pay **$28.42**, or **$28.42 plus $11.00** with Freon. A **$19.00 trip charge** applies if items aren't at the curb when the crew arrives, or if you cancel or reschedule with less than one business day's notice. Book online (staff call within 3 business days to schedule) or call **480-644-6789**. ([City of Mesa: Appliance Recycling](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Appliance-Recycling))

**Prep:** Empty all food and containers. For refrigerators and freezers, Arizona law requires you to remove attached doors, hinges, lids, glass shelves and latches (Arizona Revised Statutes § 36-1651). Incomplete prep can leave the appliance behind with a trip fee. ([City of Mesa: Appliance Recycling](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Appliance-Recycling))

**Free HHM drop-off:** Mesa residents may drop off appliances at the Household Hazardous Materials Facility at **no charge**, with a limit of **two appliances per day** on the Appliance Recycling page (the HHM accepted list also notes a daily appliance limit of 3; confirm with the facility before you go). ([City of Mesa: Appliance Recycling](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Appliance-Recycling); [Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials))

**Freon:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

**Salt River Landfill:** The City notes you can also haul appliances to the independently owned Salt River Landfill and pay that facility's fees (cash only). We couldn't verify those fees on a City page, so ask the landfill before you drive. ([City of Mesa: Appliance Recycling](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Appliance-Recycling))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Bulk Item Pick-up:** TVs are accepted (**one large or a few small**). ([City of Mesa: Bulk Item Pick-up](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Bulk-Item-Pick-up))

**HHM facility:** Computers, monitors, flat-screen and projector/tube TVs, printers, tablets, cell phones and similar electronics are accepted for Mesa residents at no additional charge (average household quantity). Wipe personal data first; Mesa is not responsible for data security. ([City of Mesa: Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials))

**Other options the City lists:** AzStRUT (Arizona Students Recycling Used Technology) and Best Buy. Check each program's own rules and any fees before you go.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Household Hazardous Materials facility:** Free for **Mesa residents who pay the Green & Clean fee**, with proof of residency (a Mesa-address driver's license, or a Mesa utility bill or rent receipt in your name plus matching photo ID). No business waste. Limit of one visit per day per household. Stay in your vehicle; staff unload at the drive-through. ([City of Mesa: Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials))

| Site | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Mesa HHM Facility | 2412 N. Center St., Bldg. 2, Mesa, AZ 85201 | Wednesday–Saturday, 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. Closed Sunday–Tuesday and city holidays (including the City's listed 2026 holiday closures) |

Phone (voicemail only; calls returned within 48 hours): **480-644-4463**. Email: **hhm.info@mesaaz.gov**.

**Paint:** Latex, oil-based, spray paint, stains, varnish, thinners and related products are accepted (containers larger than 5 gallons prohibited; 20-gallon combined maximum per day). For **water-based latex paint**, the City also says you may dry it completely in the can, replace the lid, and put it in your **black trash barrel**. ([City of Mesa: Household Hazardous Materials](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Resident-Programs/Household-Hazardous-Materials))

**Also accepted (limits apply):** automotive fluids and up to 5 tires (no rims) per visit, batteries, propane and other compressed gases, household cleaners, light bulbs, pool and garden chemicals, cooking oil (up to 10 gallons/day, containers no larger than 5 gallons), fire extinguishers, smoke detectors and more. Seal items, put them in boxes or totes (no bags), and mark them "HHM".

**Not accepted:** commercial and industrial waste, construction waste (including carpet — use Bulk Item Pick-up or a recycler), furniture and mattresses, and other categories listed on the City's not-accepted list. Explosives and large ammunition: contact Mesa Police at **480-644-2211**.

**Swap Shop:** Usable dropped-off items (including paint when available) may be free to Mesa residents during facility hours, first come, first served (limit 10 items per household; paint is for non-commercial exterior use only).

Questions? Call bulk and appliance scheduling at **480-644-6789**, general trash and recycling at **480-644-2221**, or the HHM facility at **480-644-4463**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Mesa Christmas Tree Recycling program returns December 26, 2026, through January 18, 2027. Self-service drop-off locations are available 24 hours a day during that window.

The service is only for live, non-artificial Christmas trees. Artificial trees, trees flocked with artificial snow, wreaths, and trees from commercial vendors are not accepted. All lights, ornaments, tree stands and nails must be removed. Do not leave trees outside the fenced enclosures. ([City of Mesa: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Utilities/Trash-Recycling/Mesa-Recycles/Christmas-Tree-Recycling))

Drop-off locations: Dobson Ranch Library, 2363 S. Dobson Road; Fitch Park, 261 E. 8th Street; Mountain View Park, 845 N. Lindsay Road; East Mesa Service Center, 6935 E. Decatur Street; and Superstition, 2430 E. Ellsworth.

Residents with a green yard-waste barrel may place a small tree inside the barrel. The tree must fit completely inside with the lid properly closed. Curbside bulk pickup of a Christmas tree is available for a fee; the City directs you to its Bulk Pick-up page or 480-644-6789 for details and scheduling (no amount is repeated here). Mesa also accepts donations of live potted Christmas trees, 5 feet or taller, for planting in Mesa parks, at Parks, Recreation and Community Facilities offices listed on the same page.

If you cannot get to a site or fit the tree in the barrel, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

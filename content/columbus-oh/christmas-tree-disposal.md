# Christmas Tree Recycling in Columbus, OH (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Columbus collects holiday trees at the curb with biweekly yard-waste collection for homes that get City trash service in 90-gallon or 300-gallon containers; strip every decoration and cut the tree in half if it's taller than 8 feet. Franklin County residents can also drop a live tree at SWACO's Recycling Convenience Center on Jackson Pike. If you'd rather not haul it yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of Columbus and SWACO pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Columbus Curbside Yard-Waste Collection {#yard-waste}

- **Standing rule, no seasonal window:** the City says holiday trees are collected with yard waste. Columbus partners with Rumpke for biweekly yard-waste collection for households that receive City trash collection from 90-gallon or 300-gallon containers. ([City of Columbus: Common Questions](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/How-to-Dispose-or-Recycle/Common-Questions))
- **Prep:** the tree must be completely free of decorations and, if taller than 8 feet, cut in half.
- **Placement:** at the curb in front of your home, at least 3 feet from trash and recycling containers, fire hydrants, mailboxes and trees. Don't put it in a plastic bag or your City trash container. ([City of Columbus: Yard Waste Collection](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/Yard-Waste-Collection))
- **The catch:** pickup is every other week, so you need your yard-waste day. Find it on the City's collection day lookup or call 311 at (614) 645-3111.

## Drop It Off Yourself: SWACO Recycling Convenience Center {#drop-off}

The Solid Waste Authority of Central Ohio's **Recycling Convenience Center**, 2566 Jackson Pike, accepts **holiday live trees and greenery** from Franklin County residents. It's open **Monday to Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.**, and **Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, with limited hours and closures on SWACO's posted 2026 and 2027 holiday schedules. Trees aren't on its fee list. ([SWACO: Recycling Convenience Center](https://www.swaco.org/disposal/facilities/recycling-convenience-center/))

**City yard-waste drop-off:** Columbus residents can drop off yard waste free at the City's Waste and Reuse Convenience Centers, 2100 Alum Creek Drive and 1550 Georgesville Road, Tuesday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. The City page doesn't name Christmas trees, so call 311 before you bring one. ([Yard Waste Collection](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/Yard-Waste-Collection))

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

There's no collection on City holidays, including **Christmas Day (Friday, December 25, 2026)** and **New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, 2027)**. Biweekly recycling and yard-waste collection that falls on or after the holiday is delayed one day that week, **with Friday's collection on Saturday**. Trash customers with a collection color move forward a day after each holiday; customers on a two-day window are collected on the window's first or second day, depending on whether their day falls before or after the holiday. ([City of Columbus: Common Questions](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/How-to-Dispose-or-Recycle/Common-Questions))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Does Columbus pick up Christmas trees?

Yes. Holiday trees go out with biweekly yard-waste collection for homes with City 90-gallon or 300-gallon trash containers. Remove all decorations first.

### Do I have to cut my Christmas tree in Columbus?

Only if it's taller than 8 feet. Then cut it in half before you set it at the curb.

### Where can I drop off a Christmas tree in Columbus?

At SWACO's Recycling Convenience Center, 2566 Jackson Pike, which takes live holiday trees from Franklin County residents.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Columbus?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

# Junk Disposal in Fort Worth, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Fort Worth, Texas, item by item. If you get City garbage service, Fort Worth picks up **one bulk pile a month, up to 10 cubic yards, at no extra charge** during your assigned bulk week. You can also haul most junk to one of four City **Drop-Off Stations**, which take bulk waste, freon appliances, electronics and household chemicals. Paint and chemicals also go to the **Environmental Collection Center** in east Fort Worth. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call the 311 Fort Worth Contact Center (817-392-1234) before you load up the car.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Monthly curbside bulk collection (free up to 10 cubic yards), or a City Drop-Off Station |
| Couch / furniture | Monthly curbside bulk collection, or a City Drop-Off Station; donate usable furniture |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Not allowed in curbside bulk. Take it to a City Drop-Off Station (freon doesn't need to be removed; remove food and doors) |
| TV / computer | No curbside collection. City Drop-Off Stations take computers and TVs (limit two every six months) |
| Paint / chemicals | Environmental Collection Center (up to 30 gallons of paint) or a Drop-Off Station (20 gallons of household chemicals every 3 months) |
| Christmas tree | Latest official City guidance is dated December 28, 2022; confirm with 311 for this season |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** If you get City garbage service, put it in your **monthly curbside bulk pile** (free up to 10 cubic yards) by **7 a.m. on the Monday of your bulk week**. Or haul it to a **City Drop-Off Station** with your water bill and driver's license.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Monthly Curbside Bulk Collection

1. Check whether it's still usable. The City's Waste Wizard says box springs and mattresses in good, clean condition can be donated; soiled or worn-out ones should go to a Drop-Off Station or out for bulk collection. Note that the Goodwill donation stations at the Drop-Off Stations take furniture **except bedding and mattresses**. ([City of Fort Worth Waste Wizard, on the Bulk Waste Collection page](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk), [Brennan Drop-off Station](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff/brennan-drop-off))
2. Find your bulk week on the City's [bulk collection schedule with map](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk) (PDF linked from the bulk page), or in the City's Garbage & Recycling app.
3. Set it out **by 7 a.m. on the Monday** of your assigned week, and **no earlier than 6 p.m. the Friday before**. Crews have until the following **Saturday at 5 p.m.** to collect.
4. Keep the pile **at least two feet** from trees, mailboxes, water meters and fences, and not under low branches, or it won't be collected. Only **one pile per household**, up to **10 cubic yards** (about 8 feet long, 6 feet wide and 6 feet tall). Don't put anything in plastic bags.
5. After crews collect on your street, don't add more until next month's bulk week.

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** set it out early or in the wrong week. Out-of-cycle or non-compliant piles cost **$123.52 per 5 cubic yards** ($123.52 minimum) and **$107.40 per 5 cubic yards** after that, up to 30 cubic yards. Piles over 10 cubic yards also carry an extra charge.
- **Don't** mix in garbage in bags, tires, electronics, freon appliances, contractor remodeling debris, dirt, rocks, concrete, liquids or glass.

*Sources: [Bulk Waste Collection](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk) and [Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff). Checked 10/20/2026. Rules change, so confirm on the official page before you go.*

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** Couches, chairs, tables and other furniture go in your **monthly bulk pile** under the same rules as mattresses above: one pile per household, up to **10 cubic yards free**, out by **7 a.m. Monday** of your bulk week. The City's Waste Wizard lists furniture for both bulky item pickup and the Drop-Off Stations. ([Bulk Waste Collection](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk))

**Self-haul:** The four **Drop-Off Stations** accept bulk waste (anything larger than a garbage cart) from residents with an active City solid waste account. Bring a current water bill showing sanitation charges and a matching driver's license. Apartment residents can bring only recycling and household hazardous waste. Loads must be secured or covered, and trailers can be single-axle only, up to 8 feet long and 6 feet wide, with a load no higher than 3 feet. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))

**Donate:** Each Drop-Off Station has a **Goodwill donation station** for gently used furniture (except bedding and mattresses), appliances, bicycles, books and more, with a tax receipt. The City's Waste Wizard also points to the **Tarrant County Furniture Bank**, which gives basic furniture to people moving out of homelessness. ([Brennan Drop-off Station](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff/brennan-drop-off))

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Fort Worth** only. Residents of **unincorporated Tarrant County** can use the City's Drop-Off Stations with a one-time **Tarrant County voucher**, bought from the County in advance: a Type A voucher ($19.50) covers furniture, large appliances, small electronics, brush and recyclables, and a Type B voucher ($32.50) adds freon appliances, TVs and monitors (3 each), tires (4) and household construction debris, up to 5 cubic yards per visit. Bring the physical voucher and your driver's license; household chemicals aren't accepted on either voucher. ([Tarrant County: Drop-Off Station Voucher Program](https://www.tarrantcountytx.gov/content/dam/main/engineering-services/engineering-updates/DOSBrochureFinal_JAN2026.pdf)) **Arlington** residents can set out up to two cubic yards of bulky items (about the size of a washer and dryer) on their weekly collection day, by 7 a.m. ([City of Arlington: Trash Cart Guide](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/News-Articles/2024/January/Your-Guide-to-the-Trash-Cart-Rollout-that-Begins-in-February)) Other neighboring cities set their own rules, so check your city's website. If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Curbside bulk collection does **not** take household appliances that contain **coolant, gasoline or other chemicals**, including **air conditioners, refrigerators and lawnmowers**. ([Bulk Waste Collection](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk))

**Drop-Off Stations** accept appliances such as **refrigerators and air conditioners**. **Freon does not need to be removed**, but **food must be removed**, and the City's Waste Wizard asks you to **remove all doors** from a refrigerator. Small lawn and garden equipment is accepted if you drain all liquids, gasoline and oil. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))

For large appliances without coolant (such as a washer or dryer), the City's Waste Wizard lists both the Drop-Off Stations and bulky item pickup. If you're unsure, call 817-392-1234.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

There's **no curbside collection** for TVs and other electronics, and electronic equipment such as computers isn't allowed in bulk piles. ([Bulk Waste Collection](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk))

- **Drop-Off Stations** accept **electronics**, with a limit of **two computers and televisions every six months**. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))
- The **Environmental Collection Center** does **not** take appliances or electronics. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/hazardouswaste))
- The City also points to the state's manufacturer take-back programs, **Texas Recycles Computers** and **Texas Recycles TVs**, run by the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ, 512-239-3143). ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/hazardouswaste))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

There's no curbside collection for paint or other household hazardous waste. You have two City options:

**Environmental Collection Center (ECC)**, 6400 Bridge St., Fort Worth, TX 76112 (just east of downtown, with easy access from I-30 and 820). Open **Thursday and Friday, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.**, and **Saturday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.**; closed Sunday through Wednesday. Bring proof of residence (a current water bill or valid driver's license). It's for residential waste only. Stay in your vehicle and staff unload. Per-visit limits: **paint and painting supplies, 30 gallons**; automotive fluids, cleaning chemicals and cooking oil, 10 gallons each; lawn, garden and pool chemicals, 20 gallons; light bulbs, 20. Leave products in their original, labeled containers (no trash bags). A **Help-Yourself Shelf** offers like-new paint and cleaners free. ([Environmental Collection Center](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/environmental-collection-center/environmental-collection-center))

The ECC does **not** accept ammunition or explosives, appliances or electronics, building materials, bulk trash or yard waste, butane or propane cylinders, medicines, tires, or asbestos, PCBs and radioactive items. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/hazardouswaste))

**Drop-Off Stations** also take household hazardous waste, including **paint**, with a limit of **20 gallons of household chemicals per household every 3 months**, plus light bulbs (up to 10 fluorescent tubes every 3 months) and all household and auto batteries. Fort Worth apartment residents can use a Drop-Off Station for household hazardous waste with a driver's license. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))

We didn't find a City page with dry-it-out-and-trash instructions for latex paint, so take leftover paint to the ECC or a Drop-Off Station, or ask 311.

Not sure where something goes? Use the City's Waste Wizard or Garbage & Recycling app, or contact the 311 Fort Worth Contact Center at 817-392-1234 or 1234@fortworthtexas.gov (7 a.m. to 6 p.m. weekdays, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturdays). ([Bulk Waste Collection](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/bulk))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The latest official City of Fort Worth guidance we could verify is a December 28, 2022 news release. 2025-26 and 2026-27 Christmas tree dates were not found on fortworthtexas.gov during this November 10, 2026 check. Confirm with 311 (817-392-1234) before you set a tree out.

That 2022 release said live Christmas trees can go out with normal weekly yard waste pickup after the holidays. Trees had to be less than 8 feet long (cut in two pieces if needed). Remove all decorations, lights and tree stands, and do not put the tree in a plastic bag. Flocked trees were accepted. Artificial trees were to go in the brown garbage cart. Residents could also take trees to any of the City's four drop-off stations: 2400 Brennan Avenue; 5150 Martin Luther King Freeway; 6260 Old Hemphill Road; and 301 Hillshire Drive. Trees with decorations left on were to be placed in the trash at the stations; otherwise they would be recycled. ([City of Fort Worth: Place Real Christmas Trees Curbside or Drop Them Off](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/news/2022/12/xmas-trees))

The City's current [Yard Trimmings](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/yardtrimmings) page still lists those four drop-off stations (Tuesday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m., Saturday 8 a.m.-noon; closed Sunday and Monday) but does not mention Christmas trees by name.

If you need a tree taken from the house, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

# Christmas Tree Recycling in Phoenix, AZ (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Take your bare live tree to a City of Phoenix drop-off park or transfer station any time between Monday, December 28, 2026, and Monday, January 11, 2027, during normal business hours; it's composted for free. You can also put it in a bulk trash pile or cut it to fit your black or green organics bin. If you'd rather not haul it yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of Phoenix Public Works pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of Phoenix Christmas Tree Composting {#tree-composting}

- **2026-27 dates: published.** Drop off live trees, wreaths and garlands from Monday, December 28, 2026, through Monday, January 11, 2027, during normal business hours. Trees are composted at the City of Phoenix Compost Facility. ([City of Phoenix: Christmas Tree Composting](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/popular-requests-resources/christmas-tree-composting.html); [City Calendar: Live Christmas Trees Accepted for Composting](https://www.phoenix.gov/calendar/public-works-events/live-christmas-trees-accepted-for-composting--free--.html))
- **Prep:** live trees, wreaths and garlands only, nothing plastic or artificial. Remove all decorations, lights, tinsel, the stand and any nails or metal stakes. Bring trees unbagged and tie them down for the drive with something easy to cut.
- **Flocked trees** can't be composted: put them in your black landfill container or take them to a transfer station.
- **The catch:** there's no special curbside tree pickup. You drive the tree to a site and leave it only in the marked drop-off area.

**Other City options:** the transfer stations take up to **two live trees** from City of Phoenix residents. For more than two, residents with their City services bill and matching photo ID can use their **one free load of up to 1 ton**. You can also add the tree to a **bulk trash** pile (each solid waste customer gets up to 4 bulk trash appointments a year, and the count resets January 1), or cut it to fit inside your black bin or green organics bin.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

There's no collection on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, 2026: Friday customers are collected Saturday, December 26. There's also no collection on New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027: Friday customers are collected Saturday, January 2. Other days don't change. ([City of Phoenix: Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/popular-requests-resources/holiday-collection-schedule.html))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where can I drop off my Christmas tree in Phoenix?

At any of 14 City parks, such as Deer Valley, Paradise Valley, Washington, Madison, El Reposo and Cesar Chavez parks, or at the North Gateway and 27th Avenue transfer stations.

### When does Phoenix Christmas tree recycling start in 2026?

Monday, December 28, 2026. It runs through Monday, January 11, 2027.

### Does Phoenix pick up Christmas trees at the curb?

Not as a separate program. You can include the tree in a bulk trash appointment, or cut it to fit your black or green organics bin.

### Can I recycle a flocked Christmas tree in Phoenix?

No. Flocking is synthetic, so the City says to put a flocked tree in your black landfill container or take it to a transfer station.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Phoenix?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

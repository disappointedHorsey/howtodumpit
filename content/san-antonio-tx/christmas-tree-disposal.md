# Christmas Tree Drop-Off in San Antonio, TX (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** The City of San Antonio takes live Christmas trees at its drop-off centers during the first two weeks of January each year and turns them into mulch. The 2027 dates weren't posted when we checked on November 25, 2026; last season ran January 3-14, 2026, at four centers. If you'd rather not haul the tree yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of San Antonio Solid Waste Management Department pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of San Antonio Christmas Tree Drop-Off {#tree-drop-off}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 25, 2026). The official page still shows the 2026 dates (January 3-14, 2026) and says drop-off is offered during the first two weeks of January each year. ([City of San Antonio: Christmas Tree Drop-Off](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Christmas-Tree))
- **Prep:** live trees only. If the tree is over 6 feet, cut it in half. Remove all lights, decorations and stands, even wooden ones, and don't bag the tree.
- **Not accepted:** live wreaths, garlands and plants.
- **Tarp law:** cover brush loads with a tarp or a $5 penalty may be assessed.
- **The catch:** the window is short (about two weeks) and you have to drive the tree to a center yourself.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

During Christmas week (December 24-26, 2026), Friday collection slides to Saturday, December 26. During New Year's week (January 1-2, 2027), Friday collection slides to Saturday, January 2. Other days don't change. ([City of San Antonio: My Collection Day](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Garbage/My-Collection-Day))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When is Christmas tree drop-off in San Antonio?

During the first two weeks of January. The 2027 dates weren't posted as of November 25, 2026; last season ran January 3-14, 2026.

### Where can I drop off my Christmas tree in San Antonio?

At the Culebra Road, Frio City Road and Rigsby Road drop-off centers, or the Bitters Brush Recycling Center on Wurzbach Parkway.

### Do I have to cut my Christmas tree in San Antonio?

Only if it's over 6 feet: then cut it in half. Remove the stand and all decorations, and don't bag it.

### Is San Antonio trash pickup different during Christmas week 2026?

Only Friday routes change. Friday, December 25, 2026, collection happens on Saturday, December 26.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in San Antonio?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

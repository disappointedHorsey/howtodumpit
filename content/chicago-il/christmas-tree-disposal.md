# Christmas Tree Recycling in Chicago, IL (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Drop your bare natural tree inside the recycling corral at one of 27 Chicago parks and sites from January 9 to 23, 2027, 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. The City's program is drop-off only, and its page doesn't describe curbside tree pickup, so if you can't get the tree to a corral, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 19, 2026, against official City of Chicago Department of Streets and Sanitation pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Chicago Holiday Tree Recycling Program {#tree-recycling}

- **2026-27 dates: published.** January 9-23, 2027, at 27 locations citywide, run by the Department of Streets and Sanitation with the Chicago Park District. Corrals are open 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. ([Chicago Recycles: Holiday Tree Recycling](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/chicago-recycles/home/holiday-tree-recycling.html))
- **Prep:** natural trees only. Remove all tinsel, lights, ornaments, tree stands and plastic bags, then place the tree inside the designated corral.
- **Free mulch:** the trees are chipped, and residents can pick up the mulch free at six of the locations starting Tuesday, January 12, 2027.
- **Last season:** over 23,500 trees were recycled in 2026, the City says.
- **Questions:** email dsscomposting@cityofchicago.org or call 312-744-2413.
- **The catch:** the program doesn't start until January 9, 2027, and it only runs two weeks. Trees go in the corral, not at the curb.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, 2026, has no trash service citywide and no recycling service in any service area. ([City of Chicago: Holiday Garbage Schedule](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/streets/provdrs/streets_san/svcs/non-scheduled_garbage.html)) The City's page doesn't say which day missed collections are made up, and it lists 2026 holidays only, so the New Year's Day 2027 schedule was not posted as of November 19, 2026.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When is Chicago Christmas tree recycling 2027?

January 9-23, 2027, at 27 locations, 7 a.m. to 11 p.m.

### Does Chicago pick up Christmas trees at the curb?

The City's Holiday Tree Recycling page describes drop-off corrals only and doesn't mention curbside tree pickup. Ask 311 before you put a tree out.

### Where can I get free tree mulch in Chicago?

At six sites starting Tuesday, January 12, 2027: the Forestry Site, Lincoln Park, Margate Park, Mt. Greenwood Park, North Park Village and Warren Park.

### Is there garbage pickup in Chicago on Christmas Day 2026?

No. Friday, December 25, 2026, has no trash service citywide and no recycling service.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Chicago?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

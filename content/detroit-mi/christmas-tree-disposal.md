# Christmas Tree Recycling in Detroit, MI (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Last season, the City of Detroit Department of Public Works collected Christmas trees at the curb during the first two weeks of January. The 2026-27 pickup notice had not been posted when we checked on November 25, 2026, and regular yard-waste collection ends December 19, 2026. If you'd rather not wait or haul it yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of Detroit Department of Public Works pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: DPW Christmas Tree Pickup {#tree-pickup}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 25, 2026). The latest City notice is last season's.
- **Last season (2025-26):** a City news post dated December 9, 2025, said "Christmas trees will be collected during the first two weeks of January." It did not list set-out rules or size limits. ([City of Detroit: Christmas Tree Pick-up Information](https://detroitmi.gov/news/christmas-tree-pick-information))
- **Prep:** no official prep rules were posted; ask DPW at (313) 876-0004 how to set the tree out.
- **The catch:** the pickup is a short window, and DPW's regular yard-waste season runs only **March 30 to December 19, 2026**. ([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection Guide (PDF)](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))

## Drop It Off Yourself: DPW Citizen Drop-Off Centers {#drop-off}

We didn't find an official Detroit Christmas tree drop-off site. DPW's **Citizen Drop-Off Centers** take **yard waste** free from Detroit residents with proper ID, up to 1,000 pounds a day, but the City pages don't name Christmas trees; call DPW before you bring one. They won't take tree logs more than 8 inches across or 8 feet long. ([City of Detroit: Free Citizen Bulk Drop-Off Centers](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste/free-citizen-bulk-drop-centers))

| Center | Address | Winter Hours (November 1-March 31) |
|---|---|---|
| Southfield Yard | 12255 Southfield Rd. | Monday-Saturday, 8 a.m.-4 p.m. |
| Davison Yard | W. Davison (City pages list 7901 and 8221 W. Davison) | Monday-Saturday, 8 a.m.-4 p.m. |
| J. Fons Transfer Station | 6451 E. McNichols | Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-4 p.m.; Saturday, 8 a.m.-noon |

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

DPW says collection is **pushed back one day** following Christmas and New Year's Day. Both fall on a Friday this season (December 25, 2026, and January 1, 2027), so check your day with the City's Waste Pickup Schedule tool or by texting your street address to (313) 800-7905 for reminders. ([2026 Curbside Collection Guide (PDF)](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))

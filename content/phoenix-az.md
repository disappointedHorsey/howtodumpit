# Junk Disposal in Phoenix, AZ: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Phoenix, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Bulk trash appointment (no bed-bug items) |
| Couch / furniture | Bulk trash appointment |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Not in bulk trash; accepted at City transfer stations |
| TV / computer | Transfer stations (CRTs: 2/month free) |
| Paint / chemicals | HHW home collection (reservations currently closed) |
| Christmas tree | Free drop-off December 28, 2026-January 11, 2027, at designated City parks |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** Phoenix bulk trash is **appointment-only**. Book online at **phoenix.gov/bulktrash** or call **602-262-6251**, at least **3 business days** ahead. Put the mattress out **within 5 feet of the curb** no more than 7 days before, and **by 5:30 a.m.** on your date. **Mattresses known or suspected to have bed bugs are not accepted.**

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step

1. **Book an appointment** (online or 602-262-6251). Nothing goes out before you have a confirmed date.
2. Set the mattress out **no sooner than 7 days before** your appointment.
3. Place it **curbside on your property**, parallel to the street, **no more than 5 feet** from the street/curb and **at least 5 feet** from fixed objects (mailboxes, meters, walls). Not on top of water meters and **not in the alley**.
4. Make sure it's out **by 5:30 a.m.** on the appointment date.

### What the City Won't Take

Per Phoenix City Code, **all mattresses or furniture known or suspected to be infested with bed bugs** are not acceptable bulk trash. The city flyer also excludes tires, household hazardous waste, appliances with refrigerants, and piles over 10 cubic yards. The City blocks bulk appointments around Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's.

### Need It Gone Sooner?

Self-haul to a city transfer station (see listing above) or use a paid junk removal service.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

Furniture is bulk trash. **Book a bulk trash appointment** (phoenix.gov/bulktrash or 602-262-6251) and follow the placement rules in the mattress section: within 5 feet of the curb, by 5:30 a.m., no earlier than 7 days before. Furniture known or suspected to have **bed bugs** is not accepted. ([Phoenix Bulk Trash](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/bulk-trash-pickup.html))


### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers **Phoenix city limits** and City of Phoenix bulk-trash customers. **Maricopa County does not provide** solid waste, bulk trash, household hazardous waste or recycling collection: incorporated cities run their own programs, and **unincorporated** areas use **private haulers**. ([Maricopa County: Refuse Collection Services](https://www.maricopa.gov/2977/Refuse-Collection-Services)) Neighboring cities such as **Scottsdale** and **Tempe** run their own bulk programs, so check your own city's page ([Scottsdale Brush and Bulk Collection](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/solid-waste/collection-services/brush-and-bulk-collection); [Tempe bulk trash and green organics](https://www.tempe.gov/government/public-works/solid-waste-and-recycling/bulk-uncontained-garbage-and-green-organics)). If you live in an HOA or apartment with a private hauler, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

The bulk trash flyer excludes **appliances that use refrigerants**. The City's **transfer stations** accept appliances, including refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, washers/dryers, water heaters, stoves and dishwashers. See the City's Appliance Recycling page for current terms. ([Phoenix Transfer Stations](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/about-us/transfer-stations.html))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

Phoenix **transfer stations** accept CRT and non-CRT TVs. Residents can bring **up to two CRT units per household per month free**; the City lists a fee for additional CRTs (see the official page). The City also points residents to **Westech Recyclers** for computers, phones, monitors and printers. ([Phoenix Transfer Stations](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/about-us/transfer-stations.html); [Phoenix HHW page](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/household-hazardous-waste-collection.html))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Phoenix solid waste customers can get a **free HHW home collection** (one per customer per calendar year). **As of October 12, 2026, the City's request form says reservations are currently closed.** To find out when they reopen, email **hhwcollection@phoenix.gov**. Transfer stations do not accept regulated hazardous waste. ([myPHX311 HHW](https://myphx311.phoenix.gov/householdhazardouswaste/); [Phoenix HHW](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/household-hazardous-waste-collection.html))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** Phoenix Public Works will accept live Christmas trees, wreaths and garlands for free composting from Monday, December 28, 2026, through Monday, January 11, 2027, at designated City parks during normal business hours.

Only live items are accepted, nothing plastic or artificial. Strip off all lights, decorations and other non-organic material, and place trees only in the marked drop-off areas. The trees are composted at the City of Phoenix Compost Facility. ([City of Phoenix calendar: Live Christmas Trees Accepted for Composting](https://www.phoenix.gov/calendar/public-works-events/live-christmas-trees-accepted-for-composting--free--.html))

The 2026-27 calendar pages we read confirm the date window but do not list park names on the event text. Last season (December 26, 2025, through January 9, 2026) used 14 parks: Cactus, Cesar Chavez, Deer Valley, Desert Foothills, Desert West, El Reposo, Los Olivos, Madison, Marivue, Mountain View, Mountain Vista, Paradise Valley, Sereno and Washington. That list may change. Confirm this season's sites on the official calendar before you drive. ([City of Phoenix: Christmas Tree Drop-off Sites Open December 26](https://www.phoenix.gov/newsroom/public-works-news/3311.html))

Last season's news release also noted transfer station drop-off and bulk trash as other disposal options. Ask Public Works or 602-262-3111 before you use those for a tree.

If a park drop-off is not practical, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

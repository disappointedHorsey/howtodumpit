# Christmas Tree Pickup in Fort Worth, TX (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** The City of Fort Worth's latest Christmas tree notice says a bare live tree under 8 feet can go out with your normal weekly yard waste pickup, or to one of the City's four drop-off stations. The City had not posted any 2026-27 tree dates or rules when we checked on November 18, 2026, so call 311 (817-392-1234) to confirm. If you want the tree gone sooner, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 18, 2026, against official City of Fort Worth Environmental Services pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of Fort Worth Weekly Yard Waste Pickup {#tree-collection}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 18, 2026). The most recent Christmas tree notice on fortworthtexas.gov is dated December 28, 2022. ([City of Fort Worth: Place real Christmas trees curbside or drop them off](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/news/2022/12/xmas-trees))
- **What that notice said (2022-23 season, the latest posted):** live trees go out with the normal weekly yard waste pickup after the holidays. Trees must be less than 8 feet long (cut in two pieces if needed). Take off all decorations, lights and the tree stand, and don't put the tree in a plastic bag. Flocked trees were accepted. Artificial trees go in the brown garbage cart. ([December 28, 2022 notice](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/news/2022/12/xmas-trees))
- **Set-out times:** carts and materials must be out by 7 a.m. on your collection day; crews collect from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., Monday to Friday. ([City of Fort Worth: Yard Trimmings](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/yardtrimmings))
- **Missed pickup:** report it within 24 hours through the MyFW app, 311 or 817-392-1234, and leave the material at the curb. ([Yard Trimmings](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/yardtrimmings))
- **The catch:** the City hasn't posted tree-specific guidance since 2022, trees must be cut under 8 feet, and a tree with any lights or tinsel left on may not be taken as yard waste.

## Drop It Off Yourself: City of Fort Worth Drop-Off Stations {#drop-off}

- **Sites:** Brennan, 2400 Brennan Ave.; Southeast, 5150 Martin Luther King Fwy.; Old Hemphill Road, 6260 Old Hemphill Rd.; Hillshire (North Service Center), 301 Hillshire Dr. ([City of Fort Worth: Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))
- **Hours:** Tuesday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Saturday, 8 a.m. to noon; closed Sunday and Monday, and closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff))
- **Who can use them:** residents with an active City of Fort Worth solid waste account. Bring a current water bill (within six months) showing sanitation charges plus a matching driver's license. Apartment residents can only drop off recycling and household hazardous waste, so yard waste such as a tree isn't covered for them. The City says the stations are free of charge to residents. ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff); [Garbage Service](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/garbage))
- **Trees:** the 2022 notice said to remove all lights, decorations and stands; decorated trees go in the trash, bare ones are recycled. ([December 28, 2022 notice](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/news/2022/12/xmas-trees))

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026: Monday to Thursday collections don't change and Friday collection moves to Saturday, December 26. ([City of Fort Worth: 2026 Holiday Collection Schedule (PDF)](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/3/environmental-services/solid-waste/documents/envd-solid-waste-holiday-schedule-2026-ada.pdf)) On your first pickup after Christmas Day and after New Year's Day, you may put two extra bags of garbage next to your cart. ([Garbage Service](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/garbage)) The New Year's Day 2027 collection schedule was not posted as of November 18, 2026.

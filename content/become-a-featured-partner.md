# Become a featured partner

HowToDumpIt exists to help people get rid of junk the right way. That means donating and recycling what's still usable, keeping as much as possible out of the landfill, and pointing people to the local haulers and charities who do this work every day. If you run one of those businesses or organizations, we'd love to have you in our city guides.

## Three ways to be listed

### 1. Basic listing: free

Any legitimate local junk-removal business, hauler, cleanout crew, reuse center or charity that serves one of our [cities](/#cities) can get a basic listing at **no cost**: your **name and website**. There's no catch and no obligation to upgrade.

### 2. Verified partner: $8 per call or email

- A **"Call now" button**
- **Your own tracking phone number** on our page that forwards to your real phone
- A **quote form** so customers can email you a request
- You pay **$8 for each tracked call (60 seconds or more) or email** we send you

### 3. Featured partner: $12 per call or email

- **The one Featured spot at the top** of a city's hauler section, clearly labeled **"Featured partner (paid)"**
- Everything a Verified partner gets, plus your logo and a short blurb
- **Only one Featured partner per city, first come, first served.** It goes to the first hauler who says yes and stays yours for as long as you're a partner.
- You pay **$12 for each tracked call (60 seconds or more) or email** we send you

People mostly call whoever is listed first, so the top spot is where most calls go, but the choice is yours.

## Getting started

- **New partners: your first 5 calls or emails are free.**
- **No leads, no pay.** After your free 5, you pay only for each real customer who contacts you through us.
- **No upfront fee, no card and no contract.**
- **Founding partners keep the terms they signed up on.**

## How billing works

- **Calls count only at 60 seconds or more**, so quick hang-ups and wrong numbers are never billed.
- **We bill only once you owe at least $25.**
- **Set a monthly spending cap** if you like, and **pause or cancel anytime**.
- **Any lead that wasn't a real customer enquiry is removed** if you flag it.
- **A monthly list of every call and email** we sent you, so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

It's a simple way to help your neighbors dispose of things responsibly, keep usable items out of the landfill, and support a local business: yours.

## Nonprofits and charities

Nonprofits and charities can still get a standard free listing, and can also choose Featured or Verified on the same terms as any partner.

## Honest disclosure

Featured spots are always labeled **"Featured partner (paid)"** on our city pages so readers can tell them apart from our regular listings and trust the guide. Our city guides are still based on official city sources, and being a partner never changes what we say about city programs, rules or fees.

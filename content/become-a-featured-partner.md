# Become a featured partner

HowToDumpIt exists to help people get rid of junk the right way. That means donating and recycling what's still usable, keeping as much as possible out of the landfill, and pointing people to the local haulers and charities who do this work every day. If you run one of those businesses or organizations, we'd love to have you in our city guides.

## A free basic listing, for every legitimate local business or charity

Any legitimate local junk-removal business, hauler, cleanout crew, reuse center or charity that serves one of our [cities](/#cities) can get a basic listing at **no cost**. There's no catch and no obligation to upgrade. Basic listings help our readers find good local options, and that's the point of the guide.

## The Featured partner option

If you'd like more visibility, you can become the **Featured partner** for your category in a city. A Featured partner gets:

- **The top spot** in that city's hauler section, clearly labeled **"Featured partner"**
- **Your logo**
- **A "Book now" button** that goes straight to your booking page or phone number
- **A short blurb** about what you do and why people should choose you

**Only one Featured partner per category per city.** We keep it that way so the spot means something, for you and for our readers.

### How it's paid for

- **No upfront fee.** There's nothing to pay to get started.
- **You pay only for results:** a small flat referral fee per booked job, or a percentage, agreed with you in advance.
- **A monthly report** of the clicks and leads we sent you, so you can see exactly what you're paying for.
- **If we send you nothing, you pay nothing.**

## Nonprofits are always free

ReStores, reuse centers, furniture banks and other nonprofits that take donations can be featured **free of charge, always**. Getting usable furniture, building materials and appliances to people who need them is exactly what this guide is for.

## Honest disclosure

Featured spots are always labeled **"Featured partner"** on our city pages so readers can tell them apart from our regular listings and trust the guide. Our city guides are still based on official city sources, and being a partner never changes what we say about city programs, rules or fees.

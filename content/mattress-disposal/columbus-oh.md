# How to get rid of a mattress in Columbus, OH

**Short answer:** If you live in the City of Columbus, the city will pick up a mattress **for free**, but only if you **schedule it first** through 311 at (614) 645-3111 or online at 311.columbus.gov. If you can haul it yourself, you can also drop it off for free at one of two city convenience centers (Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 5).

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step: free city pickup

1. Call **311 / (614) 645-3111** or book online at 311.columbus.gov. The city says nothing is collected without a booking.
2. On your scheduled day only, put the mattress in front of your home, clear of obstructions. If your trash is collected in the alley, put it at the alley line instead.
3. Bags, boxes, and tubs of loose stuff don't count as bulk. Those go in your regular trash container.

## What the city won't take with bulk pickup

Refrigerated appliances (fridges, freezers, dehumidifiers, AC units), furnaces, pianos, hot tubs and spas, pool tables, tires and auto parts, building materials, and hazardous materials. The city also won't collect items from an eviction, set-out, or foreclosure. For household hazardous waste, contact SWACO at (614) 871-5100.

## Live outside city limits?

Suburbs and townships around Columbus run their own trash contracts, so check your own city or township's waste page. *(Suburb-by-suburb section: to be researched.)*

## Need it gone today?

If you can't wait for a scheduled pickup, a paid junk removal service is the fallback. *(Paid options will be added once they're verified.)*

## Reporting illegal dumping

Report it through 311, or call the Franklin County Environmental Justice Program at (614) 871-5322.

# How to get rid of a mattress in Chicago, IL

**Short answer:** If you live in a **single-family home or a building with four units or fewer** served by Chicago Department of Streets and Sanitation (DSS), bulk waste collection — including oversized household items — is covered with **no extra fee** beyond the residential garbage fee. **Arrange pickup first** with your ward/district sanitation office or through **311** before you put the mattress in the alley or at the curb. If the mattress is clean and pest-free, you can also donate it at the **Chicago Furniture Bank** (drop-off on S Whipple St).

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step: city bulk pickup

1. Confirm you get **city (DSS) garbage service**. DSS serves single-family homes and apartment buildings of **four units or less**. Buildings with **five or more units** must use a private hauler — ask your landlord or property manager how bulky items are handled.
2. **Call 311** (or use 311.chicago.gov) **or contact your ward/district Streets and Sanitation office** and tell them you have a mattress (or other bulk item) for pickup. Find district office phones on the city's Division and Sanitation Offices page.
3. Wait for instructions. City guidance is to arrange the pick-up **before** putting any large item out; wards have ticketed residents who set bulk out without arranging it.
4. On the day/side they tell you, place the mattress where your trash is normally collected (many blocks use the **alley**; some use the curb). Do **not** block the alley, sidewalk, or street.
5. If the mattress has **bed bugs**, Chicago municipal code requires it to be **totally enclosed in a plastic bag and labeled as bed-bug infested** before it goes out for trash pickup. Bed-bug-infested bedding must not be recycled.

## What not to do / what the city won't take this way

- **Don't** put a mattress in the Blue Cart — mattresses are not a Blue Cart recyclable.
- **Don't** leave a mattress on the public way without arranging collection (illegal dumping / fly dumping risk, and a sanitation-code ticket risk).
- **Building/remodeling debris** is your responsibility to remove; city residential collection is not for contractor waste.
- Exact item lists and set-out quirks can vary by ward. Some ward offices publish different day-of-set-out advice than the citywide "arrange first" pages — **confirm with 311** for your address. *(Specific size/quantity limits for mattresses were not published on the city pages checked for this guide.)*

## Live in a 5+ unit building, suburb, or Cook County town?

City DSS bulk collection does not apply. Use your building's private scavenger contract, or check your suburb/township waste page. *(Suburb-by-suburb section: to be researched.)*

## Need it gone today / can't move it yourself?

City timing depends on your ward's workload. If you need same-day haul-away from inside the home, a paid junk removal service is the fallback. *(Paid options will be added once they're verified.)*

If the mattress is still usable, Chicago Furniture Bank drop-off or their scheduled pickup (suggested donation) is the donation path we could verify.

## Reporting illegal dumping

Report fly dumping (including dumped mattresses) through **311**. Illegal dumping endangers public health and can attract pests.

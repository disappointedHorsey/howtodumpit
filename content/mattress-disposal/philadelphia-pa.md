# How to get rid of a mattress in Philadelphia, PA

**Short answer:** Put it out on your **regular trash day**, but **only if it's fully sealed in a plastic mattress bag** (max two large items per week). If you'd rather not bag it, drive it to one of the City's **six sanitation convenience centers** (Mon-Sat, 8-6), where mattresses are accepted **unwrapped**.

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step: curbside

1. Get a plastic mattress bag and **fully seal** the mattress or box spring inside it.
2. Set it out with your regular trash on collection day. Limit: **two** compactible furniture items per week.

## Don't use the Bulk Collection appointment for mattresses

Philadelphia's appointment-based **Bulk Collection** program is for appliances, metal-frame furniture, TVs and tires. **Mattresses are not collected through it.** Wrapped mattresses go out with regular trash.

## What not to do

- **Don't** put out an unwrapped mattress. It won't be collected and can be fined.

## Need it gone today?

Self-haul to a convenience center or use a paid junk-removal service.

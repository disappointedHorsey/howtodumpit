# How to get rid of a mattress in Houston, TX

**Short answer:** If your home gets **City of Houston curbside service**, put the mattress out as **heavy trash** on your assigned date. Heavy trash runs **every other month**. Set it out **no earlier than 6:00 p.m. the Friday before** and **by 7:00 a.m.** on collection day.

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step

1. Look up your heavy trash date with the City's schedule lookup or the **HTX Collects** app.
2. Set the mattress out in the window: **6:00 p.m. the Friday before** through **7:00 a.m. collection day**.
3. Place it **just behind the curb line** in front of your home. Not in the street, not on the sidewalk.

## Apartment or outside city service?

Heavy trash collection is for eligible addresses with City curbside service. If you rent in a complex or live in a suburb or MUD with its own hauler, ask your property manager or hauler.

## Need it gone sooner?

Heavy trash comes every other month. To find drop-off options, use the HTX Collects Waste Wizard. For same-day haul-away, a paid junk-removal service is the fallback.

# How to get rid of a mattress in San Antonio, TX

**Short answer:** The fastest verified option is a **Bulky Waste Collection Center**. There are four, and a visit is **free if you pay the City's environmental fee**. Bring a **recent CPS Energy bill** and **photo ID**. Curbside bulky collection also runs **twice a year**. Look up your dates on the City's Brush & Bulky page.

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step: drop-off

1. Grab a recent CPS Energy bill showing the Environmental Fee, plus a photo ID.
2. Go during open hours: **Tue-Fri 8-5, Sat 8-noon**.
3. Keep the load within limits (bulky items up to 4 cubic yards, not piled above the truck's wheel fender).

## Curbside option

Curbside brush and bulky collection is provided twice per year. The City notes the curbside accepted-items list differs from the drop-off centers' list, so **check the curbside accepted-materials and collection-requirements pages** linked from the official Brush & Bulky page before setting a mattress out. Out-of-cycle pickup: call 3-1-1 to ask what's available.

## What the centers won't take

Mattresses with blood stains or bed bugs, bagged garbage, bed linens, brush and yard waste, hazardous materials.

## Reporting illegal dumping

Call 3-1-1 or 210-207-6000.

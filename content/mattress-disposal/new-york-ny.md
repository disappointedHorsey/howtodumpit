# How to get rid of a mattress in New York City, NY

**Short answer:** NYC's Department of Sanitation (DSNY) takes mattresses **free at the curb**, with **no appointment**, but the mattress or box spring **must be sealed in a plastic bag** first. Put it out between **6 PM and midnight the night before your trash-only collection day**.

<!--LISTINGS-->

## Step by step: free DSNY curbside pickup

1. Buy a mattress bag. The City doesn't provide them. NYC311 says they're sold at department stores, home improvement stores, and moving supply centers.
2. Seal the mattress or box spring **completely** in the plastic bag. NYC311 says any color except red or orange is fine.
3. Check your **trash-only** day (not your recycling day).
4. Set it at the curb between **6 PM and midnight the evening before** collection. Keep it out of the street and clear of pedestrian traffic and your neighbors' frontage.
5. Max **6 bulk items** per collection day.

## Bed frames

NYC311 treats bed frames as bulk items. If the frame is in pieces, tie the pieces into bundles no larger than 2 x 4 feet.

## What not to do

- **Don't** set out an unbagged mattress: it may be left behind and you can be fined.
- **Don't** put it out on your recycling day.
- **Don't** expect DSNY to schedule a pickup. Appointments are no longer offered for regular large items.

## Need it gone today / can't carry it downstairs?

DSNY only collects from the curb. If you need someone to carry it out of an apartment, a paid junk-removal service is the fallback (see the box above).

# Junk Disposal in Omaha, NE: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in the **City of Omaha**, Nebraska, item by item. Omaha does **not** pick up bulky items at the curb: they aren't allowed in (or beside) your black-lid waste cart. Instead, City residents can use the **bulky disposal program** at River City Transfer or the free **Spring and Fall Cleanup** Saturdays. Household chemicals go to **Under The Sink**. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call the City's Solid Waste Helpline at **402-444-5238**.

**City of Omaha only.** Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista, Ralston and other nearby cities run their own trash service, so their rules differ (see Outside City Limits below).

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** No curbside pickup. City of Omaha residents can drop a mattress at **River City Transfer** under the City's **bulky disposal program**, or bring it free to a **Spring or Fall Cleanup** site. ([City of Omaha Wasteline: Bulky Item Disposal](https://www.wasteline.org/special-waste-information/bulky-item-disposal/); [Waste Collection Information](https://www.wasteline.org/waste-collection-information/))

### Step by Step: City Bulky Disposal Program

1. **Check your address.** The program is only for homes inside Omaha city limits; the Wasteline page links an address lookup. Non-residents pay the full disposal cost.
2. **Bring two proofs of residency** (one from each category): vehicle registration, a current state-issued ID, an OPPD bill or a MUD bill. Names and street addresses must match.
3. **Tarp your load.** State and local law requires every load to be covered.
4. **Ask for the "city program"** when you arrive, before you dump. Refunds aren't given afterward.
5. **Vouchers:** a regular car load (under 2 cubic yards) is typically one voucher; a pickup, van or trailer (under 5 cubic yards) is typically two. Lost vouchers aren't replaced.
6. **Safety gear:** a safety vest and hard hat are required if you get out to unload. Bring your own or buy them on site.

**Phone numbers differ:** The City's Wasteline page lists River City Transfer at **402-889-8721**; the Keep Omaha Beautiful recycling guide lists **(402) 731-0414**. Try either before you drive. ([Omaha Recycling Guide: Mattresses](https://www.omaharecyclingguide.org/mattresses))

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask the Solid Waste Helpline before you haul one.

### Free Cleanup Saturdays

The City and participating neighborhood and homeowner associations host free drop-off sites, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. **Spring Cleanup** runs on Saturdays in April and May; **Fall Cleanup** ran on four Saturdays in October 2026. Any City of Omaha household can take part, but you must **unload your own vehicle**, and commercial waste is refused. Dates and site maps are posted on the City's Cleanup pages. ([Fall Cleanup 2026](https://www.wasteline.org/general-information/fall-cleanup/))

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Small furniture** that fits with the lid closed can go in your black-lid waste cart (cart weight limit 355 lb). **Couches, chairs, tables and other bulky furniture** go to River City Transfer under the City program or to a Cleanup site, the same as mattresses. ([Waste Collection Information](https://www.wasteline.org/waste-collection-information/); [Omaha Recycling Guide: Furniture](https://www.omaharecyclingguide.org/furniture)) Furniture in good condition is better donated; call the charity first.

**Apartments:** City trash and recycling service covers single-family households and apartment buildings whose owner has registered for City collection. If your building has its own private hauler, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled. The River City program and Cleanup Saturdays are still open to you as a City of Omaha resident. ([Wasteline FAQ: Multi-Family Recycling](https://www.wasteline.org/ufaqs/do-you-offer-recycling-for-multi-family-units-like-apartments/))

**Neighborhoods:** We found no difference in bulky rules between Omaha neighborhoods. What matters is whether your address is **inside city limits**.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Omaha** only. **Bellevue** contracts Papillion Sanitation for single-family and duplex homes; call Papillion Sanitation at **402-346-7800** or Bellevue Public Works at **402-293-3135** about large items. ([City of Bellevue: Trash & Recycling](https://www.bellevue.net/384/Trash-Recycling)) Other towns in **Douglas and Sarpy counties** set their own rules, so check your city's website. If you live outside Omaha you can still use River City Transfer, but at the **full disposal price**, and Omaha's Cleanup Saturdays are for City households only. **Under The Sink** is open to all **Douglas and Sarpy County** households. ([Sarpy County: Under The Sink](https://www.sarpy.gov/927/Under-The-Sink)) If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Not in the River City program:** The City says freon and non-freon appliances are no longer part of the bulky disposal program. Its listed options are the free **Cleanup Saturdays**, where appliances go to **designated appliance sites only**, and an online recycler search (see Other options at the bottom of this page). ([Bulky Item Disposal](https://www.wasteline.org/special-waste-information/bulky-item-disposal/))

**Other options:** The Keep Omaha Beautiful recycling guide says River City may take large appliances for a processing fee, notes that the Pheasant Point landfill does **not** accept appliances, and suggests scrap metal recyclers. Call ahead to confirm fees. ([Omaha Recycling Guide: Appliances](https://www.omaharecyclingguide.org/appliances))

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**No curbside option.** Televisions (tube and flat screen) are accepted at the free **Cleanup Saturdays**. At other times, the Keep Omaha Beautiful recycling guide points to local electronics recyclers, which charge processing fees for most TVs. ([Spring Cleanup 2026](https://www.wasteline.org/general-information/spring-cleanup/); [Omaha Recycling Guide: TVs](https://www.omaharecyclingguide.org/tvs))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you recycle them. **Rechargeable and lithium batteries** go to Under The Sink, not a Cleanup site.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never** put paint or chemicals in your cart or bring them to River City Transfer or a Cleanup site. Take them to **Under The Sink**, 4001 South 120th Street, Omaha, NE 68137, the household chemical facility for **Douglas and Sarpy County** households. ([Under The Sink: Location & Hours](https://www.underthesink.org/drop-off-info/location-hours/))

- **Drop-off hours:** Wednesday and Friday 9 a.m. to 4:45 p.m., Thursday 9 a.m. to 6:15 p.m.; **Saturday by appointment only** (402-444-SINK / 402-444-7465). Closed Monday and Tuesday. Holiday closures include **Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2026**, and **Thanksgiving week, November 26 to 28, 2026**.
- **Fees:** Free, except **latex paint and water-based coatings: $1 per container**, quart size and larger (credit card only). Latex paint isn't hazardous; once it's dried out solid, it can go in the trash. ([Under The Sink: What's Accepted](https://www.underthesink.org/drop-off-info/whats-accepted/))
- **Accepted:** oil-based and latex paint, paint thinner and stripper, automotive fluids and batteries, gasoline, pesticides, pool chemicals, fluorescent tubes and bulbs, lithium batteries, propane bottles up to one pound, and more.
- **Household waste only:** nothing from a business, even a home-based one. Keep chemicals in their original, tightly capped containers, packed upright in a box, and drive straight there.

Questions? City of Omaha Environmental Services Solid Waste Helpline: **402-444-5238**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Omaha Wasteline page says Christmas trees may be cut down to fit in the black-lid waste cart at any time. Seasonal drop-off sites run after the holidays; they are closed for the season as of this check, and 2026-27 site dates were not posted.

**Last season (2025-26):** You could begin using the sites on December 24. Sites were open daily during daylight hours and closed Friday, January 9, 2026. Leaving a tree when the sites are closed is illegal dumping, with a fine of up to $500, possible loss of a driver's license for 6 months, and cleanup costs (Omaha Municipal Code, Chapter 34, Article I, Section 4). Remove nails, wires, ornaments, the tree stand, plastic bags and lights. Flocked trees are accepted. Wreaths and evergreen roping are not. Throw the tree up on the pile so others can get in. ([City of Omaha Wasteline: Christmas Tree Disposal](https://www.wasteline.org/special-waste-information/christmas-tree-disposal/))

Last-season Omaha-area sites included a new location at Kelley Softball Complex, 12302 Fort Street (at the end of Dido Albert Federowich Memorial Drive); Omaha's Henry Doorly Zoo and Aquarium northwest parking lot near Bob Gibson Drive and South 13th Street; Orchard Park, 66th Street north of Hartman Avenue; F Street Football Field, F Street east of 156th Street; Ta Ha Zouka Park, 20801 Elkhorn Drive; Kelly West Park, 87th Street and Park View Boulevard; and Wehrspann Lake Wildlife Management Area south parking lot on Highway 370. Nearby towns also listed sites, including Bellevue Old Sarpy County Landfill, 8902 Cedar Island Road. Volunteer help at Kelley Softball Complex and F Street Football Field was limited to listed weekend hours.

Do not deliver a tree to a closed site. Questions: Solid Waste Helpline, 402-444-5238. If you cannot cut the tree to fit the cart, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

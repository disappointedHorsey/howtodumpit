# Christmas Tree Pickup and Drop-Off in Oklahoma City, OK (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** If you have City of Oklahoma City trash service, set a natural or artificial tree at the curb with your bulky waste by 6 a.m. on your monthly bulky waste date. OKC Parks also mulches live trees for free, but its 2026-27 drop-off dates were not posted as of November 16, 2026. If your bulky day is weeks away, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 16, 2026, against official City of Oklahoma City pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Bulky Waste Collection {#bulky-waste}

- **Who:** residential Oklahoma City Solid Waste customers with curbside trash and bulky waste service. Natural and artificial trees both qualify. ([City of OKC: Residents Can Mulch or Dispose of Live Christmas Trees](https://www.okc.gov/News-articles/OKC-residents-can-mulch-or-dispose-of-live-Christmas-trees))
- **When:** by 6 a.m. on your assigned monthly bulky waste date. Find it at okcutilities.com or on the City's map.
- **Placement:** keep the tree away from mailboxes, trees, shrubs and vehicles, and don't cover a water or natural gas meter. Bagged trash set beside bulky waste is not accepted.
- **Holidays:** bulky waste pickup is not affected by the holiday schedule. Regular Friday trash and recycling for Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, 2026, moves to Saturday, December 26. ([City of OKC: My Trash Day](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Trash-Services/My-Trash-Day))
- **The catch:** bulky pickup comes once a month, so the tree may sit in your house or yard until your date.

## Drop It Off Yourself: OKC Parks Tree Mulching {#mulch}

**2026-27 dates: not published.** The latest City article we found, dated December 16, 2025, describes last season. ([City of OKC: Residents Can Mulch or Dispose of Live Christmas Trees](https://www.okc.gov/News-articles/OKC-residents-can-mulch-or-dispose-of-live-Christmas-trees))

Last season, residents could drop off natural trees from December 26, 2025, through January 15, 2026, at two parks:

- Will Rogers Park, 3600 Pat Murphy Drive: the parking lot at the center of the park, south of the OKC Tennis Center.
- Wheeler Park, 1120 S. Western Avenue: the parking lot on the west side of the park, next to Western Avenue.

Trees had to be free of lights, ornaments, nails and stands, and unflocked (no fake snow). Free mulch was available from dawn to dusk through January 15; bring your own shovel and containers. The program was open to all Oklahoma City residents.

**The catch:** you need a vehicle that can carry the tree, and the dates for this season aren't out yet.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When is Christmas tree pickup in Oklahoma City?

The City picks up trees with bulky waste on your own monthly bulky waste date. There is no separate tree week, and the holiday schedule doesn't move bulky pickup.

### Can I put an artificial tree out with bulky waste in OKC?

Yes. The City says natural and artificial trees can both go out with bulky waste.

### Where can I mulch my Christmas tree in OKC this year?

OKC Parks had not posted 2026-27 sites or dates when we checked on November 16, 2026. Last season used Will Rogers Park and Wheeler Park.

### Does OKC Parks take flocked trees?

No. Last season's mulching rules said trees must be unflocked, with no fake snow on the branches.

### Who can pick up my Christmas tree before my bulky day?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

# Junk Disposal in Kansas City, MO: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Kansas City, Missouri, item by item. If you live in a **single-family home or an apartment building with 6 units or fewer**, the City picks up furniture, mattresses, appliances and TVs **for free by appointment**. **Paint and household chemicals** go free to the KC Water household hazardous waste facility. **Leaves and brush** have their own curbside rounds and drop-off sites. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call 311 (**816-513-1313**) before you set anything out.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Beds and mattresses are on the City's accepted list for **free bulky item pickup**. Book with the Bulky Pickup Scheduler or by contacting 311. ([City of Kansas City: Bulky Item Pickup](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/bulky))

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Bulky item pickup:** The City collects bulky items **free, by appointment**, for single-family homes and apartments with 6 units or fewer. Appointments are offered Monday through Friday. Accepted items include couches, tables, chairs, TVs, beds, mattresses, metal furniture, cabinets, bathroom fixtures, rugs and carpet, plus oversized items such as garage doors, lawn mowers, disassembled swing sets and metal fencing. ([City of Kansas City: Bulky Item Pickup](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/bulky))

1. **Book first.** Use the Official Bulky Scheduler or contact 311 (816-513-1313). Nothing is collected without an appointment. List every item you plan to set out; items not listed won't be collected.
2. **Watch the limits.** Up to 15 items per appointment, and no item can weigh more than 500 pounds.
3. **Set it out on time.** Put items within 3 feet of the curb by 7 a.m. on your collection day, and **not before 3 p.m. the day before**.
4. **Place it right.** Keep items at least 4 feet from your trash and recycling carts, and clear of trees, mailboxes, lamp posts, parked cars and low wires.
5. **Prepare it.** Furniture should be dry, clean and easy to reach; don't remove drawers. Carpet must be rolled up and over 4 feet long or 40 pounds to qualify. Drain fuel and oil from lawn mowers. Mark glass panes with a large masking-tape "X".
6. **Cancel or change.** Contact 311, call 816-513-BULK (2855), or use the myKCMO app.

**Not accepted:** business waste, piles of small items, bags or boxes, loose materials (including dumpster bags), closed drums or fuel tanks, loose building materials and lumber, yard waste and tires. Items mixed with trash, paint, auto parts, masonry or household hazardous waste get a violation notice and are left behind.

**Missed pickup:** If your items were set out correctly, wait until 5 p.m. on the day of service, then report it through myKCMO or 311 within one business day.

**Donate first:** Habitat for Humanity KC ReStore takes new and gently used furniture, working appliances and building supplies, and will pick up bulky donations for free. Call (816) 231-6889 or use its online scheduler. Habitat's own pages list different drop-off hours (Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on its [ReStore page](https://habitatkc.org/restore-kansas-city/); Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., with drop-offs ending an hour before closing, on its [Donating to ReStore page](https://habitatkc.org/donating-to-restore/)), so call before you drive.

### Outside City Limits?

**Larger apartment buildings:** City bulky pickup covers single-family homes and apartments with 6 units or fewer. If you live in a larger building, ask your landlord or property manager how bulky items are handled, or book paid junk removal.

**Kansas side and neighboring cities:** Kansas City, Kansas, Overland Park, Independence and other metro cities run their own trash services, so check your own city's page. KC Water's hazardous waste facility can't take anything from Kansas residents; it points Wyandotte County residents to 913-573-5311, Johnson County residents to 913-715-6907 and Olathe residents to 913-971-9311. ([KC Water: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.kcwater.us/programs/hhw/)) If you live in an HOA, check with it first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Bulky item pickup:** Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, stoves, dishwashers, washers, dryers, water heaters and furnaces are on the City's bulky list. Empty fridges and freezers of all food, and remove shelves, drawers or doors if possible. **A separate truck collects appliances with refrigerant**, so say so when you book or it won't be collected. If it cools air or food, or its label says R-22, R-134a, R-410A or Freon, it has refrigerant. ([City of Kansas City: Bulky Item Pickup](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/bulky))

**Freon:** Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

**Other electronics:** The City says to recycle electronics at a Midwest Recycling Center, and that while most electronics are free to recycle, some items may require a fee. We couldn't confirm which items carry a fee or how much, so ask before you go. ([City of Kansas City: Recycling](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/recycling))

**Hard to Recycle Item Drop-Off Event:** The City lists one on November 7, 2026, from 8 a.m. to noon. Its page doesn't say where or what's accepted, so check with 311.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**KC Water HHW facility:** Free for residents of Kansas City, Mo., and participating communities, with proof of residency such as a driver's license or utility bill. It takes paint, automotive fluids, batteries, fluorescent and other light bulbs, cooking oil, household cleaners, lawn and garden products, pesticides, pool chemicals, mercury and used oil filters. Stay in your vehicle; staff unload at the drive-through. ([KC Water: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.kcwater.us/programs/hhw/))

| Site | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| KC Water HHW Facility (Environmental Campus) | 4707 Deramus Ave, Kansas City, MO 64120 | KC Water lists Thursday and Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., for loads under 600 pounds. Jackson County's page lists Thursday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Call 816-513-8400 to confirm. |

- **Loads over 600 pounds:** by appointment only (816-513-8400). KC Water's page says these are accepted only on Wednesdays.
- **Not accepted:** appliances, asbestos, containers larger than 5 gallons, explosives, ammunition and fireworks, smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, medical waste and needles, prescription drugs, propane bottles over 1 pound, butane, Freon, tires and yard waste. No waste from businesses, nonprofits, churches, schools or rental property upkeep.
- **Closures:** KC Water lists Thanksgiving, November 26 to 28, and an end-of-year shutdown from December 21 to January 5.
- **Swap Shop:** reusable paint, cleaners and lawn products collected at the facility are free to the public; call ahead to check stock.
- **Mobile event:** November 7, 2026, 8 a.m. to noon, at Manuel Career Tech Center, 1215 E. Truman Rd., Kansas City, MO 64106.

([Jackson County: Kansas City Facility](https://www.jacksongov.org/Residents/Home-and-Family/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Disposal/Kansas-City-Facility))

## Leaves and Brush {#yard-waste}

**Curbside rounds:** Yard waste isn't taken in bulky pickup. The City collects leaves, sticks, branches and brush curbside in spring and fall. Fall 2026 Round 2 runs November 16 to 20 (North), November 30 to December 4 (South) and December 14 to 18 (Central). Use paper lawn sacks only (no plastic), up to 20 sacks and bundles per household, each under 40 pounds. Bundle branches no thicker than 3 inches into bundles no more than 2 feet across and 4 feet long, tied with twine. ([City of Kansas City: Leaf & Brush](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/leaf-brush))

**Drop-off sites:** 11660 N. Main Street and 10301 Raytown Road (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.), and 7700 E US-40 Highway (Saturdays only, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Drop-off is **free for Kansas City, Missouri residents on Saturdays** with a valid KCMO ID (or another ID plus a utility bill or tax receipt with a KCMO address). Weekday drop-offs and non-residents pay a fee; the City's page refers to a price list we couldn't verify.

Questions? Call **311** at **816-513-1313**, bulky cancellations at **816-513-2855**, the KC Water HHW facility at **816-513-8400**, or Habitat KC ReStore at **(816) 231-6889**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Kansas City, Missouri, Leaf & Brush page does not specifically mention Christmas trees. We are not going to assume a whole holiday tree is accepted. Call 311 (816-513-1313) or check with the drop-off site before you haul one.

The City maintains three leaf and brush drop-off sites that accept leaves, yard waste, grass clippings, brush (including tree trimmings) and logs: 11660 N. Main Street and 10301 Raytown Road, Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m., and 7700 E. US-40 Highway, Saturdays only, 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Drop-off is free for Kansas City, Missouri, residents on Saturdays with a valid local ID (or a matching utility bill or tax receipt). Weekday drop-off is not free; fees and a gas surcharge are described on the City page. ([City of Kansas City: Leaf & Brush](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/trash/leaf-brush))

RecycleSpot, the Mid-America Regional Council recycling directory, says natural holiday trees and greenery can be recycled at metro drop-off locations from December 26 through January every year, and year-round at yard-waste drop-off sites. That is regional guidance, not a City of Kansas City schedule. ([RecycleSpot: Holiday Trees](https://www.recyclespot.org/how-to-recycle/home-and-holiday-decor/holiday-trees))

Curbside leaf and brush rounds are seasonal and use paper lawn sacks and bundled branches; the City's accepted list for those rounds is leaves, sticks, branches and brush, not a named Christmas tree program.

If you need a tree taken from the house, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

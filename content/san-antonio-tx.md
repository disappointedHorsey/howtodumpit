# Junk Disposal in San Antonio, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in San Antonio, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** The fastest verified option is a **Bulky Waste Collection Center**. There are four, and a visit is **free if you pay the City's environmental fee**. Bring a **recent CPS Energy bill** and **photo ID**. Curbside bulky collection also runs **twice a year**. Look up your dates on the City's Brush & Bulky page.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Drop-Off

1. Grab a recent CPS Energy bill showing the Environmental Fee, plus a photo ID.
2. Go during open hours: **Tue-Fri 8-5, Sat 8-noon**.
3. Keep the load within limits (bulky items up to 4 cubic yards, not piled above the truck's wheel fender).

### Curbside Option

Curbside brush and bulky collection is provided twice per year. The City notes the curbside accepted-items list differs from the drop-off centers' list, so **check the curbside accepted-materials and collection-requirements pages** linked from the official Brush & Bulky page before setting a mattress out. Out-of-cycle pickup: call 3-1-1 to ask what's available.

### What the Centers Won't Take

Mattresses with blood stains or bed bugs, bagged garbage, bed linens, brush and yard waste, hazardous materials.

### Reporting Illegal Dumping

Call 3-1-1 or 210-207-6000.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

The four **Bulky Waste Collection Centers** accept **furniture** (not couch cushions soiled with blood or bed bugs). Bring a recent CPS Energy bill showing the Environmental Fee and a photo ID; bulky loads are limited to 4 cubic yards. **Curbside brush & bulky collection** also runs twice a year. Check the City's accepted-materials list for curbside. ([Bulky Waste Collection Centers](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Bulky-Drop-Off); [Brush & Bulky](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

The Bulky Waste Collection Centers list **large appliances** and **water heaters & AC units** among accepted materials (same ID/bill rules and hours: Tue–Fri 8–5, Sat 8–noon). Whether curbside bulky collection takes freon appliances: check the City's curbside accepted-materials list. ([Bulky Waste Collection Centers](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Bulky-Drop-Off))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

The City's **Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off Center, 7030 Culebra Road** (Tue–Fri 8–5, Sat 8–noon, except holidays) accepts **e-waste**. Bring a recent CPS Energy bill showing the Environmental Fee and a photo ID. ([San Antonio HHW](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/HHW))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

HHW must **never** go in any curbside cart. Take **paint**, auto fluids, batteries, cleaners and solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals and more to the **HHW Drop-Off Center, 7030 Culebra Road** (same hours). Limits: paint and liquids up to **five 5-gallon or 25 one-gallon cans**; HHW up to **220 lbs**. Keep items in original containers and don't mix them. ([San Antonio HHW](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/HHW))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

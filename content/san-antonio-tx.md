# Junk Disposal in San Antonio, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in San Antonio, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Bulky Waste Collection Centers (free with environmental fee) |
| Couch / furniture | Bulky Waste Collection Centers; curbside bulky twice a year |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** The fastest verified option is a **Bulky Waste Collection Center**. There are four, and a visit is **free if you pay the City's environmental fee**. Bring a **recent CPS Energy bill** and **photo ID**. Curbside bulky collection also runs **twice a year**. Look up your dates on the City's Brush & Bulky page.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Drop-Off

1. Grab a recent CPS Energy bill showing the Environmental Fee, plus a photo ID.
2. Go during open hours: **Tue-Fri 8-5, Sat 8-noon**.
3. Keep the load within limits (bulky items up to 4 cubic yards, not piled above the truck's wheel fender).

### Curbside Option

Curbside brush and bulky collection is provided twice per year. The City notes the curbside accepted-items list differs from the drop-off centers' list, so **check the curbside accepted-materials and collection-requirements pages** linked from the official Brush & Bulky page before setting a mattress out. Out-of-cycle pickup: call 3-1-1 to ask what's available.

### What the Centers Won't Take

Mattresses with blood stains or bed bugs, bagged garbage, bed linens, brush and yard waste, hazardous materials.

### Reporting Illegal Dumping

Call 3-1-1 or 210-207-6000.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

The four **Bulky Waste Collection Centers** accept **furniture** (not couch cushions soiled with blood or bed bugs). Bring the same CPS Energy bill and photo ID; bulky loads are limited to 4 cubic yards. The twice-yearly **curbside brush & bulky collection** is the other option. Check the City's accepted-materials list for curbside. ([Bulky Waste Collection Centers](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Bulky-Drop-Off); [Brush & Bulky](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky))

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers **San Antonio city limits**, where the City's Solid Waste Management Department serves single-family homes (generally those on a public street). **Apartments and other multi-family properties** don't get City curbside service and use a **private hauler**, so ask your property manager about bulky items; private-street subdivisions and HOAs may also use a private hauler. ([San Antonio Information Bulletin 576: Solid Waste Collection Services (PDF)](https://docsonline.sanantonio.gov/FileUploads/DSD/IB576-SWMD.pdf)) **Unincorporated Bexar County** has no county-wide trash or bulky pickup. In five County program subdivisions (Candlewood, Camelot, Glen North, Glen South and Crownwood), the County's contract hauler offers bulk pickup up to **4 cubic yards once per quarter**; call Bexar County Solid Waste at **210-335-6644** with questions. Everywhere else in the unincorporated county, arrange bulky pickup with your own hauler. ([Bexar County: Solid Waste](https://www.bexar.org/3374/Solid-Waste)) Separate cities surrounded by San Antonio run their own service. For example, **Alamo Heights** doesn't pick up furniture, mattresses, refrigerators, AC units, stoves or water heaters ([City of Alamo Heights: Solid Waste](https://www.alamoheightstx.gov/departments/public-works/solid-waste/)). Check your own city's official page before you set anything out.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

The Bulky Waste Collection Centers list **large appliances** and **water heaters & AC units** among accepted materials (same ID/bill rules and hours: Tue–Fri 8–5, Sat 8–noon). Whether curbside bulky collection takes freon appliances: check the City's curbside accepted-materials list. ([Bulky Waste Collection Centers](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Bulky-Drop-Off))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

The City's **Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off Center, 7030 Culebra Road** (Tue–Fri 8–5, Sat 8–noon, except holidays) accepts **e-waste**. Bring the same CPS Energy bill and photo ID as for the bulky centers. ([San Antonio HHW](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/HHW))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

HHW must **never** go in any curbside cart. Take **paint**, auto fluids, batteries, cleaners and solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals and more to the **HHW Drop-Off Center, 7030 Culebra Road** (same hours). Limits: paint and liquids up to **five 5-gallon or 25 one-gallon cans**; HHW up to **220 lbs**. Keep items in original containers and don't mix them. ([San Antonio HHW](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/HHW))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** San Antonio Solid Waste Management Department offers live-tree drop-off during the first two weeks of January and mulches the trees for customers. 2026-27 dates were not posted as of November 10, 2026.

**Last season (2025-26):** Drop-off ran January 3-14, 2026, at four centers: Culebra Road Drop-Off Center, 7030 Culebra Road; Frio City Road Drop-Off Center, 1531 Frio City Road; Rigsby Road Drop-Off Center, 2755 Rigsby Road (those three Tuesday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m.-noon); and Bitters Brush Recycling Center, 1800 Wurzbach Parkway (Monday-Saturday 8 a.m.-5 p.m.). Live trees only. If the tree is over 6 feet, cut it in half. Remove all lights, decorations and stands, even wooden ones. Do not bag trees. Live wreaths, garlands and plants are not accepted. Under the City's tarp law, brush loads must be covered or a $5 penalty may be assessed. ([City of San Antonio: Christmas Tree Drop-Off](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Brush-Bulky/Christmas-Tree))

Questions: 311 or 210-207-6000.

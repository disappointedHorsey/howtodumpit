# Christmas Tree Pickup in Cambridge and Somerville, MA (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Somerville picks up bare, unbagged trees curbside on your regular trash day through January. Cambridge took trees curbside on regular collection days from January 2 to 14 last season, then at its Recycle Center through January 31, and had not posted 2026-27 dates when we checked on November 16, 2026. For pickup on your schedule, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 16, 2026, against official City of Somerville and City of Cambridge pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Somerville Christmas Tree Curbside Pickup {#somerville}

- **Rule (standing):** place the tree curbside on your regular trash day after removing all decorations. Don't put it in a plastic bag. ([City of Somerville: Trash and Recycling](https://www.somervillema.gov/trash-and-recycling))
- **When:** Christmas trees are only collected through January. Trees picked up through January are mulched.
- **Free mulch:** pick it up through February at the DPW yard.
- **The catch:** after January, an uncollected tree is your responsibility, not the City's.

## Free: Cambridge DPW Christmas Tree Collection {#cambridge}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 16, 2026).
- **Last season (2025-26):** Cambridge DPW picked up natural trees with no decorations or bags on regular curbside collection days between January 2 and 14, 2026. ([City of Cambridge: Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day Holiday Closures and Service Information](https://www.cambridgema.gov/news/2025/12/christmasevechristmasdayandnewyearsdayholidayclosuresandserviceinfo))
- **Questions:** Cambridge DPW, 617-349-4800. ([City of Cambridge: Curbside Collections](https://www.cambridgema.gov/services/curbsidecollections))
- **The catch:** the curbside window was only two weeks last season.

## Drop It Off Yourself: Cambridge Recycle Center {#drop-off}

- **Last season (2025-26):** after January 14, Cambridge residents could bring trees to the Recycle Center during open hours through January 31, 2026. ([City of Cambridge holiday service notice](https://www.cambridgema.gov/news/2025/12/christmasevechristmasdayandnewyearsdayholidayclosuresandserviceinfo))
- **Where and when:** 147 Hampshire St., in the back right corner of the Public Works yard. Open Tuesdays and Thursdays 4-7:30 p.m. and Saturdays 9 a.m.-4 p.m. Open to all Cambridge residents. ([City of Cambridge: Recycling Center](https://www.cambridgema.gov/services/recyclingcenter))
- **Somerville:** we found no Somerville tree drop-off site on official City pages as of November 16, 2026.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

- **Somerville:** Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, 2026, and New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027, are on the City's list of holidays when trash pickup is delayed by one day. ([City of Somerville: Trash and Recycling Holiday Schedule](https://www.somervillema.gov/departments/dpw-sanitation/trash-holiday-schedule))
- **Cambridge:** the City's standing rule is that weekly collections run one day behind schedule following a holiday. Cambridge had not posted its 2026 Christmas notice as of November 16, 2026. ([City of Cambridge: Curbside Collections](https://www.cambridgema.gov/services/curbsidecollections))

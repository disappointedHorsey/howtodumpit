# Junk Disposal in Colorado Springs, CO: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Colorado Springs, Colorado, item by item. **The City does not provide municipal trash or bulky-item collection.** Residents and businesses hire **private haulers** in an open market, and most processing facilities (except the County HHW site) are privately owned. ([City of Colorado Springs: Waste Diversion Action Plan, December 2025](https://coloradosprings.gov/system/files/2026-02/FINAL_Waste%20Diversion%20Action%20Plan_English.pdf); [WastelessCOS](https://coloradosprings.gov/WastelessCOS)) **El Paso County** runs the regional **Household Hazardous Waste Facility** and publishes a **Recycling Directory** of drop-off sites. Every option links to the official page or County directory we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Call your hauler, El Paso County Environmental Division at **719-520-7878**, or the facility before you set anything out or drive.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** There is **no City of Colorado Springs curbside bulky program**. Ask **your private trash hauler** whether it offers call-ahead bulky pickup for mattresses and box springs and what it charges. Otherwise **self-haul** to a transfer station or landfill listed in El Paso County’s Recycling Directory (fees apply; call first). ([Waste Diversion Action Plan](https://coloradosprings.gov/system/files/2026-02/FINAL_Waste%20Diversion%20Action%20Plan_English.pdf); [El Paso County Recycling Directory 2027 (PDF)](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))

### Step by Step: Private Hauler or Self-Haul

1. **Call your hauler.** Confirm whether mattresses need bagging, how many items you can set out, the fee, and the pickup day. Do not leave mattresses at the curb unless your hauler has scheduled them.
2. **Or self-haul.** Directory-listed options that take mattresses/box springs (call for fees and rules) include the **El Paso County Transfer Station** at **2520 Laramie Dr., Colorado Springs** (**719-480-8000**; the County notes it is **not** affiliated with El Paso County Environmental Division), **Urban Recycling** (3341 N. Cascade Ave., **719-644-6493**), **Woodmen Dump** (8808 Cliff Allen Pt., **719-792-3867**), **Peak Disposal & Recycling** in Monument (**719-481-2340**), and **Galvanize Recycling** (**719-645-4165**). ([Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))
3. **Cover your load.** Colorado rules can charge more for uncovered or unsecured loads at landfills — tarp the truck before you go.
4. **Donation (gently used only):** Some nonprofits listed in the County directory accept queen-or-smaller mattresses that are free of rips, stains and holes (for example One Nation Walking Together and Partners in Housing). Call before you load the truck. ([Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))

**Not at County HHW:** Mattresses and box springs are on the Household Hazardous Waste Facility’s **not accepted** list. ([El Paso County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/))

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City wrapping rule for bed-bug mattresses. Ask your hauler or the drop-off facility before you transport a soiled or infested mattress.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**No City bulk pickup.** Couches, chairs and other household furniture follow the same path as mattresses: schedule through **your private hauler** or self-haul to a directory-listed facility. Urban Recycling’s County directory entry notes fees for couches, chairs and ottomans as well as mattresses. ([Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf); [Waste Diversion Action Plan](https://coloradosprings.gov/system/files/2026-02/FINAL_Waste%20Diversion%20Action%20Plan_English.pdf))

**City code (storage, not pickup):** Appliances, furniture, manufactured goods and personal items must be stored **inside an enclosed building** or **out of view in a rear yard** — leaving them stacked in public view can draw code enforcement. That is a storage rule, not a disposal service. ([City Code: What You Need to Know](https://coloradosprings.gov/city-code-what-you-need-know))

**Usable furniture:** Consider donating to a thrift or ReStore if the item is in good, sellable condition (see partners and the County donations list).

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers **Colorado Springs city limits**, where trash and bulky service run through **private haulers** (open market). **Unincorporated El Paso County** and most neighboring towns likewise rely on private collection and the same regional landfills, transfer stations and the County HHW facility — use the [El Paso County Environmental Division](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/) and [Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf) for drop-off options. **Manitou Springs** contracts a third-party hauler (**Republic Services**, **719-633-8709**) rather than open-market rates; contact Republic for missed pickups and bulky questions ([Manitou Springs: Garbage & Refuse](https://www.manitouspringsco.gov/437/Garbage-Refuse)). Other El Paso County cities (for example Fountain) run their own solid-waste programs — check your city’s official page before you set items out. If you live in an apartment, HOA or complex with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Not at County HHW:** Air conditioners, refrigerators, freezers and other appliances are **not accepted** at the El Paso County Household Hazardous Waste Facility. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/))

**Freon / refrigerants:** The County Recycling Directory lists businesses that handle appliances needing **Freon recovery**, including **Colorado Springs Landfill** (fridges and freezers; **1010 Blaney Rd.**, **719-683-2600**), **Urban Recycling**, **Waste Management Colorado Springs Transfer Station** (**1965 Commercial Blvd.**, **719-216-0966**), **WM Midway Landfill** (**8925 Rancho Colorado Blvd., Fountain**, **719-382-8383**), **Peak Disposal**, **Woodmen Dump**, and Habitat ReStores for **working** appliances only. **Call ahead** for fees, door rules and whether refrigerant must be intact. ([Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))

Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer is responsible for recovering refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

**Non-freon appliances** (washers, dryers, stoves, dishwashers): ask your hauler about bulky pickup, or use a directory-listed scrap/metal or transfer facility. Confirm fees before you go.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

In Colorado, **computers and other electronic equipment are prohibited from disposal in a landfill**. Local governments are not required to fund a public electronics program; check County and private recycler options below. ([CDPHE: Information on Residential Electronics and Computer Waste](https://cdphe.colorado.gov/hm/information-residential-electronics-and-computer-waste))

**El Paso County HHW (size limits):** El Paso/Teller County households may drop off many electronics at **3255 Akers Dr.** Each electronics type is limited to **5 per household per year**. **CRT TVs** must be **20" or smaller**; **flat screens** **40" or smaller**. Larger TVs go through **Clean Sweep** events when scheduled (2026 TV & tire events have **concluded**; ask to join the Spring **2027** email list). ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/); [Clean Sweep Hard-to-Recycle Events](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/clean-sweep-hard-to-recycle-events/); [Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))

**Private e-recyclers (directory examples):** E-Tech Recyclers (2854 N. Prospect St., **719-799-6517**), Galvanize Recycling, ITAD Electronic Recyclers, Urban Recycling. Call for fees and accepted items. ([Recycling Directory 2027](https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Recycling-Directory-2027.pdf))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you recycle them.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**El Paso County Household Hazardous Waste Facility** at **3255 Akers Dr., Colorado Springs, 80922** accepts household hazardous waste from **El Paso and Teller County households only** (proof of residency required). Call **719-520-7878**, text **719-331-3226**, or email **evs-epc@elpasoco.com**. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/); [Environmental Division](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/))

**Fall/Winter 2026–2027 hours (confirm before you go):** Monday–Friday **8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m.** and **1:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m.**; closed County holidays. 2026 Saturday hours: **November 7**, **8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m.** (no large loads of 50+ items). Appointments are required for **50+ items** or business waste (**719-520-7879**).

**Paint:** Latex, oil-based, hobby paint, stains, varnishes and related products are accepted (limits apply: containers **no larger than 5 gallons**; paint limited to **50 gallons per household per year** by container size). For paint totaling more than 50 gallons, the County points residents to **PaintCare** at **855-724-6809** or PaintCare.org. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/))

**Also accepted (examples; limits apply):** household chemicals, aerosols (caps/nozzles removed), batteries (household; clear bag rules), fluorescent tubes, propane tanks (1 lb and 20 lb), automotive fluids (quantity limits), and many electronics within the size limits above.

**Not accepted (examples):** appliances, furniture, mattresses, trash, tires (see Clean Sweep), microwaves, fire extinguishers, asbestos, business waste (see VSQG), and containers larger than 5 gallons. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://communityresources.elpasoco.com/environmental-division/household-hazardous-waste/))

Stay in the process the County describes: staff will not enter your vehicle; you must unload items onto a cart yourself.

Questions? El Paso County Environmental Division **719-520-7878** / **evs-epc@elpasoco.com**. City code and Keep It Clean COS issues: see [Keep It Clean COS](https://coloradosprings.gov/KeepItClean) or code enforcement contacts on the City site.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** Colorado Springs does not run a municipal trash utility, so holiday tree recycling is organized as a community TreeCycle drop-off, not a City curbside route. 2026-27 dates were not published as of November 10, 2026.

**Last season (2025-26):** TreeCycle listed six El Paso County locations on January 3-4 and January 10-11, 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.: Baptist Road Trailhead (Baptist Road and Old Denver Highway); Falcon Trailhead (south of Woodmen Road and McLaughlin Road); Cottonwood Creek Park (Dublin Boulevard and Monarbor Drive); Martin "Ed" Ragain Field (Barnes Road and Tutt Boulevard); Rock Ledge Ranch (Gateway Road and 30th Street); and Memorial Park (Pikes Peak Avenue and Union Boulevard). Rocky Top Resources, 1755 E. Las Vegas Street, accepted trees December 26 through January 31, 7:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. weekdays and 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. Saturdays (closed Sundays and holidays). Trees only: no stands, stand spikes, garland, plastic, twine or lights. Personal drop-off only, no commercial drop-offs. A $5 suggested minimum donation was listed to benefit community youth development nonprofits. Mulch could be picked up at Colorado Springs Utilities Leon Young Service Center, 1521 S. Hancock Expressway, and City of Colorado Springs Forestry, 1601 Recreation Way. ([TreeCycle](https://costreecycle.org/))

Check costreecycle.org or City Forestry closer to the holidays for 2026-27 dates. If you cannot get to a drop-off site, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup.

# Junk Disposal in Tucson, AZ: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Tucson, Arizona, item by item. The City of Tucson Environmental Services Department picks up furniture, appliances and other bulky items **twice a year** through its **Brush and Bulky** collection, and you can order a **Special Brush and Bulky** pickup any time for a fee. You can also drive a load to the City's **Los Reales Landfill**. **TVs, computers, paint and household chemicals** go to the **Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) program**, which is free for City residents. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call Customer Service at **520-791-3171** before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Take it to the Los Reales Landfill ($18 for a covered load under 2,000 pounds, plus a $2 fee); mattresses aren't named on the Brush and Bulky list, so call Customer Service before setting one out |
| Couch / furniture | Your area's twice-a-year Brush and Bulky collection, a Special Brush and Bulky pickup (fees apply), or the Los Reales Landfill |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Brush and Bulky (remove fridge and freezer doors), or the Los Reales Landfill for $20 per appliance plus other fees |
| TV / computer | Free at the HHW drop-off at Los Reales or a community collection event for City residents (CRT tube TVs and monitors aren't accepted); never in your blue bin |
| Paint / chemicals | Free HHW drop-off for City residents (25-gallon paint limit); dried paint can go in your household trash |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** The City's **Los Reales Landfill** lists mattresses among its accepted waste and is open to residential self-haulers. A covered load under 2,000 pounds costs **$18**, every load pays a **$2 Environmental Fee**, and an uncovered load costs **$10 more**. ([City of Tucson: Los Reales Sustainability Campus](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Los-Reales-Sustainability-Campus))

**Brush and Bulky:** The City's accepted list for its curbside Brush and Bulky collection includes furniture but doesn't name mattresses. Call Customer Service at 520-791-3171 to confirm before you put a mattress out.

**Bed bugs:** We couldn't find an official City of Tucson rule about wrapping or bagging a mattress with bed bugs. Ask Customer Service before you set one out or haul it.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Brush and Bulky collection:** Twice a year, City crews visit each of Tucson's 26 residential trash service areas to collect bulky items. Furniture and carpet are accepted. The cost is part of your monthly residential trash fee, which covers weekly trash, every-other-week recycling, Brush and Bulky collection twice a year and neighborhood cleanups. ([City of Tucson: Residential Trash Collection](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services/Residential-Trash-Collection)) Find your collection week on the City's 2026 Brush and Bulky schedule or by searching your address. ([City of Tucson: Brush and Bulky](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services/Brush-and-Bulky))

### Step by Step: Brush and Bulky

1. **Find your week.** Look up your service area on the City's Brush and Bulky page. Areas 19 and 25 are a by-appointment pilot: call the phone number on your sticker to schedule.
2. **Set it out on time.** Have everything out by 6 a.m. on the Monday of your collection week. Crews may come any day that week. Don't put materials out more than two weeks early.
3. **Use the curb.** Since January 1, 2026, piles must be at the curb in front of your home. Piles left in alleys won't be collected. ([Know Where to Throw: Brush and Bulky Guidelines](https://www.knowwheretothrow.com/_files/ugd/46a705_cb8e6cf208054780a221b83ef6397b2f.pdf))
4. **Leave space.** Keep items at least 3 feet from trash and recycling containers, utility meters, mailboxes, vehicles, walls and fences.
5. **Stay under the limit.** The maximum is **10 cubic yards per household** (one cubic yard is about the size of a washing machine). Anything over the limit stays behind.

**Not accepted at Brush and Bulky:** compressed gas or air cylinders, concrete, construction material, dirt, stone, rocks and gravel, glass and mirrors, household hazardous waste, and TVs and computer monitors.

**Special Brush and Bulky:** If your collection isn't coming soon, you can order a special pickup any time online or by calling Customer Service at 520-791-3171. Fees apply. The Brush and Bulky page doesn't list the amount, so ask when you book. Put the items where you'd normally set out for Brush and Bulky.

**Don't use your trash container:** Furniture, water heaters, tires, dirt, concrete and building materials don't go in your green trash container. If it won't fit, take it to Los Reales, wait for Brush and Bulky, or request a special collection. If you share an alley container, don't leave bulky items in or next to it.

**Landfill:** The Los Reales Landfill (5300 E. Los Reales Road, south of Los Reales Road at Craycroft Road) takes furniture, rugs, mattresses, construction debris and concrete. It's open Monday to Saturday, 6 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sundays, New Year's Day, Thanksgiving (November 26, 2026) and Christmas (December 25, 2026). Covered loads over 2,000 pounds cost $38 per ton. Bring a card: the landfill takes Apple Pay, tap, and Visa, Mastercard or Discover credit or debit cards only. Call 520-791-4183 for general information.

### Outside City Limits?

The City's residential trash service covers homes inside Tucson city limits: single-family homes and buildings with 4 units or fewer. ([City of Tucson: Residential Services](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services)) Pima County says that if you live outside the City of Tucson limits, you'll need to hire a solid waste hauler or haul it yourself to a disposal facility. The County lists permitted haulers, landfills and transfer stations on its waste page. ([Pima County: Waste and Recycling](https://www.pima.gov/waste-recycling)) Marana, Oro Valley and Sahuarita are separate towns, so check your own town's page. If you live in a larger apartment complex or an HOA with a private hauler, ask your property manager or hauler how bulky items are handled.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Brush and Bulky:** Appliances are on the accepted list. Remove refrigerator and freezer doors before you set them out. ([City of Tucson: Brush and Bulky](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services/Brush-and-Bulky))

**Landfill:** Los Reales charges **$20 per appliance** designed to use refrigerant, on top of any other fees. ([City of Tucson: Los Reales Sustainability Campus](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Los-Reales-Sustainability-Campus))

**Freon:** The City's pages don't say whether you must have refrigerant removed before a Brush and Bulky pickup. Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Not curbside:** TVs and computer monitors aren't accepted at Brush and Bulky. Electronics should never go in your blue recycling bin, because they can cause fires and damage recycling equipment.

**HHW program:** City of Tucson residents can bring electronics to the Los Reales HHW site or a community collection event. Accepted items include computers and laptops, computer monitors, printers and scanners, televisions, cell phones and tablets, keyboards and accessories, cables and chargers, and DVD players and other small electronics. ([City of Tucson: Electronics Recycling](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Household-Hazardous-Waste/Electronics-Recycling))

**CRT (tube) TVs and monitors:** The HHW program doesn't accept them. The City's Know Where to Throw site points to Suburban Miners for CRT TVs and monitors. ([Know Where to Throw: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.knowwheretothrow.com/hhw))

**Other local options:** The City lists Keeper of the Grumper, ACE Recycling and We Care Tucson as local organizations that accept electronics. Check each one's own site for what they take and any fees. The US EPA also keeps a list of [manufacturer and retailer electronics donation and recycling programs](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling).

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) program:** The program is **free for City of Tucson residents**. Residents of Marana, Oro Valley, Sahuarita and unincorporated Pima County pay a **$15 fee**. Show a driver's license or current utility bill to prove residency. ([City of Tucson: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Household-Hazardous-Waste))

| Site | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Los Reales Sustainability Campus HHW Drop-Off | 5300 E. Los Reales Rd. | Thu, Fri and Sat, 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. (closed the 2nd Saturday of each month, except in summer) |
| Community collection event, November 14, 2026 | Eastside City Hall, 7820 E. Broadway Blvd. | 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. |
| Community collection event, December 12, 2026 | Pima Community College West, 2202 W. Anklam Rd. | 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. |

Event dates are from the City's [HHW Community Collection Events](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Household-Hazardous-Waste/HHW-Community-Collection-Events) page. Events pause during the summer. For HHW questions, call 520-791-4183.

- **Paint:** up to **25 gallons** of liquid paint products. Dried paint can be thrown away in your household trash, and dried paint or empty paint cans aren't accepted at HHW.
- **Automotive fluids:** up to 10 gallons (bring a disposable container). Cooking oil is also accepted (strain out food and bring it in a plastic container).
- **Also accepted:** engine oil filters, auto batteries, rechargeable, lithium and button batteries (keep types separate), cleaning products, drain openers, mercury products, fluorescent bulbs, solvents, hobby chemicals, pesticides, lawn and garden products, pool chemicals, propane cylinders, printer cartridges, and anything labeled acid, flammable, caustic, poison, caution, toxic, danger or warning.
- **Not accepted:** alkaline batteries (these go in your regular trash), business or commercial waste, commercial gas cylinders, explosives or ammunition, infectious and radioactive waste, and medical waste such as sharps, syringes and old medications.

([City of Tucson: What Can I Bring to HHW?](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Household-Hazardous-Waste/What-can-I-bring-to-HHW))

**Never in your bins:** Don't put hazardous or toxic waste in your trash container, or pour it down the drain.

Questions? Call Environmental Services Customer Service at **520-791-3171**, email eshelp@tucsonaz.gov, or call **311**. ([City of Tucson: Environmental Services](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services))

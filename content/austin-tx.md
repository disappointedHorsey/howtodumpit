# Junk Disposal in Austin, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Austin, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Not confirmed in bulk collection: check the scheduler / 3-1-1 |
| Couch / furniture | On-demand bulk collection by appointment |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | On-demand bulk (doors removed) or Recycle and Reuse Center (ACs) |
| TV / computer | Recycle and Reuse Center |
| Paint / chemicals | On-demand curbside HHW pickup or Recycle and Reuse Center (appointment) |
| Christmas tree | Curbside on your composting day starting December 26; cut trees 6 feet or taller in half |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** We could **not confirm** from official City pages that Austin's bulk collection takes mattresses. Austin Resource Recovery runs **on-demand bulk collection by appointment** (up to 3 per year), and its scheduler shows which items it accepts. **Check there or call 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** before booking. The City's **Recycle and Reuse Center does not accept mattresses**.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step (If the Scheduler Accepts Your Mattress)

1. Schedule through the **Austin Recycles app**, the City's online scheduler, or **Austin 3-1-1**. Items are not picked up without an appointment.
2. Set items at the curb **by 5:30 a.m.** on your appointment date.
3. Keep them **5 feet** from carts, mailbox, fences, meters, hydrants and parked cars. Items in alleys, in front of vacant lots, or in front of businesses won't be collected.
4. **Don't bag or box items.** Bags are treated as extra trash and may incur fees.

### Apartments (5+ Units)

On-demand bulk is for ARR residential customers (single-family up to fourplexes). Larger complexes use their own hauler, so ask your property manager.

### Need It Gone / Not Accepted?

Use one of the local junk removal companies listed above.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Furniture** is an accepted item in Austin Resource Recovery's **on-demand bulk collection** for ARR residential customers (single-family up to fourplexes; up to three per year). Schedule through the Austin Recycles app, online, or Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000), and set items at the curb by 5:30 a.m. on the appointment date. The **Recycle and Reuse Center does not accept furniture**. ([ARR On-demand Bulk](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/programs/demand-bulk-brush-and-household-hazardous-waste-collection); [Recycle and Reuse Center](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/locations/recycle-and-reuse-drop-center))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Appliances (remove doors)** are accepted in on-demand bulk collection; set metal items in a separate pile. The Recycle and Reuse Center also lists air conditioners, stoves, washers, dryers and water heaters among accepted electronics/appliances. ([ARR On-demand Bulk](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/programs/demand-bulk-brush-and-household-hazardous-waste-collection); [Recycle and Reuse Center](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/locations/recycle-and-reuse-drop-center))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

The **Recycle and Reuse Center** accepts televisions, computers, printers, cell phones, microwaves and more. Check its page for hours and whether you need an appointment for your item. ([Recycle and Reuse Center](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/locations/recycle-and-reuse-drop-center))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

ARR curbside customers can schedule **on-demand household hazardous waste collection** (paint and thinners, cleaners, pesticides, pool chemicals, auto fluids, batteries, small propane cylinders). Don't set HHW at the curb. Leave it somewhere accessible to staff, out of rain and sun; containers up to 5 gallons and 30 gallons total per collection. You can also drop off HHW at the **Recycle and Reuse Center by appointment**. ([ARR On-demand HHW](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/programs/demand-bulk-brush-and-household-hazardous-waste-collection))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** Starting December 26, City of Austin curbside customers can recycle a natural Christmas or holiday tree at the curb on their regular composting collection day. Set the tree out by 5:30 a.m. Zilker Park drop-off dates for 2026 had not been announced as of November 10, 2026.

Austin Resource Recovery says to remove all ornaments, decorations (including tinsel), lights and tree stands, and not to bag the tree. Only natural trees are accepted. Trees sprayed with flocking (artificial snow) are not accepted. Trees 6 feet or taller should be cut in half. ([Austin Resource Recovery: Holiday Tree Recycling](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/programs/holiday-tree-recycling))

**Zilker Park:** All Austinites, including people who are not City curbside customers, can drop off all-natural holiday trees, wreaths and garlands at Zilker Park when the event is running. Remove wreaths and garlands from wire frames. The official page says "2026 dates will be announced later in 2026" and tells volunteers to check back in early December 2026.

**Other drop-off:** Individual trees are listed as free at several sites, with a contamination fee if tags, decorations or plastic are left on. Those sites include Hornsby Bend Biosolids Management Plant, 2210 FM 973 (photo ID required; free for Austin and Travis County residents; 512-974-4343), plus Travis County service centers and other locations named on the City page, including some private businesses (see Other options at the bottom of this page). Call the site before you go, especially if you have more than one tree.

If you missed collection or cannot move the tree yourself, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

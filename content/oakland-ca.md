# Junk Disposal in Oakland, CA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in the **City of Oakland**, California, item by item. Every Oakland household, **owners and renters**, can book a **bulky curbside pickup** and a **bulky drop-off** of up to **4 cubic yards** each, included with your trash service. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call WM's bulky line at **1-888-WM-BULKY (1-888-962-8559)**.

**City of Oakland only.** Oakland's bulky service is run by **Waste Management of Alameda County (WM)**, while recycling, household battery and used motor oil pickup is run by **California Waste Solutions**. **Piedmont** is a separate city surrounded by Oakland, with its own hauler and rules (see Outside City Limits below).

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Free bulky curbside pickup (up to 4 per pickup for 1–4 unit homes, 2 for apartments) |
| Couch / furniture | Free bulky curbside pickup or free Davis Street drop-off (up to 4 cubic yards) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Free bulky curbside pickup (2 appliances for 1–4 unit homes, 1 for apartments) |
| TV / computer | Bulky pickup or drop-off, or the free County hazardous waste facility; never in your carts |
| Paint / chemicals | Free Alameda County hazardous waste facility, 2100 East 7th Street |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Book the **City's bulky curbside pickup**. Homes with 1 to 4 units can set out up to **4 mattresses or box springs**; apartment and condo residents can set out up to **2**, as part of a pickup of up to 4 cubic yards. It's included with your trash service. ([Oakland Recycles: Bulky Junk Services](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/bulky-service/))

### Step by Step: Bulky Curbside Pickup

1. **Book it.** Call **1-888-WM-BULKY (1-888-962-8559)** or [request an appointment online](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/bulky-service/). Renters can book directly, without property manager approval. Give a valid email address so the confirmation and instructions reach you right away.
2. **Expect the timing.** Single-family appointments are normally scheduled within two weeks. Apartment and condo pickups are usually the last week of each month. No curbside appointments are offered during the holiday tree pickup period (end of December to the second week of January).
3. **Stay within the limits.** Up to **4 cubic yards** (about 3 ft x 3 ft x 12 ft) of furniture, carpets (rolled and taped), boxed or bagged items and remodeling debris, plus the large items listed above. Keep large items separate. Set-outs over the limits are charged.
4. **Set it out** at the curb by **6 a.m.** on your appointment day, and not more than one day before, or you may face a City fine.
5. **Second pickup:** homes with 1 to 4 units can get a second curbside pickup at no extra cost, 45 days after using one bulky drop-off and one curbside pickup. A 2026 City flyer gives a different waiting period, so confirm with WM when you book.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find an Oakland rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask WM at 1-888-962-8559 before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, chairs, tables and other furniture** go out with a **bulky curbside pickup**, booked and set out the same way as a mattress. Or haul them yourself: every Oakland household can book **one free drop-off of up to 4 cubic yards** at the **Davis Street Resource Recovery Complex**, 2615 Davis Street, San Leandro. Bring your appointment confirmation and proof of Oakland residency (such as a driver's license or household bill), and pack so you can unload yourself; staff can't help. ([Oakland Recycles: Bulky Junk Services](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/bulky-service/))

**Not accepted** at either: hazardous or medical waste, rock, dirt, concrete or fiberglass.

**Donate first:** StopWaste's [RE:Source](https://resource.stopwaste.org/) guide lists reuse, repair and donation options across Alameda County. Confirm accepted items with the organization first.

**Apartments and condos:** Renters book their own pickup and drop-off. For larger buildings, **property managers** can also schedule a free roll-off container and a bulky removal day for tenants. ([Oakland Recycles: Apartment & Condo Residents](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/services/apartment-condo-residents/))

**Council districts:** We found no difference in bulky service rules between Oakland's council districts or neighborhoods. What changes the limits is whether you live in a 1–4 unit home or a larger apartment or condo building.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Oakland** only, and the bulky drop-off requires proof of Oakland residency. **Piedmont** single-family homes get **2 free bulky pickups a year** (July 1 to June 30), up to 6 cubic yards each, by calling Republic Services at (800) 320-8077 at least 5 business days ahead; apartment pickups must be requested by the owner or manager. ([City of Piedmont: Get rid of bulky items](https://piedmont.ca.gov/services/waste/curbside/bulky)) **Berkeley, Emeryville, Alameda, San Leandro** and **unincorporated Alameda County** each have their own hauler and bulky rules; look up your city on StopWaste's [RE:Source curbside guide](https://resource.stopwaste.org/) or check your city's own website. If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Curbside:** Appliances go out with a **bulky curbside pickup**: up to **2 appliances** for homes with 1 to 4 units, or **1** for apartments and condos. Keep them separate from your other items. Appliances are also accepted at the Davis Street bulky drop-off. ([Oakland Recycles: Bulky Junk Services](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/bulky-service/))

**Not at the hazardous waste facility:** Alameda County's household hazardous waste program does not take refrigerators, washers, dryers, air conditioners or stoves. ([StopWaste: HHW Schedule 2026](https://www.stopwaste.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/HHW%20Flyer.pdf))

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Never in your carts:** Oakland Recycles lists electronics with hazardous waste that should never go in your recycling, compost or trash carts. Oakland Recycles says to use the **bulky service**: a curbside pickup takes up to **4 monitors, CPUs or TVs** (homes with 1 to 4 units) or **2** (apartments), plus consumer electronics. ([Oakland Recycles: Hazardous Waste](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/what-goes-where/hazardous-waste/))

**Drop-off:** The free Alameda County facility at 2100 East 7th Street (below) also takes computers, monitors, TVs, phones and microwaves.

**Batteries:** Put household dry-cell batteries (alkaline, button cell, nickel-cadmium) in a clear zip-lock bag on top of your recycling cart on pickup day; tape the ends of button-cell and nickel-cadmium batteries. Car batteries go to the hazardous waste facility.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never** put paint, solvents, pesticides, cleaners, spray cans, fluorescent bulbs or car batteries in your carts. Alameda County residents can drop them off **free**, with **no appointment**, at the **Oakland Household Hazardous Waste Facility**, 2100 East 7th Street, Oakland: ([StopWaste: Drop-off Facilities](https://www.stopwaste.org/recycling-disposal/hazardous-waste/household-hazardous-waste/drop-off-facilities))

- **Hours:** Wednesday to Friday 9 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed Sunday to Tuesday and on holidays; the 2026 holiday closures listed include **November 23 to 28** and **December 21 to 26**.
- **Bring** a driver's license, state ID or recent utility bill showing your Alameda County address. Stay in your vehicle; staff unload.
- **Limits:** 125 pounds or 15 gallons per vehicle, and no container over 5 gallons or 50 pounds.
- **Not accepted:** medicines, large appliances, construction materials, business waste and large e-bike or vehicle lithium batteries.

**Used motor oil:** California Waste Solutions picks up used oil and filters at the curb in its approved jug and bag; call (510) 625-5263 if you need them. ([Oakland Recycles: Hazardous Waste](https://www.oaklandrecycles.com/what-goes-where/hazardous-waste/))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page or with WM at 1-888-962-8559 before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

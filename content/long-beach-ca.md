# Junk Disposal in Long Beach, CA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in the **City of Long Beach**, California, item by item. If your home has City trash service, you get **12 free special collections a year** for furniture, appliances, TVs and other bulky items. Long Beach residents can also leave old mattresses at the City's free **24/7 Mattress Drop-Off**. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call the Environmental Services Bureau Call Center at **(562) 570-2876**.

**City of Long Beach only.** Long Beach sits inside Los Angeles County, but the City runs its own trash service, so County and neighboring-city rules don't apply inside city limits. **Signal Hill** is a separate city surrounded by Long Beach, with its own hauler and rules (see Outside City Limits below).

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Long Beach residents can drop mattresses and box springs at the free **24/7 Mattress Drop-Off**, 970 W. Chester Place, any time of day. The City sends them to be recycled. Only mattresses and box springs are accepted, and no staff are on site to help unload. ([City of Long Beach: 24/7 Mattress Drop-Off](https://www.longbeach.gov/pw/services/247-mattress-drop-off/))

### Step by Step: Special Collection

1. **Check you qualify.** Special collections are for **City-serviced** refuse accounts. Each account gets **12 special collections per calendar year at no additional cost**. ([City of Long Beach: Special Collection 101](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/refuse/special-collections/special-collection-101/))
2. **Request it.** Use the online [Special Collection Request](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/refuse/special-collections/request-a-special-collection/) form or call (562) 570-2876. The Call Center contacts you by email or phone to confirm a date; you are not scheduled until you get that confirmation.
3. **Stay within the limits.** Each pickup takes up to **8 large items** or **25 bags, bundles or boxes**, and you may use two free pickups on the same day. Items may not exceed 72 x 48 inches; if something is bigger (such as a sofa), tell the City when you schedule.
4. **Set it out** at your regular collection spot by **6 a.m.** on your confirmed day. Don't put items out before your scheduled day; the request page says not until the evening before. Crews can't enter private property.
5. **Mattresses:** don't stack other items on top of a mattress.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask the Call Center before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, chairs, tables and other furniture** go out with a **special collection**, booked and set out the same way as a mattress. **Wrap large glass** (tabletops, mirrors, glass doors) in cardboard or heavy plastic first; unwrapped glass won't be collected. Tied bundles may not exceed 48 inches or 40 pounds, and bags may not exceed 25 pounds.

**Donate first:** The City's [Donation Guide](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/waste-reduction/reduce-waste/donation-guide/) lists local nonprofits that take reusable furniture and household goods; some offer free pickup. Confirm accepted items with the organization first.

**Apartments and condos:** If you rent, ask your **apartment manager** to request a special collection for you; the City asks managers to coordinate bulky item pickups with tenants. If your building uses a **private hauler**, the manager contacts that hauler instead, and the City says to confirm with them whether a special pickup is included. ([City of Long Beach: For Tenants](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/recycling/for-tenants/))

**Council districts:** We found no difference in special collection rules between Long Beach's council districts or neighborhoods. What matters is whether your home is a City-serviced account or uses a private hauler.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Long Beach** only, and the 24/7 Mattress Drop-Off is for Long Beach residents only. **Signal Hill** residents get two free bulky item collections a year (up to four items each) by calling EDCO at 562-597-0608, plus citywide bulky item collection weeks in spring and fall. ([City of Signal Hill: Bulky Item Pick-Up](https://www.cityofsignalhill.org/203/Bulky-Item-Pick-Up)) In **Lakewood**, single-family homes get up to six free EDCO bulky item pickups a year by calling 562-531-3054; apartment and condo residents don't qualify and should ask their property manager. ([City of Lakewood: Bulky Item Pickup Service](https://www.lakewoodca.gov/Residents/Trash-recycling-and-hazardous-waste/Trash-and-recycling-services/Bulky-Item-Pickup)) In **unincorporated Los Angeles County**, most residential haulers offer free bulky item pickup; call the hauler named on your bill or bin, or use the County's service locator. ([LA County Public Works: Mattress Recycling Program](https://cleanla.lacounty.gov/mrp/)) If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Curbside:** Appliances go out with a **special collection**. Refrigerators and freezers must have their **doors removed or taped shut**. Appliances, TVs, computer monitors and tires are picked up by a **separate truck**, so keep them apart from your other items. ([Special Collection 101](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/refuse/special-collections/special-collection-101/))

**Fee:** The City says a **regulatory fee may apply** for items that need special handling, such as appliances. The amount isn't listed on the City's pages, so ask the Call Center at (562) 570-2876 when you book.

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Never in your carts:** E-waste such as TVs, computers, monitors, printers and cell phones may not go in trash or recycling carts. If it still works, consider donating it. Otherwise, take it to a free household hazardous waste collection event, or to a private recycler (see Other options at the bottom of this page). ([City of Long Beach: E-Waste](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/hazardous-waste-101/ewaste/))

**Curbside:** Televisions, computers and monitors are also on the special collection list. They ride on the separate special handling truck, and a fee may apply.

**Batteries:** Household alkaline and rechargeable (NiCad, nickel metal hydride) batteries can go to the Environmental Services Bureau, 2929 E. Willow St., or to drop-off points at several council district offices. Lithium and lithium-ion batteries are not accepted there; take them to a hazardous waste collection event. ([City of Long Beach: Universal Waste](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/hazardous-waste-101/universal-waste/))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never** put paint, cleaners, motor oil, pesticides, pool chemicals or batteries in your trash or recycling carts. Long Beach and Los Angeles County residents can drop household hazardous waste off **free** at: ([City of Long Beach: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/hazardous-waste-101/household-hazardous-waste/))

- **Los Angeles County EDCO Recycling & Transfer Environmental Collection Center**, 2755 California Ave., Signal Hill (open on scheduled days only; check the County's date list before you go).
- **Gaffey Street S.A.F.E. Center**, 1400 N. Gaffey St., San Pedro, Saturdays and Sundays, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. (1-800-98-TOXIC).

**Leftover house paint:** PaintCare drop-off sites take house paint, primers, stains, sealers and clear coatings for free (a recovery fee is already built into the purchase price). They don't take aerosol spray cans, solvents or industrial paint; take those to a hazardous waste site above. ([City of Long Beach: Paint](https://www.longbeach.gov/lbrecycles/hazardous-waste-101/paint-use-recycling/))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page or with the Call Center at (562) 570-2876 before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

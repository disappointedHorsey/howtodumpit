## Mattresses and box springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** Take it to the **Miramar Landfill Mattress Collection Site** (5161 Convoy St., Mon-Fri 7 a.m.-3 p.m.). It's **free**, with **no drop-off limit**. The City says it **does not provide bulky item or curbside pickup**, so if you can't haul it yourself, you'll need a paid service.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by step: free drop-off

1. Make sure the mattress is dry, not soiled or badly damaged, and bed-bug free.
2. **Take it out of any plastic bag or box.** Bagged or boxed mattresses aren't accepted.
3. Detach futon mattresses from the frame. Futons that don't detach aren't accepted.
4. Drive to the east side of the Miramar Landfill entrance on a weekday, 7-3.
5. Bring **only** mattresses and box springs. Mixed loads may be charged landfill fees.

### Not accepted for recycling

Mattress pads/toppers, crib mattresses, air mattresses, waterbeds, sofa beds, sleeping bags and loose bedding. Mattresses that are too soiled to recycle must go in the trash (see the City page).

### Can't haul it?

The City doesn't do curbside bulky pickup, and Bye Bye Mattress doesn't pick up from individuals. A paid junk-removal service is the option (see the box above).

Checked October 12, 2026. Rules change, so confirm on the City page before you go.*

## Couches and furniture {#furniture}

The City says it **does not provide bulky item or curbside pickup**. We couldn't verify an official City drop-off option for furniture. Call the Environmental Services Department at **858-694-7000** for disposal resources, or use a paid junk-removal service. ([San Diego mattress page](https://www.sandiego.gov/environmental-services/recycling/residential/mattresses); [San Diego HHW](https://www.sandiego.gov/environmental-services/ep/hazardous))

## Appliances (fridges, freezers, AC: freon) {#appliances}

The City's HHW Transfer Facility lists **appliances as not accepted**. We could not verify an official City option for refrigerators or ACs. Call **858-694-7000** for help finding a disposal resource. ([San Diego HHW](https://www.sandiego.gov/environmental-services/ep/hazardous))

## TVs, computers and electronics (e-waste) {#electronics}

The City's HHW Transfer Facility **does not accept electronic waste**. The City's HHW page links to a recycling-location finder for e-waste such as phones, computers, monitors and printers. ([San Diego HHW: E-Waste](https://www.sandiego.gov/environmental-services/ep/hazardous))

## Paint and household hazardous waste {#hazardous}

City residents can drop off HHW **free, by appointment only**, at the **HHW Transfer Facility, Miramar Landfill entrance, 5161 Convoy St.**: **Wednesdays and Saturdays, 9 a.m.–3 p.m.** (closures listed on the City page). Bring proof of City residency. Accepted: paints, cleaners, motor oil, batteries, pesticides, mercury devices and similar. Not accepted: electronics, appliances, containers over 5 gallons, explosives, treated wood, business waste. Leftover paint can also go to a **PaintCare** drop-off site at no charge (most take up to 5 gallons). ([San Diego HHW](https://www.sandiego.gov/environmental-services/ep/hazardous))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

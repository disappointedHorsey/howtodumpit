# Junk Disposal in Houston, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Houston, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Heavy trash day (every other month) |
| Couch / furniture | Heavy trash day |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Heavy trash only with a technician's refrigerant-removed tag |
| TV / computer | Environmental Service Centers or monthly e-waste events (not the curb) |
| Paint / chemicals | Environmental Service Centers (HHW) |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** If your home gets **City of Houston curbside service**, put the mattress out as **heavy trash** on your assigned date. Heavy trash runs **every other month**. Set it out **no earlier than 6:00 p.m. the Friday before** and **by 7:00 a.m.** on collection day.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step

1. Look up your heavy trash date with the City's schedule lookup or the **HTX Collects** app.
2. Set the mattress out in the window: **6:00 p.m. the Friday before** through **7:00 a.m. collection day**.
3. Place it **just behind the curb line** in front of your home. Not in the street, not on the sidewalk.

### Apartment or Outside City Service?

Heavy trash collection is for eligible addresses with City curbside service. If you rent in a complex or live in a suburb or MUD with its own hauler, ask your property manager or hauler.

### Need It Gone Sooner?

Heavy trash comes every other month. To find drop-off options, use the HTX Collects Waste Wizard. If you can't wait for the city, a paid junk removal service is the fallback.

*Sources: [Houston Solid Waste: Heavy Trash Collection](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/heavytrash.html); [HTX Collects](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/htxcollects.html). Checked October 12, 2026. Rules change, so confirm on the official page before you set anything out.*

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

Household furniture (couches, tables, dressers) and carpet (bundled) go out on your **heavy trash** date. Use the same set-out window: **6 p.m. the Friday before through 7 a.m. collection day**, just behind the curb. Houston also lists **six Residential Drop-Off Centers** for bulk items. ([Heavy Trash](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/heavytrash.html); [Drop-Off Centers](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/dropoff.html))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Stoves, washers, dryers and water heaters are accepted as heavy trash. **Refrigerators and freezers are accepted only with a certified technician's tag showing the refrigerant has been removed.** ([Heavy Trash](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/heavytrash.html))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Don't put electronics at the curb** with garbage or heavy trash. Take them to an **Environmental Service Center**: **South, 11500 S. Post Oak Rd.** (Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat 8–5) or **North, 5614 Neches St.** (second Thursday of each month 9–3). Limit: **5 large electronic items per visit**. Monthly Saturday e-waste events also run 9–3 at Kingwood (1st Saturday) and Ellington/Clear Lake (2nd Saturday). ([Environmental Service Centers](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/esc.html); [Electronics & Recycling](https://houstontx.gov/solidwaste/ewaste.html))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Household hazardous waste goes to the **Environmental Service Centers** (addresses and hours above), City of Houston residents only, no business waste. Latex paint, used oil, antifreeze and batteries can also go to the **Westpark Consumer Recycling Center**. The City's limits: two 5-gallon and ten 1-gallon paint cans, 5 gallons of oil, 5 gallons of antifreeze. HHW information line: **713.551.7355**. ([Houston HHW](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/hhw.html))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

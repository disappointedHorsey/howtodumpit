# Junk Disposal in Philadelphia, PA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Philadelphia, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Regular trash, only if sealed in plastic |
| Couch / furniture | Wood-frame: regular trash (2/week); metal-frame: Bulk Collection appointment |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** Put it out on your **regular trash day**, but **only if it's fully sealed in a plastic mattress bag** (max two large items per week). If you'd rather not bag it, drive it to one of the City's **six sanitation convenience centers** (Mon-Sat, 8-6), where mattresses are accepted **unwrapped**.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Curbside

1. Get a plastic mattress bag and **fully seal** the mattress or box spring inside it.
2. Set it out with your regular trash on collection day. Limit: **two** compactible furniture items per week.

### Don't Use the Bulk Collection Appointment for Mattresses

Philadelphia's appointment-based **Bulk Collection** program is for appliances, metal-frame furniture, TVs and tires. **Mattresses are not collected through it.** Wrapped mattresses go out with regular trash.

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** put out an unwrapped mattress. It won't be collected and can be fined.

### Need It Gone Today?

Self-haul to a convenience center or use a paid junk removal service.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

Compactible furniture like sofas goes out with **regular trash**, limited to **two items per week**. Furniture with a **wooden frame** goes out with regular trash. Furniture with a **metal frame** goes through the appointment-based **Bulk Collection** program (up to 4 items per appointment, one appointment per household per week; single-family homes and buildings up to six units). Book at bulkcollection.phila.gov or through 311. ([Residential trash rules](https://www.phila.gov/departments/department-of-sanitation/about/rules-and-regulations/residential-trash-and-recycling-rules/); [Bulk Collection](https://bulkcollection.phila.gov/))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Refrigerators (**doors removed**), air conditioners, water heaters and other major appliances can be picked up through **Bulk Collection** by appointment. You can also take up to **two** bulk items, including items containing refrigerants, per day to a **sanitation convenience center**. ([Bulk Collection](https://bulkcollection.phila.gov/); [Convenience centers](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/find-a-sanitation-convenience-center-to-drop-off-trash-or-recycling/))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

Computers, monitors and TVs go to a **sanitation convenience center** (six sites, **Mon–Sat 8–6**, proof of residency required). Bulk Collection also takes **flat-screen TVs** by appointment. E-waste is **not** accepted at HHW events. ([Convenience centers](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/find-a-sanitation-convenience-center-to-drop-off-trash-or-recycling/); [Bulk Collection](https://bulkcollection.phila.gov/))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Oil, latex and water-based **paint**, solvents, pesticides, auto fluids and other HHW go to the City's **seasonal HHW collection events** (9 a.m.–3 p.m., no registration). Remaining 2026 date: **Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, Port Richmond, 3901 N. Delaware Ave.** Convenience centers also take **latex/water-based paint if solidified** (add kitty litter or newspaper). ([HHW events](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/dispose-of-household-hazardous-waste/); [Convenience centers](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/find-a-sanitation-convenience-center-to-drop-off-trash-or-recycling/))

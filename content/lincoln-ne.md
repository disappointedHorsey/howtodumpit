# Junk Disposal in Lincoln, NE: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in **Lincoln**, item by item. The City of Lincoln does **not** run household trash or bulky item pickup: homes hire one of the City's listed **private refuse haulers**, so ask your hauler first about large items. For self-haul, the City's **North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station** takes loads from Lancaster County residents for a small vehicle fee. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call the transfer station gatehouse at **402-441-8104**.

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Ask your private trash hauler whether it picks up mattresses and what it charges, or take the mattress yourself to the **North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station**. We didn't find a City page that names mattresses specifically, so call the gatehouse at 402-441-8104 before you drive. ([City of Lincoln: North 48th Street Solid Waste Management Facility](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Facilities-and-Services/North-48th))

### Step by Step: Self-Haul to North 48th Street

1. **Check you qualify.** The transfer station serves **Lancaster County** customers in cars, passenger or cargo vehicles and small trailers with a cargo area **under 60 square feet**.
2. **Cover the load.** Tarp, net or tie everything down; uncovered loads pay an extra **50% of the vehicle fee**. ([Solid Waste Rates, Effective January 1, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/14/ltu/utilities/solid-waste-management/solid-waste-rates.pdf))
3. **Go during open hours:** Monday to Friday, **6:45 a.m. to 4:15 p.m.**; Saturday, **6:45 a.m. to 3 p.m.**; Sunday, **6:45 a.m. to noon**. Closed New Year's Day, Memorial Day, the 4th of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.
4. **Pay the vehicle fee:** **$5.25** for a car, **$9.75** for a passenger vehicle, **$18** for a cargo vehicle or trailer only, and **$22.50** for a vehicle plus trailer with waste in both. Credit cards, cash and checks are accepted.
5. **Sort as told.** Appliances, metals, tires, used oil, lead-acid batteries and yard waste go in separate marked areas. Material should be cut to about **6 feet** long.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask your hauler or the gatehouse before you bring one.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, chairs, tables and other furniture:** there's no City bulky pickup, so start with your trash hauler. The City keeps the official list of licensed garbage and recycling collectors in Lincoln, with phone numbers; call yours for bulky item pickup rules and prices. ([City of Lincoln: Garbage and Recycling Collectors](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Recycling/Collectors)) Or haul it to the **North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station**, which the City says is meant for Lancaster County residents without collection service or with special disposal needs. ([North 48th Street Solid Waste Management Facility](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Facilities-and-Services/North-48th))

**Bigger loads:** the Bluff Road Landfill (6001 Bluff Road) is for refuse haulers and trailers with more than 60 square feet of cargo space, not everyday self-haul. ([City of Lincoln: Solid Waste Management](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management))

**Apartments and condos:** your building's hauler handles the dumpsters, so ask your property manager or HOA before you leave anything out.

### Outside City Limits?

Outside Lincoln but inside **Lancaster County**, you can still use the North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station and HazToGo, which both serve Lancaster County residents. ([City of Lincoln: Solid Waste Management](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management)) Residents of other counties should check their own county's landfill or transfer station. Apartment, condo and HOA communities with a **private hauler** should ask their property manager or HOA.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, washers, dryers and other household appliances** can go to the **North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station** for **$8.25 per appliance**, on top of the vehicle fee. Appliances must be placed in the designated appliance area. ([Solid Waste Rates, Effective January 1, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/14/ltu/utilities/solid-waste-management/solid-waste-rates.pdf); [North 48th Street Solid Waste Management Facility](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Facilities-and-Services/North-48th)) Your trash hauler may also take appliances; ask about the price.

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**TVs and computers:** the City says a number of recyclers, nonprofits and local retailers take computer and TV equipment, and most charge a recycling fee. ([City of Lincoln: Solid Waste FAQ](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/FAQ)) HazToGo does **not** take electronics or computers. Wipe personal data before you let a device go.

**Holiday lights:** don't put them in curbside recycling bins or at the City's recycling sites. ([City of Lincoln: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Events/Tree-Recycling))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Latex paint** isn't hazardous: let it dry out until solid and put it in your regular trash. **Oil-based paint** and other household chemicals go to **HazToGo**, Lincoln's Hazardous Waste Center, run by the Lincoln-Lancaster County Health Department. ([Solid Waste FAQ](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/FAQ))

**HazToGo**, 5101 North 48th Street, is open **Wednesdays and Fridays, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.**, and the **third Saturday of each month, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.** (December 19, 2026, and January 16, 2027). It's closed Friday, December 25, 2026, and Friday, January 1, 2027. There's **no cost** to Lincoln and Lancaster County residents for household hazardous waste. It takes cleaners, oil-based paint, paint thinner, stains, pesticides, weed killer, pool chemicals, mercury and mixed fuels; it does not take latex paint, electronics, tires, ammunition or large propane grill cylinders. Call 402-441-8021 with questions. ([Lincoln-Lancaster County Health Department: HazToGo](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/Health-Department/Environmental/Waste-Management))

**Tires:** the North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station takes up to **9 tires** per customer at **$5.25** per car tire plus the vehicle fee.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Lincoln sets up **Christmas tree drop-off sites from Christmas Day through mid-January** each year. Take off all decorations, tinsel, lights, skirts, bags and stands. You can also ask your trash hauler about tree pickup. ([City of Lincoln: Solid Waste FAQ](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/FAQ))

**Last season (2025-26):** drop-off ran **December 25, 2025, through January 19, 2026**, at six parks: Ballard Park (3901 North 66th Street), Oak Lake Park (North First Street, three blocks south of Cornhusker), Seng Park at University Place (North 50th and Garland streets), Holmes Lake Park (South 70th and Normal streets), Tierra Park (South 29th Street and Tierra Drive) and Woods Park (South 31st and "J" streets). Boy Scout Troop 8 also offered free curbside tree pickup on set days (donations appreciated). The 2026-27 sites and dates were **not posted** as of November 25, 2026. ([City of Lincoln: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/Utilities/Solid-Waste-Management/Events/Tree-Recycling))

**Holiday collection:** Lincoln's trash is collected by private haulers, so Christmas and New Year's schedules vary; check with your own hauler. The North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station is closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Does Lincoln have bulk trash pickup?

Not from the City. Lincoln homes hire a private trash hauler, and bulky pickup depends on your hauler. Call yours, or self-haul to the North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station.

### How much does the Lincoln dump cost?

At the North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station, the 2026 vehicle fee is $5.25 for a car, $9.75 for an SUV or minivan and $18 for a pickup or cargo van. Appliances are $8.25 each on top.

### Where can I get rid of a refrigerator in Lincoln, NE?

At the North 48th Street Waste Transfer Station, for $8.25 per appliance plus the vehicle fee. Put it in the marked appliance area.

### Where do I take old paint in Lincoln?

Dry out latex paint and throw it away. Take oil-based paint to HazToGo at 5101 North 48th Street, free for Lancaster County residents.

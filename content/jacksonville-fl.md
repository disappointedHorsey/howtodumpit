# Junk Disposal in Jacksonville, FL: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Jacksonville, Florida, item by item. The City of Jacksonville picks up **household bulk items** such as mattresses and furniture **at the curb without an appointment**, on your garbage day or your bulk collection day depending on your service area. **Appliances** (including fridges and AC units) must be **scheduled** for curbside pickup. Paint, chemicals and electronics go to the City's **Household Hazardous Waste Facility**, free for Duval County residents. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call (904) 630-CITY (2489) before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Curbside on your garbage day (non-automated areas) or bulk collection day (automated areas); no appointment needed |
| Couch / furniture | Curbside household bulk collection, same schedule as mattresses |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Schedule a curbside appliance pickup at (904) 630-2489 or through MyJax; doors secured or removed, food removed |
| TV / computer | Free drop-off at the Household Hazardous Waste Facility or a mobile collection event (limit 3 CRTs and TVs); flat-panel TVs can be scheduled with appliance pickup |
| Paint / chemicals | Free for Duval residents at the Household Hazardous Waste Facility, 2675 Commonwealth Ave. (up to 27 gallons of paint per 30 days) |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Put it at the curb **by 6 a.m.** on the right day for your area: your **regular garbage day** if you don't have City-issued automated carts, or your **bulk collection day** if you do. **You don't need to call to schedule it.**

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Curbside Bulk Collection

1. Find out whether your area has **automated** collection (City-issued garbage cart) or **non-automated** collection (your own cans and bags). Confirm your days with the City's [Collection Schedule lookup](https://myjax.custhelp.com/app/home) or call (904) 630-CITY (2489).
2. Pick the day. The City's Waste Wizard says to place mattresses and box springs at the curb on your **garbage day** (non-automated areas) or your **bulk collection day** (automated areas). In the Waste Pro automated service area, bulk collection day comes **every other week**. ([Waste Wizard](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/waste-wizard), [Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards))
3. If the mattress is being thrown out because of **bed bugs**, **wrap it in plastic**. ([Waste Wizard](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/waste-wizard))
4. Set it **within 5 feet of the curb** by **6 a.m.** on collection day, and not before 5 p.m. the day before. ([Household Bulk Collection](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/household-bulk-collection), [Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards))

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** dump it. Illegal dumping on vacant land, roads, streets, drains and ditches is against the law; report it at (904) 630-CITY (2489). ([Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards))
- **Don't** put hazardous waste of any kind out with bulk or garbage; it's unlawful under Municipal Code Section 380.209.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** The City's household bulk list includes **mattresses, sofas, chairs, other furniture and BBQ grills**. Follow the same schedule as mattresses above. Other bulk rules: **glass** (mirrors, table tops) must be **wrapped and taped**; **rolled carpet and padding** no longer than 5 feet; wire fencing rolled and secured, wooden fencing cut to 5 feet or less; treated or painted wood cut to 6 feet or shorter. Place it all within 5 feet of the curb. ([Household Bulk Collection](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/household-bulk-collection))

**Home projects:** Small amounts of homeowner building materials are collected too: up to **one cubic yard weekly** on garbage day in non-automated areas, or up to **two cubic yards every other week** on bulk day in the Waste Pro automated area, with pieces no longer than 5 feet and no heavier than 40 pounds. Debris from work done by a hired contractor must be removed by the contractor. ([Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards))

**Donate:** For household goods in good condition, the City's Waste Wizard lists donation options including Vietnam Veterans of America, Salvation Army, Goodwill Industries and City Rescue Mission. Call first to confirm what they accept. ([Waste Wizard](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/waste-wizard))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Major appliances are prohibited by law in landfills**, so the City collects them separately from household garbage, and **you must schedule the pickup**. Call **(904) 630-CITY (2489)** or [schedule appliance collection online through MyJax](https://myjax.custhelp.com/app/create_sr/pname/Waste%20Collection/p/129/c/380). ([Used Appliances](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/recycling/used-appliances))

- **Accepted:** refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, water heaters, stoves, microwaves, dishwashers, **air conditioners**, furnaces or heaters, and **flat-panel TVs**.
- **Prep:** **secure or remove the doors** for the safety of children, crews and animals, **remove food** and other materials, and place it within 5 feet of the curb.

The **Household Hazardous Waste Facility** at 2675 Commonwealth Ave. also lists **appliance disposal** among its services. ([Household Hazardous Wastes (HHW)](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/disposal-operations/household-hazardous-wastes-(hhw)))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

The City **encourages recycling** electronics. Small home electronics (computers, TVs, printers) **may** go out with household garbage, but the City notes that once they're in the garbage they **can't be recycled** and go to the landfill. ([Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards), [Electronic Wastes](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/recycling/electronic-wastes))

- **Household Hazardous Waste Facility**, 2675 Commonwealth Ave., Jacksonville, FL 32254, (904) 387-8847. **Tuesday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**; closed Sunday and Monday. Accepts TVs, computer monitors, CPUs, keyboards, printers, scanners, stereo equipment, radios, VCRs and DVD players, camcorders, phones, power tools, small kitchen appliances (microwaves, toaster ovens) and health and beauty appliances. **Limit: 3 CRTs and televisions per customer.** Residential e-waste only. ([Electronic Wastes](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/recycling/electronic-wastes))
- **Mobile collection events** take household hazardous waste and e-waste for Duval County residents (no businesses), 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Dates change, so check the [event schedule](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/disposal-operations/hhw-mobile-collection-events) or call (904) 387-8847.
- **Flat-panel TVs** are on the City's list for scheduled curbside appliance collection. ([Used Appliances](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/recycling/used-appliances))

Some retailers take back electronics and rechargeable batteries; check with the store. Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Household hazardous waste **can't be collected curbside**, and it's unlawful to put it in residential garbage, bulk or recycling. Take it to the City's **Household Hazardous Waste Facility**, 2675 Commonwealth Ave., Jacksonville, FL 32254, **Tuesday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** (904-387-8847), or to a mobile collection event. ([Household Hazardous Wastes (HHW)](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/disposal-operations/household-hazardous-wastes-(hhw)))

- **Cost:** **free for Duval County residents.** Small businesses and non-residents pay a fee.
- **Limits:** **27 gallons of paint** and/or 27 gallons of motor oil in a **30-day period** per Duval County resident.
- **Accepted:** paint, varnish, shellac, thinners and strippers; pesticides, pool chemicals and other chemicals or acids; antifreeze, motor oil and used oil filters; gas, propane, kerosene or diesel fuel and tanks; fireworks, flares, fire extinguishers, gunpowder and ammunition; drain cleaners; thermometers and thermostats; car and rechargeable batteries; photographic chemicals; fertilizer; aerosol cans; and fluorescent and CFL bulbs.

**Empty or nearly empty paint cans:** The City's Waste Wizard says paint cans that are **empty and dry can go in your garbage**. For small amounts of leftover paint, add **cat litter or oil dri** to dry it out first. Full or partly full cans of latex or oil-based paint go to the HHW Facility. ([Waste Wizard](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/waste-wizard))

Not sure where something goes? Search the City's [Waste Wizard](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/waste-wizard), call (904) 630-CITY (2489), or visit [MyJax](https://myjax.custhelp.com/). For landfill disposal information, call Trail Ridge Landfill at (904) 748-6015. ([Service Standards](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/garbage/service-standards))

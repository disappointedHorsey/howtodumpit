# Junk Disposal in Memphis, TN: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Memphis, Tennessee, item by item. The City of Memphis Solid Waste Division gives its residential customers three main options. You can set bulky items at the curb for **Outside the Cart bulk collection** (twice a month), **drop them at a Solid Waste Convenience Center**, or take **one truckload a month to a landfill**. **Paint and household chemicals** go to the **Shelby County Household Hazardous Waste Facility** on Haley Road, not the curb. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call **311** (**901-636-6500**) before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Set it at the curb on your Outside the Cart bulk week, or take it to a Solid Waste Convenience Center |
| Couch / furniture | Set it at the curb on your bulk week (twice a month), or drop it at a Convenience Center with your MLGW bill and ID |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Set it out on your bulk week with the doors removed or taped shut; the City's pages don't cover refrigerant removal, so ask 311 first |
| TV / computer | The County hazardous waste facility takes one computer, monitor and keyboard per family every 30 days, but not TVs; for TVs, ask 311 |
| Paint / chemicals | Take oil-based paint and chemicals to the Shelby County Household Hazardous Waste Facility, 6305 Haley Road; dry out latex paint and bag it in your trash cart |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Memphis picks up bulky items at the curb on your **bulk weeks**, twice a month on your regular collection day. The City names **furniture and mattresses** among the bulky household items it collects on those weeks. ([City of Memphis: Bulk Waste Collection During Fall Season](https://memphistn.gov/city-of-memphis-solid-waste-department-prepares-for-increase-in-bulk-waste-collection-during-fall-season/))

### Step by Step: Outside the Cart Bulk Collection

1. **Find your bulk weeks.** Bulk collection happens **twice a month on your regular collection day**. Check the **Memphis Curbside Collection app** or the City's Solid Waste page for your weeks. ([City of Memphis: Solid Waste](https://memphistn.gov/solid-waste/))
2. **Place it within 3 feet of the street.** Keep bulky piles away from mailboxes, parked cars, fences, trees and low-hanging limbs.
3. **Set it out before the crew comes.** Don't put anything out after crews have passed your house.
4. **Call 311.** The City asks you to call 311 so crews know your items are at the curb and ready for pickup. ([City of Memphis: Household Garbage](https://memphistn.gov/household-garbage/))
5. **Keep tree limbs separate.** Put branches in their own pile, away from furniture, mattresses and other bulky items, so the City can mulch or compost them. ([City of Memphis: Bulk Waste Collection During Fall Season](https://memphistn.gov/city-of-memphis-solid-waste-department-prepares-for-increase-in-bulk-waste-collection-during-fall-season/))

**How big can the pile be?** The City's sources don't agree. The Household Garbage page says bulky piles **must not exceed 10 cubic yards**, while the City's 2025 Clean City Guide says bulky item piles are **limited to half a cubic yard**. ([Memphis City Beautiful: Clean City Guide 2025 (PDF)](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Memphis-Clean-City-Guide-2025-for-Web-and-Email-1.pdf)) Ask 311 before you set out a large pile.

**Bed bugs or soiled mattresses:** We couldn't find an official City rule about wrapping or bagging a mattress for bulk collection. Ask 311 before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Bulk collection:** Furniture goes out on your bulk weeks, with the same set-out rules as mattresses above. ([City of Memphis: Solid Waste](https://memphistn.gov/solid-waste/))

**Solid Waste Convenience Centers:** You can also drop items off at one of the City's two centers. The Solid Waste page lists both as open **Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.** Bring your **latest MLGW bill and a picture ID**.

| Convenience Center | Address | Hours (City Solid Waste Page) |
|---|---|---|
| Collins Yard | 304 Collins Street, Memphis, TN 38112 | Fri to Sun, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. |
| Farrisview | 3207 Farrisview Blvd., Memphis, TN 38118 | Fri to Sun, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. |

([City of Memphis: Solid Waste](https://memphistn.gov/solid-waste/))

A City news release from October 31, 2025 lists Farrisview as open **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.** instead, so call 311 before you drive to Farrisview. ([City of Memphis: Bulk Waste Collection During Fall Season](https://memphistn.gov/city-of-memphis-solid-waste-department-prepares-for-increase-in-bulk-waste-collection-during-fall-season/)) The City's Clean City Guide adds rules for Collins Yard: **Memphis residents only** with ID, **one trip per day** per resident or address, single-axle trailers only, and no commercial haulers, contractors or U-Hauls. Paint, motor oil, batteries, pool chemicals, liquids and other hazardous waste are not allowed. ([Memphis City Beautiful: Clean City Guide 2025 (PDF)](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Memphis-Clean-City-Guide-2025-for-Web-and-Email-1.pdf))

**Monthly landfill load:** Solid Waste Fee paying customers can take **up to one truckload of non-hazardous waste to the landfill each month**. ([City of Memphis: Solid Waste](https://memphistn.gov/solid-waste/)) The Clean City Guide names Republic Services' North Shelby Landfill and South Shelby Landfill, which the City doesn't own or run, and says to bring a valid driver's license or MLGW bill as proof of residency. Landfill fees aren't listed on the City's pages, so call the landfill first.

**Renovation debris:** City ordinance bars the City from collecting dirt, brick, rock, concrete, lumber, roofing, fencing, carpet or other construction and demolition debris, and contractors must remove the waste they create. ([City of Memphis: Household Garbage](https://memphistn.gov/household-garbage/))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Bulk collection:** Residents can place **appliances** at the curb on their designated bulk weeks. Make refrigerators safe by **removing the doors or sealing them shut with tape**. ([Memphis City Beautiful: Clean City Guide 2025 (PDF)](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Memphis-Clean-City-Guide-2025-for-Web-and-Email-1.pdf))

**Freon:** The City's pages don't say whether refrigerant has to be removed before pickup, and the County's hazardous waste facility lists refrigerants as not accepted. ([Shelby County: 2026 Household Hazardous Waste Brochure](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/44346/2026-HHW-Brouchure)) The US EPA says that when a town requires refrigerant to be recovered first, you'd need a professional, such as an appliance repair shop, to remove it. Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal)) Ask 311 how to prepare a fridge, freezer or AC unit before you set it out.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Computers:** The Shelby County Household Hazardous Waste Facility takes **computer screens, monitors and keyboards**, up to **one monitor, one keyboard and one computer per family every 30 days**. Delete your personal data first.

**TVs:** The facility **does not accept televisions**. ([Shelby County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/439/Household-Hazardous-Waste)) The City's pages don't say whether TVs can go out with bulk collection. Check the Waste Wizard in the Memphis Curbside Collection app or call 311 before you set a TV at the curb. The US EPA keeps a list of [manufacturer and retailer electronics donation and recycling programs](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling).

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Shelby County Household Hazardous Waste Facility:** **6305 Haley Road, Memphis, TN 38134**, open **Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.**, except Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day. Phone **(901) 222-7729**.

- **Shelby County residents only.** Staff ask for your car tag and ZIP code. Members of the military are exempt from the tag requirement.
- **Limit:** 100 pounds per vehicle every 30 days.
- Bring items in a **clear tote or box**. Staff unload your materials.
- **Oil-based paint only.** The facility no longer takes latex paint.

([Shelby County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/439/Household-Hazardous-Waste))

The City of Memphis's own hazardous waste page lists older hours (Tuesdays and Saturdays, 8:30 a.m. to 1 p.m.), so call the facility if you're unsure. ([City of Memphis: Hazardous Waste](https://memphistn.gov/hazardous-waste/))

**Accepted, per the County's 2026 brochure:** aerosol sprays, automotive fluids, antifreeze, used motor oil, car batteries, oven and toilet cleaners, adhesives, stains and varnishes, flammable liquids (gas, kerosene, paint thinner), drain cleaners, CFL bulbs and tubes, herbicides, pesticides, fertilizers, oil-based paint, pool chemicals, cooking oil and mercury thermostats. **Not accepted:** business waste, explosives or ammunition, medicine, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, refrigerants, TVs, microwaves, tires, latex paint, unidentifiable materials and empty containers. ([Shelby County: 2026 Household Hazardous Waste Brochure](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/44346/2026-HHW-Brouchure))

**Latex paint:** Dry it out first. Remove the lid and leave the can in a safe, well-ventilated place; for cans more than a quarter full, mix in cat litter or mulch. Once it's dry, put it in a separate bag inside your garbage cart, along with empty dried cans. ([Shelby County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/439/Household-Hazardous-Waste))

Questions? Call **311** (**901-636-6500**). ([City of Memphis: Household Garbage](https://memphistn.gov/household-garbage/))

*Rules, fees and hours change. Confirm on the linked official page or with 311 before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

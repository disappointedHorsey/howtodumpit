# Christmas Tree Pickup in Baltimore, MD (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Last season, Baltimore City DPW took bare Christmas trees with weekly trash collection and offered free mulching at the Sisson Street Residential Recycling Center through early February 2026. DPW had not posted 2026-27 tree dates when we checked on November 16, 2026. If you want the tree gone sooner, or it won't fit at the curb, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 16, 2026, against official Baltimore City Department of Public Works pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: DPW Christmas Tree Collection With Weekly Trash {#trash-collection}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 16, 2026, on the [DPW News and Media page](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/news-and-media)).
- **Last season (2025-26):** DPW said residents could put trees out with their weekly trash collection until Saturday, February 7, 2026, at the exact spot where their trash is collected. If a tree wasn't collected on trash day, residents were told to call 311. ([Baltimore City DPW: Closings in Observance of Martin Luther King Jr. Day](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/news-and-media/dpw-offices-and-sanitation-yards-closed-in-observance-of-martin-luther-king-jr-day))
- **Prep:** DPW's 2026 calendar says to remove all ornaments, tinsel and decorations first. ([Baltimore City DPW 2026 Calendar](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/dpw-2026-calendar))
- **Holidays:** last season there was no tree mulching or collection on New Year's Day or on Martin Luther King Jr. Day. ([DPW 2026 Calendar](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/dpw-2026-calendar); [DPW MLK Day notice](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/news-and-media/dpw-offices-and-sanitation-yards-closed-in-observance-of-martin-luther-king-jr-day))
- **The catch:** the window ends in early February, and an uncollected tree means a call to 311 and another wait.

## Drop It Off Yourself: Sisson Street Mulching {#drop-off}

- **Last season (2025-26):** free Christmas tree mulching for Baltimore City residents through Saturday, February 7, 2026, at the Sisson Street Residential Recycling Center, 2840 Sisson St., 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Saturday. ([DPW MLK Day notice](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/news-and-media/dpw-offices-and-sanitation-yards-closed-in-observance-of-martin-luther-king-jr-day))
- **Prep:** remove ornaments, tinsel and other decorations. Bring bags or containers if you want to take mulch home.
- **2026-27 dates: not published** as of November 16, 2026. Confirm with 311 before you drive.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

DPW's 2026 calendar names Saturday, December 26, 2026, as the trash and recycling make-up day for Christmas, and it applies to Friday collections only. City offices and sanitation yards are closed on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25. ([Baltimore City DPW 2026 Calendar](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/dpw-2026-calendar))

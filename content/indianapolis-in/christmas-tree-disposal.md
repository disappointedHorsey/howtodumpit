# Christmas Tree Recycling in Indianapolis, IN (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Indianapolis asks Marion County residents to drop off bare, real Christmas trees at City park sites so they can be mulched, but it had not posted 2026-27 dates or sites when we checked on November 25, 2026. Last season, drop-off ran from December 25, 2025, through January 31, 2026, dawn to dusk, at seven parks. If you'd rather not haul the tree yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official Indy.gov Department of Public Works pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Indianapolis Christmas Tree Drop-Off {#tree-drop-off}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 25, 2026). The City's page still shows the 2025-26 season. ([Indy.gov: Christmas Tree Disposal](https://www.indy.gov/activity/christmas-tree-disposal))
- **Last season (2025-26):** Thursday, December 25, 2025, until Wednesday, January 31, 2026, from dawn until dusk. Collected trees were mulched into soil blends by GreenCycle.
- **Prep:** remove the tree stand and all ornaments, tinsel, lights and decorations.
- **Who can use it:** Marion County residents only; not for commercial use.
- **The catch:** you have to drive the tree to a listed park. Leaving a tree anywhere not on the list counts as illegal dumping, with fines of up to $3,000, and the City warns the parks can change from year to year.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Christmas Day and New Year's Day are on the City's slide schedule: trash, heavy trash and recycling pickup is delayed one day. Christmas Day and New Year's Day 2026-27 both fall on a Friday, so Friday pickups slide to Saturday, December 26, 2026, and Saturday, January 2, 2027. ([Indy.gov: Find Your Trash Pickup Day](https://www.indy.gov/activity/trash-pickup)) The City page we checked doesn't say whether a whole tree can go out as heavy trash; ask the Department of Public Works at 317-327-4000.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where can I drop off my Christmas tree in Indianapolis?

Last season, at seven City parks: Ellenberger, Garfield, Northwestway, Perry, Riverside, Sahm and Riverwood. The 2026-27 list wasn't posted when we checked.

### When does Indianapolis Christmas tree recycling start?

Last season it started on Christmas Day, December 25, 2025, and ran through January 31, 2026. The 2026-27 dates hadn't been posted as of November 25, 2026.

### Can I leave my Christmas tree at any Indianapolis park?

No. Only the listed sites accept trees. Leaving one anywhere else is illegal dumping, with fines of up to $3,000.

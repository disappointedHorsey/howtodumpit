# Junk Disposal in Detroit, MI: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Detroit, Michigan, item by item. The City of Detroit Department of Public Works (DPW) gives you three main options. You can **put bulk items at the curb** on your scheduled collection day (up to 2 cubic yards), **drop them off free at a DPW Citizen Drop-Off Center** if you're a Detroit resident with ID, or **book a DPW paid pickup** for bigger loads. **TVs, computers, paint and household chemicals** go to the City's free, residents-only **Household Hazardous Waste facility**, not the curb. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call DPW's Solid Waste Division at **(313) 876-0004** before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Put it out with curbside bulk on your collection day (2 cubic yard limit) or drop it at a free DPW Citizen Drop-Off Center |
| Couch / furniture | Curbside bulk on your collection day or a free Citizen Drop-Off Center; for more than 2 cubic yards, book a DPW paid pickup |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Curbside bulk only after the doors are removed and the refrigerant is drained; the HHW facility doesn't take appliances or AC units |
| TV / computer | Take it to the free Household Hazardous Waste facility at 2000 E. Ferry St. (Detroit residents only) or use a free manufacturer takeback program |
| Paint / chemicals | Take it to the Household Hazardous Waste facility (Thursdays and the fourth Saturday of the month, free for Detroit residents); never put it at the curb |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** The City of Detroit lists **mattresses** as **bulk items** that are collected at the curb on your scheduled bulk collection day. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide)) You can also drop a mattress off for free at a DPW Citizen Drop-Off Center (see the furniture section below).

### Step by Step: Curbside Bulk Collection

1. **Find your collection day.** Use the City's [Waste Pickup Schedule tool](https://detroitmi.gov/webapp/find-your-waste-pickup-schedule), or text your street address to **(313) 800-7905** for reminders the evening before each pickup. ([City of Detroit: Bulk & Yard Waste](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste))
2. **Stay under the limit.** Put out **no more than 2 cubic yards** (about the size of two stoves) and **no more than 1,000 pounds** of bulk items. ([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection guide](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))
3. **Set it out on time.** Place bulk items at the curb **no earlier than 6 p.m. the evening before** and **no later than 7 a.m.** on collection day, away from your trash cart, yard waste and recycling. Bag or box smaller items such as clothing, toys and lamps. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide))
4. **Only in front of your own home.** Don't put bulk items in front of a vacant lot or another house. That can get you a ticket, and items over the limit won't be collected. The City says the fine for going over the bulk limit is **up to $1,500 for a first offense** and **up to $10,000 for a fourth offense**. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide))

**Missed pickup?** Call your City contractor on your collection day: **Priority Waste at (855) 927-8365** for East and Southwest Detroit, or **Waste Management at (844) 233-8764** for the west side. After your service day, call DPW at (313) 876-0004. ([City of Detroit: Refuse Collection](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection))

**Bed bugs or soiled mattresses:** We couldn't find an official City rule about wrapping or bagging mattresses. Ask DPW at (313) 876-0004 before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside bulk:** **Furniture** is a bulk item, collected at the curb on your scheduled collection day with the same 2 cubic yard and 1,000 pound limit and set-out times as mattresses above. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide))

**More than the limit? Book a DPW paid pickup.** If you have more than 2 cubic yards or 1,000 pounds, DPW offers a paid pickup. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide))

1. Fill out the City's [Paid Pickup Bulk Collection Request Form](https://detroitmi.gov/forms/paid-bulk-waste-pick-request-form) or call DPW at **(313) 876-0004**.
2. A DPW employee responds **within 1 business day** with an estimate. The charge is **based on the size and weight** of the debris.
3. Pay DPW (credit cards and cash are accepted at a DivDat kiosk), then DPW removes the debris.
4. Most debris is accepted, including **couches**, but it must be **reachable from the front curb**. DPW won't remove debris from garages, alleys or yards.

([City of Detroit: Paid Pick-Up Bulk Collection Service](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste/paid-pick-bulk-collection-service))

**Free Citizen Drop-Off Centers:** Detroit residents with proper ID can drop off bulk items for free at these DPW sites, up to **1,000 pounds per person per day**. ([City of Detroit: Free Citizen Bulk Drop Off Centers](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste/free-citizen-bulk-drop-centers))

| Drop-Off Center | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Southfield Yard | 12255 Southfield | Mon to Sat, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. (April 1 to October 31); 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. (November 1 to March 31) |
| Davison Yard | 8221 W. Davison | Mon to Sat, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. (April 1 to October 31); 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. (November 1 to March 31) |
| J. Fons Transfer Station | 6451 E. McNichols | Mon to Fri, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Sat, 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. |

([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection guide](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf)) The City's drop-off page lists the Davison Yard at **7901 W. Davison**, while its 2026 guide and Bulk & Yard Waste page say **8221 W. Davison**, so call (313) 876-0004 if you can't find it.

**Not accepted at the drop-off centers:** household garbage, remodeling, construction or demolition debris, building materials, chemicals and containers, car or truck parts, fuel tanks, tree logs over 8 inches across or 8 feet long, tires on rims, and more than 4 tires. Commercial vehicles aren't allowed at the Citizen Drop-Off Centers. ([City of Detroit: Free Citizen Bulk Drop Off Centers](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste/free-citizen-bulk-drop-centers))

**Renovation debris:** J. Fons Transfer Station takes renovation materials for a fee: the City's 2026 guide lists **$95 for the first 2,000 pounds** (2,000 pound minimum), with a prorated cost after that. It doesn't take tires, paint, liquids or hazardous materials. ([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection guide](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))

**Still usable?** The City-listed **Recycle Here!** drop-off at **5960 Lincoln St.** runs a take-something, leave-something area called the "Junk Hole" for items that can be reused or upcycled. It's free for Detroit residents and open Monday and Wednesday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. The City's page doesn't say whether furniture is accepted there, so ask first. ([City of Detroit: Recycle Here! Drop-off Facility](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/detroit-recycles/recycle-here-drop-facility))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Curbside bulk:** The City lists **appliances** and **hot water tanks** as bulk items. Before you put one at the curb, the City says to **remove the doors and drain the refrigerants**. The same 2 cubic yard and 1,000 pound limit applies. ([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection guide](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))

**Freon:** The City's pages don't say who should drain the refrigerant or what it costs. The US EPA says that when a town requires refrigerant to be recovered before pickup, you'd need a professional, such as an appliance repair shop, to remove it. Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal)) Ask DPW at (313) 876-0004 if you're not sure your appliance is ready for the curb.

**Not at the HHW facility:** The City's Household Hazardous Waste facility lists **appliances** and **air conditioning units** as **not accepted**. ([City of Detroit: Household Hazardous Waste Information](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/household-hazardous-waste-information))

**Drop-off:** We couldn't confirm on the City's pages whether the Citizen Drop-Off Centers take fridges, freezers or AC units, so call (313) 876-0004 before you go.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Take them to the Household Hazardous Waste facility.** The City's HHW facility accepts **televisions, computer monitors, terminals, laptops, keyboards, mice, printers, scanners, fax machines, cable boxes, cell phones, DVD players and VCRs, gaming units, typewriters and microwaves**. It's **free and open to Detroit residents only**. ([City of Detroit: Household Hazardous Waste Information](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/household-hazardous-waste-information))

- **Address:** 2000 E. Ferry St. (near I-75 and I-94)
- **Hours:** every **Thursday, 7:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.**, and the **fourth Saturday of each month, 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.**
- **Phone:** (313) 923-2240

**Not at Recycle Here!** The City's Recycle Here! drop-off doesn't take electronics, batteries, light bulbs or ink cartridges. ([City of Detroit: Recycle Here! Drop-off Facility](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/detroit-recycles/recycle-here-drop-facility))

**Free manufacturer takeback:** Michigan law requires makers of computers, printers and TVs sold in the state to offer free takeback. TV makers must take back **any brand of TV**, while computer makers only have to take back their own brand. ([Michigan EGLE: Electronics Takeback FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/egle/faqs/recycling/electronic-takeback)) Find a recycler or your brand's program on EGLE's [Properly Dispose of Old TVs, Computers, and Electronics](https://www.michigan.gov/egle/about/organization/materials-management/ewaste/disposal) page. Delete any personal data from computers and phones first.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never put these at the curb.** The City says flammables, explosives, **paint**, batteries, medical waste, fuel, oil, tires, car parts, needles, radioactive materials and chemicals don't go out with bulk collection. ([City of Detroit: 2026 Curbside Collection guide](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2026-02/2026%20DPW_CurbsideColl_2026_021226.pdf))

**Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Receiving Facility:** 2000 E. Ferry St., every Thursday from 7:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. and every fourth Saturday of the month from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. It's **open to Detroit residents only, and there's no charge**. Accepted items include:

- Paint and paint products, aerosol cans
- Pesticides and herbicides, pool chemicals, oxidizers, corrosives (acids and bases)
- Alkaline and auto batteries, fluorescent tubes, mercury
- Gasoline, kerosene, lighter fluid, fuel, oil and antifreeze
- Propane cylinders, fire extinguishers and smoke detectors
- Furniture polish and floor care products
- Medical sharps (in a rigid, sealed container such as a hard plastic detergent bottle) and over-the-counter medicine

**Not accepted:** commercial waste, ammunition or explosives, lab chemicals, construction materials, car parts, nonstandard or unknown gases, radioactive or shock-sensitive materials, controlled substances, regular household trash and yard waste. Call **(313) 923-2240** with questions. ([City of Detroit: Household Hazardous Waste Information](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/household-hazardous-waste-information))

**Latex paint:** We couldn't find an official City rule on drying out latex paint for the trash. Take it to the HHW facility or ask DPW.

**Illegal dumping:** Report it on the City's [Improve Detroit](https://detroitmi.gov/webapp/improve-detroit-report-issue-online) app or call **(313) 876-0426**. ([City of Detroit: Yard and Bulk Waste Guide](https://detroitmi.gov/document/yard-and-bulk-waste-guide))

Questions? Call the DPW Solid Waste Division at **(313) 876-0004**. ([City of Detroit: Bulk & Yard Waste](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/department-public-works/refuse-collection/bulk-yard-waste))

*Rules, fees and hours change. Confirm on the linked official page or with DPW before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

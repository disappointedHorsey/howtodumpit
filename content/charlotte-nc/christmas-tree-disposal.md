# Christmas Tree Pickup in Charlotte, NC (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** At single-family homes with City of Charlotte service, put a bare natural tree at the curb with yard waste on your regular collection day; artificial trees, wreaths and all trees at multifamily properties must be scheduled as bulky pickups. The City had not posted a 2026-27 start date when we checked on November 17, 2026 (last season, natural tree collection began January 5, 2026). If you want the tree gone sooner, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 17, 2026, against official City of Charlotte Solid Waste Services and Mecklenburg County Solid Waste pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of Charlotte Christmas Tree Collection {#tree-collection}

- **Single-family homes:** natural trees go to the curb for yard waste collection on your regular collection day, free of all decorations, including lights, ornaments and tinsel. Artificial trees and all wreaths must be scheduled for bulky item collection. ([City of Charlotte: 2026 Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/2026-Holiday-Collection-Schedule))
- **Multifamily properties** (apartments, condominiums and townhomes served under the City's multifamily contract with dumpsters or compactors): natural trees, artificial trees and all wreaths must be scheduled for bulky item collection. The City says these collections occur throughout January. ([2026 Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/2026-Holiday-Collection-Schedule))
- **Set-out rules:** at the curb by 6 a.m. on your collection day, within 6 feet of the curb and at least 3 feet from other items, mailboxes, poles and vehicles, and no sooner than the day before. Yard waste set out earlier than the day before can draw a minimum $150 citation. ([City of Charlotte: Collection Guidelines](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/Collection-Guidelines))
- **Scheduling bulky pickup:** call 311 (704-336-7600), use the CLT+ app or request online. You must currently get City of Charlotte garbage collection, and unscheduled items are not collected. ([Collection Guidelines](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/Collection-Guidelines))
- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 17, 2026, on the [2026 Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/2026-Holiday-Collection-Schedule) page).
- **Last season (2025-26):** the City said natural Christmas tree collection would begin Monday, January 5, 2026 ([City news, December 22, 2025](https://www.charlottenc.gov/City-News/Solid-Waste-Services-Christmas-and-New-Year%E2%80%99s-Collection-Schedule)). A January 15, 2026, notice said it would continue throughout January, asked single-family residents to remove tree stands as well as decorations, and said no yard waste would be collected the week of Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 19, 2026). ([City news, January 15, 2026](https://www.charlottenc.gov/City-News/Martin-Luther-King-Jr.-Holiday-Collection-Schedule))
- **The catch:** yard waste set out more than a day early can draw a citation, last season's tree collection didn't start until January 5, and apartment residents and anyone with an artificial tree have to book a bulky pickup and wait for the date.

## Drop It Off Yourself: Mecklenburg County Recycling Centers {#drop-off}

- **Last season (2025-26):** Mecklenburg County said residents could drop off up to two natural Christmas trees at no cost, plus one artificial tree free, with no decorations, at its full-service and staffed recycling centers. ([Mecklenburg County news release, December 16, 2025](https://news.mecknc.gov/no-grinches-here-go-green-holiday-recycling-right))
- **Sites named last season:** Compost Central (140 Valleydale Rd., Charlotte), Foxhole (17131 Lancaster Hwy., Charlotte), Hickory Grove (8007 Pence Rd., Charlotte), North Mecklenburg (12300 N. Statesville Rd., Huntersville) and the William R. Davie Staffed Recycling Center (4635 Pineville-Matthews Rd., Charlotte). ([December 16, 2025 release](https://news.mecknc.gov/no-grinches-here-go-green-holiday-recycling-right))
- **Hours:** the four full-service centers are open Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m.; William R. Davie is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ([Mecklenburg County: Recycling and Disposal Centers](https://wipeoutwaste.mecknc.gov/where-can-i-recycle))
- **2026 holiday closings:** the County lists its centers as open Thursday, December 24, 2026, and closed Friday, December 25, and Saturday, December 26, 2026. ([Recycling and Disposal Centers: 2026 Holiday Schedule](https://wipeoutwaste.mecknc.gov/where-can-i-recycle))
- **2026-27 tree terms: not published** as of November 17, 2026. Check the County page before you drive.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

The City is closed Friday, December 25, 2026, with no collection; Friday routes run one day late, on Saturday, December 26. ([City of Charlotte: 2026 Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/2026-Holiday-Collection-Schedule)) The City's New Year's Day 2027 collection schedule was not posted as of November 17, 2026 (the [2026 schedule](https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Trash-and-Recycling/2026-Holiday-Collection-Schedule) says New Year's Day 2026 collection ran as scheduled).

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When does Charlotte pick up Christmas trees?

At single-family homes, natural trees go out with yard waste on your regular collection day. Last season the City started tree collection on January 5, 2026. The 2026-27 start date was not posted as of November 17, 2026.

### Do I need to schedule a pickup for my Christmas tree in Charlotte?

Only for artificial trees, wreaths, and any tree at an apartment, condo or townhome served under the City's multifamily contract. Those are bulky items, booked through 311, the CLT+ app or online.

### Can I drop off my Christmas tree for free in Mecklenburg County?

Last season the County took up to two natural trees and one artificial tree free at its full-service and staffed recycling centers. Check the County page for this season's terms.

### Is Charlotte trash pickup different during Christmas week 2026?

Only Friday routes change. Friday, December 25, 2026, collections happen on Saturday, December 26.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Charlotte right now?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

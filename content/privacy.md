# Privacy & Cookie Policy

**Last updated October 25, 2026**

HowToDumpIt (howtodumpit.com) is a free information guide to getting rid of junk legally and cheaply. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, how cookies and affiliate links work on this site, and the choices you have.

## The Short Version

- You can read every page without giving us any personal information. There are no accounts and no forms.
- Our own pages don't set cookies, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
- Some links are affiliate or referral links. If you click one, the partner's site may set its own cookies, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
- We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
- California residents: see [Your California Privacy Rights](#your-california-privacy-rights) and [Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information](#do-not-sell-or-share-my-personal-information).
- Questions or requests: email [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com).

## Information We Collect

**When you browse.** Our pages don't use forms, accounts, analytics, ad networks, social media widgets or embedded videos. The small scripts on the site (the city search box and the pricing calculator for haulers) run only in your browser and don't send what you type anywhere.

**Our web host.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub handles that data under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We don't receive these logs.

**When you email us.** If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and, for haulers and charities, to set up and manage a listing or partnership. Our email is handled by our email service providers.

**Partner contact features.** We don't currently run call-tracking numbers or quote forms on this site. If we add them, so you can contact a local hauler through us, we'll update this page before they go live.

## Cookies and Tracking

**Our site.** Pages on howtodumpit.com don't set cookies or use tracking pixels. Because of that, we don't show a cookie banner today.

**Affiliate and referral links.** Some links on this site are affiliate or referral links, marked as sponsored or affiliate links. They may be run through affiliate networks such as Rakuten Advertising or through a partner's own referral program. When you click one, you leave our site, and the partner, the affiliate network or both may set cookies or use similar technology on their site to record that you came from HowToDumpIt. That lets them pay us a commission if you buy or sign up. It never costs you anything extra. Those cookies are controlled by the partner and the network under their own privacy policies, so please read them. We don't receive your name or contact details from these programs; we may receive reports such as clicks, sign-ups or sales and the commission earned.

**Analytics.** We don't use analytics right now. We may add analytics later to see how many people read our guides. If we do, we'll update this page first, say which tool we use, and honor your opt-out choices.

**Your controls.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies on a partner's site won't affect how our guides work.

## How We Use and Share Information

We use the information you send us only to answer you and run HowToDumpIt. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run the site and our email, or when the law requires it.

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Since we don't sell or share personal information today, there's nothing more you need to do, and we'll keep honoring GPC if that ever changes.

## Your Privacy Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you (usually just emails you've sent us), give you a copy, correct it or delete it. People in places such as the EU and UK may have more rights under their local laws, including the right to complain to a privacy regulator. To make a request, see [How to Make a Request](#how-to-make-a-request) below.

## Your California Privacy Rights

This section is for California residents. It covers the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and California's Shine the Light law.

### Personal Information We Collect

In the past 12 months we have collected these categories of personal information:

| Category | What it is | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identifiers | Your email address, and your name if you include it, when you email us | You | To reply to you |
| Identifiers | Your IP address, logged by our web host (GitHub Pages) when you visit | Collected automatically by our host | Site security, handled by GitHub |
| Professional or commercial information | For haulers and charities who contact us: business name, city served, what you haul or accept, and anything else you choose to send | You | To set up and manage a listing or partnership |

We don't collect sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers, financial account details, precise location, health information or account logins).

### How We Use It

We use personal information only for these business purposes: answering your questions, setting up and managing listings and partnerships, keeping the site secure, and meeting legal, tax and accounting duties.

### Who We Disclose It To

We disclose personal information only to service providers that help us run HowToDumpIt: our web host (GitHub) and our email service providers. We don't give your personal information to advertisers or affiliate networks.

### Do We Sell or Share Personal Information?

**No.** We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We haven't done either in the past 12 months, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.

Our own pages don't set cookies and don't load third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. The one thing to know about is affiliate links: if you click an affiliate or referral link, you go to the partner's website, and the partner or its affiliate network (such as Rakuten Advertising) may set cookies there to record that you came from HowToDumpIt so they can pay us a commission. Those cookies are placed on the partner's site, not ours, and are covered by the partner's and network's own privacy policies, including any opt-out choices they offer. We don't pass your personal information to them.

### Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We don't sell or share it, but you can still send us an opt-out request, and we'll record it and honor it if our practices ever change. Two ways to opt out:

- Email [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com) with "Do Not Sell or Share" in the subject. You don't need to verify your identity for an opt-out.
- Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser or with a browser extension. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request.

To opt out of cookies set on a partner's site after you click an affiliate link, use that partner's cookie settings or your browser's cookie controls.

### Your Rights

As a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know and access:** ask what personal information we've collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, who we disclosed it to, and get a copy of it
- **Delete:** ask us to delete your personal information
- **Correct:** ask us to fix personal information that's wrong
- **Opt out of sale or sharing:** see above
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information:** we don't collect sensitive personal information, so there's nothing to limit
- **Non-discrimination:** we won't deny you service, charge you a different price or treat you differently for using any of these rights

### How to Make a Request

Email [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com) with "Privacy Request" in the subject and tell us what you'd like us to do.

**Verification.** To protect your information, we'll confirm the request comes from you, usually by replying to the email address you used to contact us and asking you to confirm. We'll ask only for what we need to match you to the information we hold, and we'll use it only to verify the request.

**Authorized agents.** You can have someone else make a request for you. We'll ask the agent for your signed permission, and we may ask you to confirm your identity with us directly.

**Response time.** We'll confirm we received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to another 45 days), we'll tell you why. We may keep some information if the law allows or requires it, such as partner billing records, and we'll tell you if we do.

### How Long We Keep Information

- **Emails from readers:** kept as long as needed to answer you and any follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.
- **Emails and records for haulers and charities:** kept while you're listed or a partner, and afterward only as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting duties.
- **IP address logs:** kept by GitHub under its own policies; we don't receive or store them.

### Do Not Track (CalOPPA)

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There's no common standard for DNT, so our site doesn't change its behavior when it receives one. That said, we don't track you across other websites over time, and we don't allow third parties to collect personal information about your online activities over time and across websites through our site. We honor Global Privacy Control as described above.

### Shine the Light (Cal. Civ. Code Section 1798.83)

We don't disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes. If you have questions, email [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com).

## Children

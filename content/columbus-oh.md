# Junk disposal in Columbus, OH: mattresses, couches, furniture, appliances, TVs & electronics, paint & hazardous waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Columbus, item by item. Every option links to the official city or county page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call your city's service line.

## Mattresses and box springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** If you live in the City of Columbus, the city will pick up a mattress **for free**, but only if you **schedule it first** through 311 at (614) 645-3111 or online at 311.columbus.gov. If you can haul it yourself, you can also drop it off for free at one of two city convenience centers (Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 5).

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by step: free city pickup

1. Call **311 / (614) 645-3111** or book online at 311.columbus.gov. The city says nothing is collected without a booking.
2. On your scheduled day only, put the mattress in front of your home, clear of obstructions. If your trash is collected in the alley, put it at the alley line instead.
3. Bags, boxes, and tubs of loose stuff don't count as bulk. Those go in your regular trash container.

### What the city won't take with bulk pickup

Refrigerated appliances (fridges, freezers, dehumidifiers, AC units), furnaces, pianos, hot tubs and spas, pool tables, tires and auto parts, building materials, and hazardous materials. The city also won't collect items from an eviction, set-out, or foreclosure. For household hazardous waste, contact SWACO at (614) 871-5100.

### Live outside city limits?

Suburbs and townships around Columbus run their own trash contracts, so check your own city or township's waste page. *(Suburb-by-suburb section: to be researched.)*

### Need it gone today?

If you can't wait for a scheduled pickup, a paid junk removal service is the fallback. *(Paid options will be added once they're verified.)*

### Reporting illegal dumping

Report it through 311, or call the Franklin County Environmental Justice Program at (614) 871-5322.

## Couches and furniture {#furniture}

The City's free scheduled bulk collection takes **furniture** (leave it assembled; tape any glass). Schedule through **311 / (614) 645-3111 or 311.columbus.gov**. Nothing is collected unless it's scheduled. You can also drop furniture free at the Alum Creek or Georgesville Waste and Reuse Convenience Centers (Tue–Sat, 9–5). ([Columbus Bulk Collection](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/Bulk-Collection))

## Appliances (fridges, freezers, AC: freon) {#appliances}

City bulk pickup takes **non-refrigerant appliances** (e.g. dishwashers, washing machines). It does **not** take refrigerated appliances (refrigerators, freezers, dehumidifiers, heat pumps, water coolers, air conditioners), furnaces, pianos, spas or hot tubs. ([Columbus Bulk Collection](https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Trash-Recycling-Bulk-Collection/Bulk-Collection))

For freon appliances, SWACO (the county solid waste authority) says its **Recycling Convenience Center (2566 Jackson Pike)** accepts freon-containing appliances such as refrigerators, AC units and dehumidifiers. Its **HHW Facility (645 E. 8th Ave.)** also accepts scrap metal and appliances, including appliances with freon. Franklin County residents only. Check SWACO for any fees. ([SWACO Recycling Convenience Center](https://www.swaco.org/disposal/facilities/recycling-convenience-center/))

## TVs, computers and electronics (e-waste) {#electronics}

SWACO's **Recycling Convenience Center, 2566 Jackson Pike** (Mon–Fri 9:30–5, Sat 9–4), accepts household electronics, flat and CRT monitors, and TVs for Franklin County residents. SWACO lists fees for some items: **LCD TV $15; CRTs $25 (under 20"), $35 (under 35"), $45 (over 35")**, with the note "fees subject to change". Credit, debit or check only. ([SWACO Recycling Convenience Center](https://www.swaco.org/disposal/facilities/recycling-convenience-center/))

## Paint and household hazardous waste {#hazardous}

SWACO's **Household Hazardous Waste Facility, 645 E. 8th Ave.** (Mon–Fri 9–5, first Saturday 9–2; 614-294-1300) takes most HHW free for Franklin County residents: oil-based paint, spray paint, stains, thinners, cleaners, pesticides, auto fluids, batteries, propane tanks, CFLs and more. **Latex and other water-based paints are not part of the free program.** SWACO says they can be disposed of for a fee through EEI, or hardened (paint hardener, cat litter or sawdust) and thrown in regular trash once dry. SWACO's Recycling Convenience Center also lists latex, oil-based and spray paint among accepted items. ([SWACO HHW](https://www.swaco.org/diversion/residents/household-hazardous-waste/))

*Rules, hours and fees change. Confirm on the linked official page before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

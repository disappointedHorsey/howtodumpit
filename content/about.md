# About HowToDumpIt and How We Check Facts

HowToDumpIt is an independent, free guide to getting rid of bulky junk legally and cheaply in US cities. We answer one question per page: how do I get rid of this item in my city, and what are my options? We don't haul anything, and we aren't part of any city government.

## How We Work {#who}

Every city rule, date and fee on a page has to come from an official source linked on that page. Questions and corrections go to [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com).

## Where Our Facts Come From {#sources}

- **Official sources only.** City rules, collection schedules, drop-off sites, hours and fees come from city, county or solid waste authority pages, or from the city's own contracted collector. Each fact links to the page it came from.
- **No guessing.** If we can't confirm something on an official page, we leave it out or say so plainly and point you to the official page or 311. We never invent fees, phone numbers, addresses or dates.
- **Seasons we can't confirm yet.** When a city hasn't published this season's dates, as with many Christmas tree programs in November, we show the latest published season, label it as last season, and link the official page.
- **City limits.** A city guide covers the rules inside that city's limits. Where a county or neighboring city has different rules, we say so and link its own page.

## What "Last Verified" Means {#last-verified}

The Last verified date on a page is the day we last checked its facts against the linked official pages. Rules, hours and fees can change after that date, so always confirm on the official page before you set something out or drive anywhere. We recheck seasonal pages, such as Christmas tree pages, around the time cities usually publish new dates, and we fix errors as soon as we learn about them.

## How Local Junk Removal Listings Work {#listings}

- **Local first.** City pages feature local junk removal companies and genuine nonprofits that serve that city. Before we list a company, we confirm on its own website that it serves the city.
- **Free listings are marked Not verified.** A free listing shows the company name and a phone number only. Not verified means the company hasn't confirmed its details with us. It is not a review or an endorsement.
- **Paid placements are labeled.** A Recommended listing is a paid placement, and the card says so: "Paid placement. We receive referral fees from this partner." Paid placement never changes the official rules, dates or fees we report.
- **National companies stay in small print.** National chains, retailers and other big commercial businesses appear only in a small "Other options" note at the bottom of a city page.
- **We don't vouch for anyone's work.** Listed companies are independent businesses. Check their pricing, licensing and reviews before you hire.

## Affiliate Links and Privacy {#money}

Some links are affiliate links and are marked sponsored. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We count visits and taps on call buttons with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that uses no cookies. Details are in our [Privacy &amp; Cookie Policy](/privacy/).

## Corrections {#corrections}

If you spot a wrong date, fee or rule, or a listing that no longer serves your city, email [adam@howtodumpit.com](mailto:adam@howtodumpit.com) with the page link and the official source. We check it and update the page.

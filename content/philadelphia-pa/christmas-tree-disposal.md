# Christmas Tree Recycling in Philadelphia, PA (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** You can put a live tree out at the curb, but the City takes curbside trees as trash, not compost. To recycle it, bring the bare, untied tree to a City drop-off during the Christmas Tree Recycling Program (2026-27 dates not posted when we checked on November 25, 2026; last season ran January 5-17, 2026) or to one of six sanitation convenience centers. If you'd rather not haul it yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of Philadelphia Department of Sanitation pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Department of Sanitation Christmas Tree Recycling Program {#tree-recycling}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 25, 2026). The City's page still shows last season. ([City of Philadelphia: Dispose of Christmas Trees](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/dispose-of-christmas-trees/))
- **Last season (2025-26):** Monday, January 5, 2026, through Saturday, January 17, 2026, at designated drop-off locations and the sanitation convenience centers, shown on the City's map.
- **Prep:** trees must be untied, with no decorations. Recycled trees go to a vendor that chips them into a bulking agent for composting.
- **The catch:** there's no curbside recycling. A tree left at the curb is collected as trash.

**Other options on the City's page:** last season the City also listed community tree collection events and private pickup services at **$20 per tree** (donation or fee), each with its own dates and areas. Those dates have passed; confirm any 2026-27 event with the group before you go.

## Drop It Off Yourself: Sanitation Convenience Centers {#drop-off}

All six **sanitation convenience centers** list Christmas trees among their accepted materials. They're open **Monday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.**, closed City holidays. Bring proof that you live in Philadelphia; vehicles must weigh under 6,000 pounds, and commercial loads aren't allowed. Use the City's center finder for the nearest address. ([City of Philadelphia: Find a Sanitation Convenience Center](https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/find-a-sanitation-convenience-center-to-drop-off-trash-or-recycling/))

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

The City doesn't collect trash or recycling on City holidays, and collection runs **one day behind schedule for the rest of the week** after a holiday. ([City of Philadelphia: City Holidays and Closures](https://www.phila.gov/city-holidays-and-closures/)) Christmas Day 2026 and New Year's Day 2027 both fall on a Friday. The City had not posted its 2026 Christmas-week collection notice as of November 25, 2026; check the Department of Sanitation page that week.

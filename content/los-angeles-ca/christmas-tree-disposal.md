# Christmas Tree Pickup in Los Angeles, CA (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** If LA Sanitation and Environment (LASAN) collects your trash, a bare real tree goes in or next to your green bin on your regular trash day; last season that ran until January 31, 2026, and trees over 6 feet needed a bulky item pickup. LASAN had not posted 2026-27 dates when we checked on November 18, 2026. If you want the tree gone sooner, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 18, 2026, against official LA Sanitation and Environment (LASAN) and City of Los Angeles pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: LASAN Christmas Tree Recycling {#tree-collection}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 18, 2026). LASAN's tree page still shows last season's dates. ([LASAN: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://sanitation.lacity.gov/services/christmas-tree-recycling))
- **Rules on that page:** remove all ornaments, tinsel, lights, decorations and tree stands; only bare, real trees are recycled. Flocked (fake-snow) and artificial trees can't be recycled: cut them up and put them in your black trash bin. ([LASAN: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://sanitation.lacity.gov/services/christmas-tree-recycling))
- **Last season (2025-26) curbside:** trees were collected on your regular trash day until January 31. Cut the tree to fit inside your green bin with the lid closed, or set the whole bare tree at the curb next to the green bin. ([LASAN: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://sanitation.lacity.gov/services/christmas-tree-recycling))
- **Big or late trees:** if the tree is taller than 6 feet, or you miss the January 31 deadline, schedule a bulky item pickup through the 24-hour Customer Care Center (1-800-773-2489) or the MyLA311 app. ([LASAN: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://sanitation.lacity.gov/services/christmas-tree-recycling))
- **The catch:** last season's curbside window closed January 31, trees over 6 feet needed a booked bulky pickup, and flocked or artificial trees can't be recycled at all.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When does LA pick up Christmas trees?

On your regular trash day after the holidays, if LASAN collects your trash. Last season that ran until January 31, 2026. LASAN had not posted 2026-27 dates as of November 18, 2026.

### Can I put my Christmas tree in the green bin in Los Angeles?

Yes, if it's bare and real. Cut it to fit with the lid closed, or set the whole tree next to the green bin.

### What if my Christmas tree is taller than 6 feet?

LASAN says to schedule a bulky item pickup through MyLA311 or 1-800-773-2489.

### Can I recycle a flocked or artificial tree in LA?

No. LASAN says to cut flocked and artificial trees up and put them in the black trash bin.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Los Angeles right now?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

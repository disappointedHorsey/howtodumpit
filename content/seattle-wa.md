## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** Seattle Public Utilities (SPU) will pick up a mattress or box spring through **Special Item Pickup** for **$31 per item** (SPU's posted price on October 12, 2026), charged to your utility bill. The **utility account holder** schedules it online or at **(206) 684-3000**. Renters go through their landlord.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step (Single-Family Homes)

1. Log into your SPU account and choose **Special Item Pickup**, or call (206) 684-3000.
2. SPU collects on your **next regular garbage day** after you book.
3. Set the mattress where your garbage is normally collected, on flat ground, easily reached from the street, **by 7:00 a.m.** Cover it if it's raining.
4. Don't leave items outside until your pickup is confirmed.

### Apartments and Condos

If your building has 5+ units, the property manager or owner has to book the pickup and agree on a set-out spot the driver can reach without keys or fobs.

### Other Options

SPU's "Where Does It Go?" tool lists reuse and donation options for items in good condition. If you can't wait, use one of the local junk removal companies listed above.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

Sofas, chairs, tables, dressers, cabinets and other furniture larger than 2'×2'×2' are picked up through **SPU Special Item Pickup** at **$31 per item** (SPU price on October 12, 2026), billed to the utility account. Limits: 300 lbs, 8 ft tall, 4 ft diameter. Schedule it the same way as a mattress. ([SPU Special Item Pickup](https://www.seattle.gov/utilities/your-services/collection-and-disposal/garbage/special-item-collections))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, washers, dryers, dishwashers and ACs are **$31 per item** through Special Item Pickup. **Items containing CFCs, such as refrigerators and freezers made before 2005, are $39.25 per item.** Remove latched doors, or secure them tightly, and cover items on rainy days. ([SPU Special Item Pickup](https://www.seattle.gov/utilities/your-services/collection-and-disposal/garbage/special-item-collections))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

TVs, monitors and computers larger than 2'×2'×2' are **$31 per item** via Special Item Pickup. Small electronics (phones, tablets, keyboards) can go in a **Special Items Box** (one free per year, then $20). For other options, SPU points to its "Where Does It Go?" tool. ([SPU Special Item Pickup](https://www.seattle.gov/utilities/your-services/collection-and-disposal/garbage/special-item-collections))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Seattle's two HHW facilities are **free for all King County residents, no appointment**: **North, 12550 Stone Ave N** (Sun–Tue 9–5) and **South, 8100 2nd Ave S** (Thu–Sat 9–5). Seattle **transfer stations do not accept** HHW. Household Hazards Line: (206) 296-4692. ([SPU: Where to Dispose of HHW](https://seattle.gov/utilities/your-services/collection-and-disposal/garbage/hazardous-waste-items/where-to-dispose-of-hazardous-waste))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** Seattle Public Utilities (SPU) offers free Christmas tree composting at the curb and at transfer stations during a posted January window. 2026-27 dates were not published as of November 10, 2026.

**Last season (2025-26):** An official SPU blog post dated December 15, 2025, said customers, including apartment and condo residents, could compost trees and other holiday greens for free through January 31. Starting February 1, extra yard-waste fees applied. Curbside: place up to 2 undecorated trees or bundled greens next to your food and yard waste cart on your regular collection day. Cut trees into 4-foot lengths or shorter. Remove all decorations, lights, tinsel, ornaments, metal clips, flocking and wiring. Bundle branches with sisal string or twine, not plastic. Trunk diameter may not exceed 4 inches. At apartment and condo buildings, two trees may be placed next to each food and yard waste cart at no extra charge each collection day; additional trees were picked up as extra yard waste with fees. ([SPU: Give Your Christmas Tree a Second Life](https://atyourservice.seattle.gov/2025/12/15/give-your-christmas-tree-a-second-life-compost-it-for-free-this-january/))

**Transfer stations (last season):** Drop-off at Seattle's North or South Transfer Station was free through January 31, up to 3 trees per vehicle, each no more than 8 feet long and with trunks no more than 4 inches in diameter, with decorations, flocking and wiring removed.

If you missed that window, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

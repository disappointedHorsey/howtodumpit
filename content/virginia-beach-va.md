# Junk Disposal in Virginia Beach, VA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in the **City of Virginia Beach**, Virginia, item by item. If your home receives City curbside services, you can book a **bulky/large item pickup** through **VB311**. Residents can also drive items to the City's **Landfill & Resource Recovery Center**, which recycles electronics and metal and takes household hazardous waste for free. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call Waste Management customer service at **757-385-4650** or email **wastemgt@vbgov.com**.

**City of Virginia Beach only.** Virginia Beach is an independent city, so no separate county rules apply inside city limits. Norfolk, Chesapeake and other Hampton Roads cities run their own trash service, so their rules differ (see Outside City Limits below).

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | City bulky/large item pickup (book through VB311), or the City Landfill |
| Couch / furniture | City bulky/large item pickup, or donate it if it's in good shape |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Bulky pickup (empty, shelves and doors removed), or free drop-off at the City HHW facility |
| TV / computer | Free e-cycling at the Resource Recovery Center (no tube TVs or monitors); not curbside |
| Paint / chemicals | Free household hazardous waste drop-off at the City Landfill; never liquid paint in your cart |
| Christmas tree | Curbside as regular yard waste on your collection day, or the City Landfill for mulch |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Book a **bulky/large item pickup** if your home receives City curbside services, or take it to the **Landfill & Resource Recovery Center** yourself. ([City of Virginia Beach: Bulky/Large Item Pickup](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/curbside-services/bulky-large-item-pickup))

### Step by Step: Bulky/Large Item Pickup

1. **Book it.** Use [VB311](https://vb311.virginiabeach.gov/service-navigation?c__service_code=BULKPKSW) online (the City's preferred way), call 757-385-4650, email wastemgt@vbgov.com, or go in person to 3024 Holland Road. The City asks you to gather several items into one request instead of booking single items.
2. **Book early.** The bulky pickup page says to request at least **three days** before your regular weekly trash day, but the City's main Trash & Recycling page says **two weeks or more**. Book two weeks ahead to be safe. ([Trash & Recycling](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling))
3. **Prepare it.** All beds must be taken apart, and waterbeds must be drained.
4. **Set it out** between 5 p.m. the day before collection and 7 a.m. on collection day, with at least three feet of clearance on all sides (from carts, yard waste, poles, mailboxes and vehicles). Don't put it in the street, on the sidewalk, or under power lines or low tree limbs.
5. **Clean up.** Small debris left after the pickup goes in your black trash cart.

**Mattresses by name:** The City's bulky page covers beds but does not list mattresses or box springs by name, and they are not on its not-eligible list. Mention the mattress when you book.

**Cost:** The bulky page lists no separate charge. City curbside services are funded by the **Waste Collection and Disposal fee, $30.55 a month** on the City Services Bill. ([Customer Service](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/customer-service))

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask customer service before you set one out.

### Drop It Off Yourself

The **Landfill & Resource Recovery Center**, 1989 Jake Sears Road, is open to Virginia Beach residents Tuesday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (757-385-1980). The City's main Trash & Recycling page gives the address as 1985 Jake Sears Road, so use either for directions. ([Landfill & Resource Recovery Center](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/landfill-and-rrc))

- **Proof of residency:** a valid Virginia driver's license showing your primary Virginia Beach address, or a photo ID plus a recent utility bill, rent receipt or check draft in the same name. Digital ID isn't accepted, and the resident must be there.
- **Vehicles:** only residential vehicles (cars, minivans, passenger vans, unmodified low-sided pickups). No ladder racks, commercial signage, cargo or panel vans. Trailers must be under 10 feet with no dump bed.
- **Unloading:** everything goes into the convenience center containers, which you must lift items over, so bring someone to help.
- **Delays and closures:** the City warns that an equipment shortage may delay service, and the facility closes in bad weather. City offices and the Landfill are closed on Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, chairs, tables and other furniture** go out with a **bulky/large item pickup**, booked and set out the same way as a mattress. For **patio furniture**, remove the glass first; furniture with attached cement or glass isn't collected. ([Bulky/Large Item Pickup](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/curbside-services/bulky-large-item-pickup)) **Not eligible** for bulky pickup: building and renovation materials, carpet, cabinets, doors, sinks, toilets, glass, wood pallets, cardboard boxes, bags, tires, paint, propane tanks, hazardous waste, boats, RVs, vehicle parts and computer towers. Call 757-385-4650 if you're unsure.

**Donate first:** The City's alternative disposal page points to CHKD Thrift Stores, Goodwill, the Salvation Army and Samaritan House for usable household items (listed for information only, not as endorsements). ([Alternative Disposal Options](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/alternative-disposal-options))

**Apartments and condos:** Bulky pickup is only for residents who **receive City curbside services**. If your apartment or condo community uses a private hauler or shared dumpsters, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled. As a Virginia Beach resident, you can still use the City Landfill with proof of residency.

**Neighborhoods:** We found no difference in bulky rules between parts of the city, from the Oceanfront to Pungo. What matters is whether your home receives City curbside services.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Virginia Beach** only, and the City Landfill is for Virginia Beach residents only. **Chesapeake** residents served by the City's Waste Management Division book bulk pickup by calling 757-382-CITY (2489) or online by the night before their collection day; household bulk is limited to 12 collections a year, and hazardous waste goes to a regional SPSA transfer station. ([City of Chesapeake: Bulk Trash Collection](https://www.cityofchesapeake.net/1069/Bulk-Trash-Collection)) **Norfolk** residents must schedule bulk pickup through MyNorfolk or Norfolk Cares at (757) 664-6510 by 3 p.m. the day before collection. ([City of Norfolk: Trash & Recycling](https://www.norfolk.gov/2734/Trash-Recycling)) Other Hampton Roads cities set their own rules, so check your city's website. If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Curbside:** Appliances can go out with a **bulky/large item pickup**. Refrigerators and freezers must be **empty with all shelves and doors removed**. For fridges, freezers and air conditioners (window, wall or condenser units), tell the City **whether the freon/refrigerant has been removed** when you book. Water heaters must be drained. ([Bulky/Large Item Pickup](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/curbside-services/bulky-large-item-pickup))

**Drop-off:** The City's **household hazardous waste** facility at the Landfill takes appliances containing freon (refrigerators, freezers, AC condensers and window air conditioners) free from Virginia Beach homes. The **Resource Recovery Center** recycles stainless steel appliances and other scrap metal for free. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/landfill-and-rrc/household-hazardous-waste); [Resource Recovery Center](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/landfill-and-rrc/resource-recovery-center))

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Not curbside:** Computer towers are on the bulky pickup not-eligible list. Instead, Virginia Beach residents can recycle electronics for free at the **Resource Recovery Center** at the Landfill: televisions, computers, monitors, laptops, tablets, cell phones, DVD players, radios and more. **Tube (CRT) TVs and monitors are not accepted.** ([Resource Recovery Center](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/landfill-and-rrc/resource-recovery-center))

Delete your personal data and remove batteries before you recycle; rechargeable and lithium batteries go to the Landfill's hazardous waste area, not the trash. The City also suggests manufacturer and retailer take-back programs. ([Alternative Disposal Options](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/alternative-disposal-options)) Last season the City promoted a free drive-through **E-Cycling event** at the Virginia Aquarium (January 10, 2026); 2027 details were not posted when we checked. ([City of Virginia Beach: Holiday Disposal Tips](https://virginiabeach.gov/connect/blog/how-to-properly-dispose-of-holiday-items))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Never** put liquid paint or chemicals in your black cart or out for bulky pickup. Virginia Beach residents can drop household hazardous waste off **free** at the **HHW facility** inside the Landfill & Resource Recovery Center, 1989 Jake Sears Road, Tuesday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Tell the gate attendant you have HHW. ([Household Hazardous Waste](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/landfill-and-rrc/household-hazardous-waste))

- **Limit:** five gallons of liquid or 75 pounds of solid waste per visit, from your primary Virginia Beach residence only. The same residency and vehicle rules apply as for the Landfill.
- **Accepted:** oil or enamel-based paint, stains and finishes, thinners and strippers, motor oil, antifreeze and gasoline, automotive and rechargeable batteries, cleaners and pool chemicals, pesticides and fertilizer, mercury thermostats and compact fluorescent bulbs, propane tanks and aerosol cans.
- **Dried paint:** The City's HHW page says acrylic, latex and oil-based paint can go in the trash once it's fully dried out with a paint solidifier (such as kitty litter or shredded paper), bagged and placed in your black cart. The City's trash collection page lists paint cans among items not allowed in the black cart, so ask customer service at 757-385-4650 if you're unsure. ([Trash Collection](https://pw.virginiabeach.gov/trash-recycling/curbside-services/trash-collection))

Questions about the Landfill or HHW? Call **757-385-1980**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City says Christmas trees are collected as **regular yard waste on your normal collection day**. Set the tree at the curb free of its stand, tinsel, lights and ornaments. You can also take a bare tree to the **Landfill & Resource Recovery Center**, where trees are chipped into mulch for City landscape crews. ([City of Virginia Beach: Holiday Disposal Tips](https://virginiabeach.gov/connect/blog/how-to-properly-dispose-of-holiday-items))

**Last season (2025-26):** The Virginia Aquarium also collected bare, live-cut trees on **January 10, 2026** only, in its East Parking Lot during the E-Cycling event, with free advance registration encouraged. The Landfill was closed on January 1. 2026-27 event dates were not posted when we checked.

If you can't get the tree to the curb, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. See our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide for other cities.

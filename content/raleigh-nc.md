# Junk Disposal in Raleigh, NC: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Raleigh, North Carolina, item by item. If you get **City of Raleigh curbside garbage**, you can schedule a paid **Bulky Load** for mattresses and furniture (**$35 per load**), a paid **Special Load** for appliances and similar items (**$70 per load**), and free **E-waste Collection** (up to four items per week, Mondays only). **Wake County** also runs **Convenience Centers**, **Multi-Material Recycling Facilities** and **Household Hazardous Waste** sites for County residents. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call City Solid Waste Customer Support at **919-996-3245** or Wake County Solid Waste at **919-856-7400** before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | City Bulky Load ($35 per load), or Wake County Convenience Centers (up to 2 mattresses/box springs per day at listed sites) |
| Couch / furniture | City Bulky Load ($35 per load), or Wake County Convenience Centers (bulky items under 8 feet) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | City Special Load ($70 per load), or free drop-off at a Wake County Multi-Material Recycling Facility |
| TV / computer | Free City E-waste Collection (Mondays only, up to 4 items/week), or Wake County Multi-Material Recycling Facilities; TVs and computers are banned from NC landfills |
| Paint / chemicals | Wake County Household Hazardous Waste facilities (Wake County residents only; latex paint can also be dried with kitty litter for household trash) |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Mattresses and box springs are on the City's **Bulky Load** accepted list. Schedule by calling **919-996-3245**. A single Bulky Load is about **four cubic yards** (roughly three queen-sized mattresses or two couches) and costs **$35 per load** (effective April 1, 2026), with **no limit** on the number of qualifying loads. Loads are charged in full-load increments; partial loads are not prorated. ([City of Raleigh: Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection); [Solid Waste Fees](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/solid-waste-fees))

### Step by Step: City Bulky Load

1. **Confirm eligibility.** You must receive City of Raleigh curbside garbage collection. Businesses and commercial entities are not eligible. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))
2. **Call to schedule.** Call **919-996-3245** with your name, address, phone and a description of the items. The City bills the charge on your next utility bill.
3. **Place it right on the scheduled day only.** Put items at the curb on a flat area (not in the street), **five feet** from mailboxes, light poles, cars and other obstructions, on the **main street** in front of the home (not alleyways). Townhome residents without a front driveway should place items at the end of their parking space with the same five-foot clearance. Items placed at the curb **before** you schedule are subject to City Code fines.
4. **Cancel in time for a refund.** Cancel by **noon on the business day before** the scheduled collection to be eligible for a refund.

**Not on Bulky Load:** yard waste, recyclables, cardboard, e-waste, extra garbage, construction/demolition debris, hazardous waste, or bagged items small enough for the garbage cart. Appliances go on a **Special Load** instead. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

**Self-haul:** Wake County residents may drop off **up to two mattresses or box springs per day** at Convenience Center **Sites 1, 2, 4, 7 and 11** (residency verification required). Additional mattresses and box springs may go to the shredder at the **South Wake Landfill** for a processing fee (confirm the fee before you drive). Centers are open daily **7 a.m. to 7 p.m.** with listed holiday closures. ([Wake County: Convenience Centers](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/convenience-centers))

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City of Raleigh bed-bug wrapping rule on the Bulky Load page. Ask Customer Support at **919-996-3245** when you schedule if your mattress has bed bugs.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Bulky Load:** Furniture (couches, love seats, recliners and similar household items that do not fit in a City garbage cart) is accepted on a scheduled **Bulky Load** for City garbage customers. Cost is **$35 per load** (about four cubic yards). Schedule and set-out rules are the same as for mattresses above. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

**Special Load (when it applies):** Some large items that are not eligible for Bulky Load (for example exercise equipment, excess bags of garbage, or excess yard waste) can go on a **Special Load** at **$70 per load** (also about four cubic yards). Carpeting is on the Special Load "not accepted" list. Call **919-996-3245** so staff can assign the right service. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

**Self-haul:** Wake County Convenience Centers accept household garbage and bulky items **under 8 feet** long or wide for Wake County residents. Appliances, electronics and paint are redirected to other County facilities (see below). ([Wake County: Convenience Centers](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/convenience-centers))

**Never collected by the City (examples):** construction/demolition debris (toilets, sinks, flooring, cabinets, etc.), tires, car parts, batteries, glass, windows, mirrors, tree stumps, and contractor materials. Use a private hauler or the appropriate Wake County facility. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers **Raleigh city limits** and residents who get City of Raleigh curbside garbage. Neighboring towns run their own bulk programs: **Cary** schedules bulky trash by appointment (**$15** for the first item and **$8** for each additional item on the same trip; call 311) ([Town of Cary: Garbage Collection](https://www.carync.gov/services-publications/garbage-recycling-yard-waste/garbage-collection)); **Apex** offers fee-based Bulk Item Collection (not for businesses or apartments; mattresses and box springs on Fridays; **$30** trip fee for 1–2 items, then **$15** per item for 3+, with mattresses/box springs at **$15** each on top of the trip fee) via Ask Apex at **919-249-3311** ([Town of Apex: Bulk Item Collection](https://www.apexnc.org/279/Bulk-Item-Collection)). Unincorporated Wake County and other Wake towns generally use **Wake County Convenience Centers**, **Multi-Material Recycling Facilities** and **HHW** sites (proof of Wake County residency required). If you live in an apartment, HOA or complex with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager how bulky items are handled — City Bulky/Special/E-waste collection is for residents who receive City curbside garbage.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Not on Bulky Load:** White goods such as refrigerators, mini-refrigerators, ranges, freezers, washers and dryers go on a **Special Load**, not a Bulky Load. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

**City Special Load:** Schedule by calling **919-996-3245**. A Special Load is about **four cubic yards** (roughly four standard washers or two refrigerators) and costs **$70 per load**, charged in full-load increments. Cancel by noon the business day before for a refund. The City page we checked does **not** publish a separate freon surcharge or door-removal rule for Special Load appliances — ask Customer Support when you book. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection); [Solid Waste Fees](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/solid-waste-fees))

**Wake County Multi-Material Recycling Facilities:** Wake County residents and businesses may drop off large appliances including **air conditioners, dishwashers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ovens, washing machines and water heaters** at the County's Multi-Material sites. North Wake (9029 Deponie Drive, Raleigh) and South Wake (6130 Old Smithfield Road, Apex) are open **Monday–Saturday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.**; East Wake (5051 Wendell Boulevard, Wendell) is open **Saturday and Sunday only, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.** (Wake County residents only at East Wake). ([Wake County: Multi-Material Recycling Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/multi-material-recycling-facilities))

**Freon / refrigerants:** North Carolina bans **white goods** from landfill disposal. ([NC DEQ: Items Banned from Disposal in Landfills](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/waste-management/solid-waste-section/compliance-and-monitoring/items-banned-disposal-landfills)) Wake County HHW facilities list **Freon/refrigerants** as **not accepted** there — use Special Load or a Multi-Material facility for freon appliances instead. ([Wake County: Household Hazardous Waste Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/household-hazardous-waste-facilities)) Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer is responsible for recovering refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

In North Carolina, **discarded televisions and computer equipment** (computers, monitors, video displays, printers, scanners and printer-scanner-fax devices; keyboards and mice are excluded) are **banned from landfill disposal**. ([NC DEQ: Electronics Management](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/waste-management/solid-waste-section/special-wastes-and-alternative-handling/electronics-management); [Items Banned from Disposal in Landfills](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/waste-management/solid-waste-section/compliance-and-monitoring/items-banned-disposal-landfills))

**City E-waste Collection (free):** City of Raleigh residents who get curbside garbage can request free curbside **E-waste Collection** for up to **four items per week**. Effective **October 26, 2026**, collection is on **Mondays only**. Accepted examples include computer monitors, TVs, toasters, microwaves, lamps and vacuum cleaners (specific small household appliances with a plug and electronic devices). Call **919-996-3245** to schedule. Additional items beyond four will not be collected. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

**Wake County Multi-Material Recycling Facilities:** Electronics (TVs, cell phones, computers, monitors, game consoles, stereos, microwaves and any corded device) are accepted at the Multi-Material sites listed in the appliances section. Some Convenience Centers (Sites 1, 4 and 11) also take electronics on **weekends only**. ([Wake County: Multi-Material Recycling Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/multi-material-recycling-facilities); [Convenience Centers](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/convenience-centers))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you recycle them.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Wake County HHW facilities** accept household hazardous waste from **Wake County residents only** (no business waste). Proof of residency is required. Stay in your vehicle. ([Wake County: Household Hazardous Waste Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/household-hazardous-waste-facilities))

| Site | Address | Hours (Wake County residents) |
|---|---|---|
| North Wake HHW | 9037 Deponie Drive, Raleigh, NC 27614 | Saturdays only, 8 a.m.–4 p.m. **Service update:** the facility is temporarily closed due to fire damage; in the interim Wake County holds an HHW collection event every Saturday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m., at the North Wake Facility |
| South Wake HHW | 6150 Old Smithfield Road, Apex, NC 27539 | Monday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m. |
| East Wake HHW | 5051 Wendell Blvd., Wendell, NC 27591 | Saturdays and Sundays only, 8 a.m.–4 p.m. |

Closed New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve (at noon) and Christmas Day. General inquiries: **919-856-7400**.

**Paint:** Latex paint (10-gallon limit; consolidate cans if more than 10 gallons), oil-based paint, thinners, stains and related products are accepted. Rusted or dried-out paint cans should go in regular trash. Wake County encourages residents to **dry latex paint with kitty litter** and dispose of dried paint in regular trash. Small amounts of latex paint, latex caulk, spackle, putty or grout can also go in household garbage. ([Wake County: Household Hazardous Waste Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/household-hazardous-waste-facilities))

**Also accepted (limits apply):** household and rechargeable batteries, lead-acid vehicle batteries (limit 6), aerosols, fluorescent bulbs/tubes (limit 12, boxed), fire extinguishers (household size), propane tanks up to 20 lb, pool and garden chemicals, and more on the County list.

**Not accepted at HHW (examples):** freon/refrigerants, business waste, ammunition/explosives, lithium iron phosphate car/generator batteries, medical waste, smoke detectors (return to manufacturer or contact NC Radiation Protection at **919-814-2250**), and spray foam tanks. Antifreeze, motor oil and oil filters go to **Multi-Material Recycling Facilities**, not HHW. ([Wake County: Household Hazardous Waste Facilities](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/solid-waste-management/facilities/household-hazardous-waste-facilities))

The City does **not** collect hazardous waste on Bulky or Special Loads. ([Bulky, Special, and E-Waste Collection](https://raleighnc.gov/landfill-and-reuse/services/bulky-special-and-e-waste-collection))

Questions? Call City Solid Waste Customer Support at **919-996-3245** (email **sws@raleighnc.gov**) or Wake County Solid Waste at **919-856-7400**.

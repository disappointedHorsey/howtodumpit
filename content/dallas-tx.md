## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}


**Mattress short answer:** Dallas picks up mattresses **monthly** in **brush and bulky item** collection for residential customers. Find your collection week on the City's calendar, then set the mattress out **Thursday-Sunday before** that week, **just behind the curb** in front of your home.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step

1. Find your collection week on the City's [2026 Brush & Bulky calendar](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sanitation/pages/brush_and_bulky.aspx) or call 3-1-1.
2. Set the mattress out **between Thursday and Sunday before** your collection week. Earlier set-outs can be cited.
3. Place it **just behind the curb line in front of your home**. Not beside the house, not in an alley, not on a neighbor's lot.
4. Keep it **5 feet** from carts, mailboxes, fences/walls, water meters, hydrants, poles, signs and parked cars, and out from under low limbs or power lines.

### Watch the Fees

Set-outs over 10 cubic yards (or with prohibited items) are generally collected **and billed at $60 per 5 cubic yards**, and can also be cited. If you have a big clean-out, request the **once-a-year 20 cubic yard exception** through 311 the week before your collection week.

### Need It Gone Today?

Use a paid junk removal service (see above).

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Household furniture** and carpet are accepted in **monthly brush & bulky** collection: up to 10 cubic yards, set out Thursday–Sunday before your collection week, just behind the curb. Same placement rules as the mattress section. ([Dallas Brush & Bulky](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sanitation/pages/brush_and_bulky.aspx))

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Dallas** only. What matters is whether your address is inside Dallas city limits and gets City Sanitation service. **University Park** is a separate city with its own service: special pickups of furniture, appliances and other large items are booked through its 311 line or a service request form and are charged per item ($35.30 for items like a sofa, bed, washer or dryer, and $115.07 for a refrigerator, freezer or AC unit; rates effective October 1, 2026). ([City of University Park: Appliance, Furniture & Large Item Collection](https://www.uptexas.org/275/Appliance-Furniture-Large-Item-Collectio)) **Irving** collects up to four bulky items a week, such as a sofa, table or mattress, on your regular trash day; call (972) 721-8059. ([City of Irving: Brush + Bulky Waste Collection](https://irvingtx.gov/bulky-waste)) Highland Park, Garland, Mesquite, Richardson and other nearby cities set their own rules, so check your city's website. If you live in an apartment, condo or HOA community with a **private hauler**, ask your property manager or HOA first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Stoves, washers, dryers and water heaters are accepted curbside. **Appliances containing coolant, gasoline or other chemicals are NOT**: air conditioners, refrigerators, freezers, dehumidifiers, lawnmowers. We could not verify an official City drop-off for freon appliances. **Call 3-1-1.** ([Dallas Brush & Bulky](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sanitation/pages/brush_and_bulky.aspx))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

Electronics are **not** accepted curbside. Dallas residents can drop off TVs (CRT, flat-screen, console, projection), computers, monitors, laptops, printers, microwaves, game consoles and much more at the **McCommas Bluff Landfill or one of the three Sanitation transfer stations**. See the City's Landfill & Transfer Stations page for hours. ([Dallas Electronics Recycling](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sanitation/Pages/electronic_waste.aspx))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Paint and chemicals are not accepted curbside. Dallas residents get **free** disposal at the Dallas County **Home Chemical Collection Center, 11234 Plano Road** (214-553-1765), or at network collection events. Bring proof of current address. Accepted: paint and home repair products, lawn and garden chemicals, aerosols, pool chemicals, cleaners, batteries, auto fluids, fluorescent tubes, computers and cell phones. Not accepted: appliances, TVs, microwaves, containers over 5 gallons, medicines. The City page lists hours (Tue–Thu plus 2nd and 4th Saturdays), but some of its holiday info is dated, so **call to confirm before you go**. ([Dallas Home Chemical Waste Disposal](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sanitation/Pages/home_chemical.aspx))

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Dallas recycles live, natural Christmas trees at McCommas Bluff Landfill and its transfer stations during a posted holiday window. Trees do not go in the blue recycling cart. 2026-27 dates were not published as of November 10, 2026.

**Last season (2025-26):** From December 26, 2025, through January 24, 2026, Dallas residents could drop off natural trees with all decorations removed at the transfer stations (one tree per household) or at the landfill (multiple trees per household). Do not bag or wrap the tree. Remove all decorations, tinsel and lights. Recycled trees were mulched for on-site road and drainage projects at McCommas Bluff. Trees could also be placed out during scheduled brush and bulky item collection, but the City said trees collected that way generally cannot be separated for recycling. Artificial trees are not recyclable and may go to a landfill or transfer station, or out with brush and bulky items. ([Dallas City News Hub: Christmas Tree Recycling](https://www.dallascitynews.net/christmas-tree-recycling); [City of Dallas Sanitation](https://www.dallas.gov/Sanitation))

Last-season sites: Northwest Transfer Station, 9500 Harry Hines Boulevard; Northeast Transfer Station, 7677 Fair Oaks Avenue; Southwest Transfer Station, 4610 S. Westmoreland Road; and McCommas Bluff Landfill, 5100 Youngblood Road. Confirm hours and the new season's dates on the Sanitation pages before you drive.

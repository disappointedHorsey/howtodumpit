# Junk disposal in Oklahoma City, OK: mattresses, couches, furniture, appliances, TVs & electronics, paint & hazardous waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, item by item. The City picks up **bulky waste once a month** at the curb for single-residential households, and the **first 4 cubic yards** (about the size of two large refrigerators) are **included in your monthly trash bill**. Bigger piles are billed extra per cubic yard. **Fridges, freezers and AC units with freon** need a **scheduled pickup** (call **(405) 297-2833**), and paint and chemicals go to the City's **free Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center**. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call Utilities Customer Service at (405) 297-2833 before you set anything out.

## Quick answer by item

| Item | Best verified option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Monthly curbside bulky pickup, set out loose by 6 a.m. on your bulky day; wrap and label it if it has bed bugs |
| Couch / furniture | Monthly curbside bulky pickup (first 4 cubic yards included in your bill); or a Free Landfill Day (twice a year, typically May and September) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Remove all doors, then call (405) 297-2833 to schedule pickup during your regular bulky window |
| TV / computer | Not taken at the HHW center or curbside list; the City points TVs to Best Buy (fee required) and computers to Best Buy, Staples or Goodwill; also the City's spring Special Collection for computers |
| Paint / chemicals | Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center, 1621 S. Portland Ave., free to Oklahoma City residents (bring your City water bill); latex paint can also be dried out and put in the trash |

## Mattresses and box springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Put it out with your **monthly bulky waste**, loose at the curb, **by 6 a.m. on your assigned bulky day** and **no more than three days early**. If it has **bed bugs** or **biohazardous substances**, **wrap it in plastic** and write or paint **"bed bugs"** or **"biohazard"** on the bag so crews can handle it safely. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))

### Step by step: monthly bulky waste pickup

1. **Find your bulky day.** Use the City's [MyTrashDay](https://www.okc.gov/MyTrashDay) map, log in at okcutilities.com or the My OKC Utilities app, or look at your paper water bill. Bulky routes changed for some neighborhoods in 2026 (Phase 2 began May 1, 2026), so check your current date. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))
2. **Know the window.** Bulky pickup starts the first Monday of each month and runs weekly in two three-day windows: **Monday-Tuesday-Wednesday** and **Wednesday-Thursday-Friday**, starting with your assigned date. Crews may come on any of those days, and may need extra time in busy periods (typically late spring and early fall). Bulky service **doesn't change for holidays**.
3. **Set it out right.** Items go **loose at the curb** with **5 feet of clearance on all sides**, away from trees, power lines, mailboxes, fences, gas meters, vehicles, sidewalks and water meter vaults. **No bagged waste** with bulky items, and don't put piles in the street, driveway, empty lots or unoccupied property.
4. **Watch the size.** The **first 4 cubic yards** are included in your monthly fees. Between 4 and 10 cubic yards, your pile is tagged and the extra is billed on next month's utility bill. Over 10 cubic yards, staff leave a door tag asking you to call Utilities Customer Service to approve the charges. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste)) The City's rate table lists the **excess bulky fee** at **$14.59** (rate from November 2025) and **$15.32** (from November 2026); rates adjust each November. ([Utilities Rates & Fees](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Pay-Bill/Utilities-Rates-Fees))
5. **Missed?** Report it within **two business days** after the end of your collection window: log in at okcutilities.com and use the "Service" tab, or call **(405) 297-2833 Ext. 3** (you'll need your account number). Otherwise you may have to wait until next month. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))

**Live in an apartment?** The curbside bulky program is for single-residential households, and the City treats apartment complexes as commercial properties (their residents can't use Free Landfill Days). Ask your property manager how bulky items are handled. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste), [Free Landfill Day](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Free-Landfill-Day))

### What not to do

- **Don't** set it out more than three days before your bulky day, or after it. Late items may not be collected.
- **Don't** block storm drains, sidewalks or water meters.

## Couches and furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** **Furniture and carpets** go out with your **monthly bulky waste**, using the same set-out rules and 4-cubic-yard allowance as mattresses. The City also collects fencing, small trees and DIY landscape waste (limbs cut to 10 feet or shorter), small DIY home-repair waste (no contractor waste), and glass and mirrors **wrapped in cardboard and labeled "GLASS."** ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))

Bulky pickup **does not** take bagged trash, bricks, concrete or rocks, contractor or remodeling debris, tires and automotive parts, or batteries and chemicals. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))

**Free Landfill Days:** Twice a year (typically **May and September**; the 2026 dates were **Saturday, May 2** and **Saturday, September 12**), City residential trash customers can take **furniture and bulky items**, household trash, non-freon large appliances and bagged leaves to participating landfills **at no cost**: East Oak Landfill (3201 Mosley Rd.), Oklahoma City Landfill (7001 S. Bryant Ave.) and Oklahoma Landfill (7600 SW 15th St.); GFL Landfill in Spencer takes only DIY construction and demolition debris. Bring an **original, current City trash bill** or proof of account in the **My OKC Utilities** app. Renters need a copy of the bill showing trash service plus a valid ID matching the address. The 2027 dates weren't posted when we checked. ([Free Landfill Day](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Free-Landfill-Day))

**Still usable?** The City suggests a local "buy nothing" or give-away group, or checking with your favorite charity to see what it accepts. ([Reuse and Repurpose](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Recycling/Reuse-and-Repurpose))

## Appliances (fridges, freezers, AC: freon) {#appliances}

**Large appliances** are collected with **monthly bulky waste**, but **refrigerators, freezers, AC units and anything else with freon need special handling**:

- **Remove all doors**, per City ordinance.
- **Call the Customer Care Center at (405) 297-2833** to schedule pickup **during your regular bulky waste window**.

([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste)) Utilities Customer Service hours are **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** ([Trash Services](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Trash-Services))

**Freon appliances are not accepted on Free Landfill Days.** Non-freon large appliances are. ([Free Landfill Day](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Free-Landfill-Day))

The City pages we checked don't say whether the freon pickup has an extra fee or whether it counts toward your 4-cubic-yard allowance. Ask when you call (405) 297-2833.

## TVs, computers and electronics (e-waste) {#electronics}

**The City's Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center does not take computer equipment.** ([Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection-Center)) **Electronic waste is not accepted on Free Landfill Days**, though the same page lists televisions among accepted bulky items, so confirm with the City before you bring one. ([Free Landfill Day](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Free-Landfill-Day)) The curbside bulky page doesn't list TVs or electronics either way; check the City's [What Goes Where](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/What-Goes-Where) tool or call (405) 297-2833 before setting a TV at the curb.

**Where the City points you:**

- **Televisions:** Best Buy, with a **fee required**. Check with your metro-area store.
- **Computers, monitors, printers, keyboards and mice:** Best Buy, Staples, and Goodwill (through the Dell Reconnect program), per the City's list.
- **Cell phones and small devices:** the Oklahoma City Zoo's Eco-Cell program (proceeds benefit the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund), Best Buy and most cell phone carriers.

([Reuse and Repurpose](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Recycling/Reuse-and-Repurpose)) Store programs and fees change, so call the store first.

**Spring Special Collection:** Each spring the City holds a **Special Collection** for **computers and peripherals**, tires (no rims), ammunition and medications, for Oklahoma City residents with a current City water bill. **Televisions are not accepted** there. The 2026 event was Saturday, April 11, at OKC Fair Park. ([Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection-Center), [City news, April 1, 2026](https://www.okc.gov/News-articles/OKC-hosts-Special-Collection-for-tires-computers-and-more-April-11))

**State law:** Under the Oklahoma Computer Equipment Recovery Act, manufacturers must offer a **take-back program** for desktop and notebook computers and computer monitors (many by mail). **TVs aren't covered.** ([Oklahoma DEQ: OCERA](https://oklahoma.gov/deq/divisions/land-protection/sust-materials-management/recycling/electronics-recycling/ocera.html)) Oklahoma DEQ also suggests Best Buy or Staples, or a certified (e-Stewards or R2) recycler. ([Oklahoma DEQ: How to Recycle Electronics](https://oklahoma.gov/deq/divisions/land-protection/sust-materials-management/recycling/electronics-recycling/how_to_recycle_electronics.html))

Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and household hazardous waste {#hazardous}

Paints, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, oils, fuels and batteries are **not picked up with bulky waste**. Take them to the City's **Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center**. ([Bulky Waste](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Bulky-Waste))

- **Address:** 1621 S. Portland Ave., Oklahoma City, OK 73108. Phone **(405) 682-7038**.
- **Hours:** **Tuesday to Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.**; **Saturday, 8:30 to 11:30 a.m.** "Swap Shop" hours: Tuesday to Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 4:45 p.m.
- **Cost:** **Free to Oklahoma City residents.** Bring your **City water bill** as proof of residency. Residents of The Village, Yukon, Tinker Air Force Base, Shawnee, El Reno, Edmond, Bethany, Nichols Hills, Warr Acres, Mustang and Moore can also use the center, but may be charged through their own city.
- **Accepted (examples):** latex and oil-based paint, stains, varnishes, paint thinner and stripper, aerosols, cleaners, bleach and ammonia, pesticides and weed killers, fertilizers, pool chemicals, antifreeze, motor oil and filters, gasoline (in containers), propane and helium tanks, batteries of all kinds (including e-cigarette and vape devices), CFL, LED and fluorescent bulbs, mercury thermometers and thermostats, and cooking oil.
- **Not accepted:** ammunition, explosives, radioactive waste, biomedical waste, commercial hazardous waste, high-pressure cylinders, tires, medications and computer equipment.
- **Packing:** Leave products in their **original containers**. Don't pour chemicals together into one container.

All of the above is from the City's [Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection-Center) page.

**Leftover latex paint:** The City says latex (water-based) paint can be taken to the center or, alternatively, **dried out and disposed of with regular trash**. ([Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection-Center))

**Neighborhood events:** HOAs and neighborhood associations can schedule a **Mobile Neighborhood Collection** with the City for the same household hazardous waste the center accepts. Call (405) 682-7038. ([Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection-Center))

**Medications** aren't taken at the center. The City lists the Oklahoma County Sheriff's Department at 201 N. Shartel Ave. and some pharmacies as take-back options. ([Reuse and Repurpose](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Recycling/Reuse-and-Repurpose))

Not sure where something goes? Check the City's [What Goes Where](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/What-Goes-Where) database or call Utilities Customer Service at (405) 297-2833 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.). ([Trash Services](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Water-Trash-Recycling/Trash-Services))

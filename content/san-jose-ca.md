# Junk Disposal in San Jose, CA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in San José, California, item by item. The City of San José runs a **no-cost curbside Junk Pickup** for owners and renters: book it through **San José 311**, list **up to 12 items**, and the crew comes on your regular collection day. That covers mattresses, couches, most appliances (including refrigerators and window AC units) and many electronics. **E-waste is against the law in your garbage or recycling cart**, and paint and chemicals go to the **free Santa Clara County household hazardous waste program** (appointment required) or a **PaintCare** drop-off. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call San José 311 at (408) 535-3500 before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | No-cost City Junk Pickup through San José 311 (no bed bugs or mold; cover it if rain is forecast), or retailer take-back when a new mattress is delivered |
| Couch / furniture | No-cost City Junk Pickup, up to 12 items per request (each couch section counts as 1 item) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | No-cost City Junk Pickup: empty it, remove glass shelves, tape doors shut or remove them; window or portable AC units only (no central units, no commercial appliances) |
| TV / computer | Illegal in the garbage or recycling cart. City Junk Pickup (computers and monitors boxed with cords), or free County household hazardous waste drop-off by appointment |
| Paint / chemicals | Free County household hazardous waste drop-off by appointment ((408) 299-7300), or free PaintCare drop-off for house paint; dried latex paint can go in the garbage |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Book a **no-cost Junk Pickup** through **San José 311** (311.SanJoseCA.gov, the San José 311 app, 3-1-1 or (408) 535-3500). The City accepts mattresses and box springs as long as they **don't have bed bugs or mold**. If you're buying a new mattress, California law requires the retailer to **offer to take the old one back** at no additional cost.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: City Junk Pickup

1. If the mattress is still in good shape, consider donating it first. The City's San José Recycles guide suggests St. Vincent de Paul, which recycles mattresses rather than reselling them. ([Mattresses and Box Springs, San José Recycles](https://sanjoserecycles.org/guide/mattresses-box-springs/))
2. Request a pickup at [311.SanJoseCA.gov](https://311.sanjoseca.gov/), in the San José 311 app, by dialing 3-1-1 or by calling **(408) 535-3500**. A request can include **up to 12 items**, and the appointment falls on your regular recycling and garbage collection day. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))
3. Watch for a **separate appointment confirmation email within two business days** (it's different from the automated 311 email, so check your spam folder). If you need help sooner, contact your recycling collection company during business hours; look it up in the 311 My Collection Schedule. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))
4. If rain is forecast on pickup day, **cover the mattress or box spring with plastic or a tarp** to keep it dry. ([Junk Pickup list of acceptable items](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/69710/637656480205670000))
5. Set it out **no more than 24 hours before** your appointment, on the street next to the curb, the same way you set out your carts. **Not** in a red zone, blocking a hydrant or directly next to a parked car. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))

**Apartments and condos:** The program is available to both owners and renters in single-family homes and multi-family complexes. In a multi-family complex, check with your property manager for where to place items. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** set it out early or in the wrong spot. Early or improper set-out may mean your items aren't picked up, or a **citation**, because it may be considered illegal dumping. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))
- **Don't** dump it by the road. The City notes it's expensive for public agencies to pick up illegally dumped mattresses. ([San José Recycles](https://sanjoserecycles.org/guide/mattresses-box-springs/))
- **Don't** book a mattress with bed bugs or mold; the City's list says those aren't accepted. We couldn't find an official San José page that says what to do with an infested mattress, so call 311 for guidance.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Curbside:** Couches, armchairs, dressers, tables, futons, bed frames, entertainment centers and similar furniture are on the City's [list of acceptable Junk Pickup items](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/69710/637656480205670000). Book them through San José 311 the same way as a mattress (up to 12 items per request). Prep rules from that list:

- **Couch:** each section or piece counts as **1 item**.
- **Dressers, cabinets, armoires, shelves, vanities, entertainment centers:** remove any glass or mirror and put it in the trash.
- **Tables:** remove glass; bundle or tie leaves and legs (each bundle counts as 1 item, max 60 pounds).
- **Bed frames:** disassemble; bundle rails and slats (max 60 pounds per bundle). Headboard and footboard each count as a separate item.

The City notes that this list was **established May 27, 2020**. The list also has an "Other item not listed" entry; for those, the City says it will contact you for more information. All items must come from your own residence. ([Electronics](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/residents/how-to-recycle-right/electronics), [Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))

**Donate or reuse first:** The City encourages repairing, reupholstering or donating usable items to a thrift store, nonprofit, friends, neighbors or BuyNothing groups. The City-owned Environmental Innovation Center, 1608 Las Plumas Ave., is home to a **Habitat for Humanity ReStore**, where you can buy and donate new and gently used furniture, household products and building materials. ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup), [Environmental Innovation Center](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/energy/climate-smart-san-jose/energy/green-building/environmental-innovation-center))

**Items the Junk Pickup won't take:** Find a drop-off location at [RecycleStuff.org](https://www.recyclestuff.org/), self-haul to a landfill (the City lists nearby sites at SJEnvironment.org/Landfill-Transfer; call ahead), or hire a licensed or permitted hauler. If you hire a hauler, the City recommends asking for a **copy of the weight ticket** to help prevent illegal dumping. For a debris box, use a City-authorized hauler (SJEnvironment.org/AuthorizedHaulers). ([Junk Pickup](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/junk-pickup))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

The City's Junk Pickup accepts **refrigerators, freezers, wine refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, wall ovens, water heaters and air conditioners** at no cost. Book through San José 311. Prep rules from the City's [list of acceptable items](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/69710/637656480205670000):

- **Refrigerators:** empty all food; remove glass shelves and put them in the garbage; all doors must be unlocked and **securely taped shut** with duct tape (or similar), or **removed** and placed next to the item. **No commercial appliances.**
- **Freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, wall ovens:** tape doors shut and empty all contents.
- **Wine refrigerators:** tape doors shut, empty, and remove any glass doors.
- **Air conditioners:** **window or portable units only; no central units.** Wrap and secure the loose cord. Heaters: portable units only (no central units or air handlers).
- **Water heaters:** drain all water from the tank.

The City's list doesn't mention refrigerant (freon) removal by residents. For a central AC system or a commercial unit, which the program won't take, ask the installer or a licensed hauler, or call 311.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**It is against the law to put e-waste in your garbage or recycling containers** in San José. That includes TVs, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers and copiers, DVD and VCR players, stereos and video game consoles. ([Electronics](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/residents/how-to-recycle-right/electronics))

- **City Junk Pickup:** TVs (wrap and secure the loose cord); computers, monitors, laptops, printers, modems, cable boxes and DVD players **in a box with their cords and cables, max 60 pounds per box**. ([list of acceptable items](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/69710/637656480205670000))
- **Santa Clara County Household Hazardous Waste Program:** free drop-off for County residents **by appointment**, and e-waste is on its accepted list. See the appointment steps below. ([County HHW](https://hhw.santaclaracounty.gov/))
- **Other take-back sites:** search [RecycleStuff.org](https://www.recyclestuff.org/) or call the **Santa Clara County Recycling Hotline, (800) 533-8414** (live help Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.). ([Electronics](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/residents/how-to-recycle-right/electronics))

If a device still works, the City suggests repair, selling, a trade-in or donating it to an organization that accepts e-waste. Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you let them go.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

Never pour leftover hazardous materials down the sink, toilet or storm drain, or put them in the garbage or recycling. San José residents can dispose of household hazardous waste **for free** through the **Santa Clara County Household Hazardous Waste Program**. ([San José Recycles: Hazardous Waste](https://sanjoserecycles.org/what-to-do/hhw/), [City HHW page](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/recycling-garbage/residents/how-to-recycle-right/household-hazardous-waste))

- **Appointment required.** Schedule online at [hhw.santaclaracounty.gov](https://hhw.santaclaracounty.gov/drop-household-waste) or call **(408) 299-7300**. Bring **proof of residency**, put the waste in the back of your vehicle (trunk, truck bed or rear of an SUV), and don't bring pets. Containers aren't returned. If you're more than 15 minutes late, you may not be seen. ([Drop off household waste](https://hhw.santaclaracounty.gov/drop-household-waste))
- **Where and when:** San José's permanent HHW facility is at the City's Environmental Innovation Center. The County sends the exact drop-off address after you book, to prevent illegal dumping when sites are closed. Its [operating schedule](https://hhw.santaclaracounty.gov/drop-household-waste/household-hazardous-waste-operating-schedule) shows San José drop-off days on most **Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays**, with holiday closures (for example, no drop-off November 26 to 28, 2026). **Never leave waste outside the facility**; the City treats that as illegal dumping. ([Environmental Innovation Center](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/energy/climate-smart-san-jose/energy/green-building/environmental-innovation-center))
- **Accepted:** paints (dry or wet), petroleum products, polishes and gasoline; acids, bases, batteries and drain cleaners; poisons, pesticides, garden chemicals, ammonia and solvents; pool chemicals, hydrogen peroxide and other oxidizers; plus e-waste, propane, helium, small oxygen tanks, smoke detectors, fluorescent lamps and sharps. **Medications are no longer accepted** at the San José and San Martin facilities (since January 16, 2025). ([County HHW](https://hhw.santaclaracounty.gov/))

**Paint, step by step:**

- **Dried latex paint** is OK in the garbage. **All other paint, including spray paint,** is hazardous waste; take it to the County HHW program for free. ([Paint, San José Recycles](https://sanjoserecycles.org/guide/paint/))
- **PaintCare:** recycle unwanted house paint, primer, stain or varnish for free at a PaintCare drop-off site. Latex and oil-based products are accepted. All sites take **up to five gallons per visit** (some take more); containers must be **5 gallons or smaller with original labels**. No leaking, unlabeled or empty containers, and **no spray paint**. ([Paint, San José Recycles](https://sanjoserecycles.org/guide/paint/), [PaintCare California](https://www.paintcare.org/states/california/))
- **Empty cans:** recycle them only when the residual paint is **completely dry and the lid is removed**. Wet brushes and rollers are hazardous waste; dry ones can go in the garbage. ([Paint, San José Recycles](https://sanjoserecycles.org/guide/paint/))

**Used motor oil:** curbside collection of used motor oil is available for residential homes, townhomes and mobile home parks. ([San José Recycles: Hazardous Waste](https://sanjoserecycles.org/what-to-do/hhw/))

Not sure where something goes? Use San José 311 ([311.SanJoseCA.gov](https://311.sanjoseca.gov/) or (408) 535-3500), search [RecycleStuff.org](https://www.recyclestuff.org/), or call the County Recycling Hotline at (800) 533-8414.

*Rules, schedules and accepted-item lists change. Confirm on the linked official page or by calling San José 311 at (408) 535-3500 before you set anything out or drive anywhere.*

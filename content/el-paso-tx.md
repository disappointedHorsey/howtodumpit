# Junk Disposal in El Paso, TX: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in El Paso, Texas, item by item. The City of El Paso gives you two main options. You can **book a fee-based special pickup** at the curb by calling Environmental Services, or you can **drop items off at one of five Citizen Collection Stations** if you're an El Paso resident with a pass. **TVs and computers** go to a Collection Station or the landfill, not your blue bin, and **paint and household chemicals** go to a Collection Station, with a monthly limit. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call Environmental Services at **(915) 212-6000** (or 311) before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Book a City special pickup ($35 for up to 5 cubic yards, plus tax) or drop it at a Citizen Collection Station |
| Couch / furniture | Book a City special pickup or drop it at a Citizen Collection Station; give usable pieces to the City's free Country Store |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | The City's special pickup takes appliances only if they're empty; call (915) 212-6000 to ask about freon units first |
| TV / computer | Drop it at a Citizen Collection Station or the Greater El Paso Landfill; it doesn't go in the blue bin or the special pickup |
| Paint / chemicals | Take it to a Citizen Collection Station (up to 5 gallons of paint and motor oil a month); free reused paint is available there too |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** The City of El Paso lists **mattresses and box springs** as accepted items for its **fee-based special pickup**. Call Environmental Services at **(915) 212-6000** to book it. You can also take a mattress yourself to one of the City's **Citizen Collection Stations** or to the **Greater El Paso Landfill**, following each site's rules. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

### Step by Step: City Special Pickup

1. **Call to book.** Call Environmental Services at **(915) 212-6000** between **7 a.m. and 2 p.m., Monday through Friday**.
2. **Know the fee.** The City lists **$35.00 for 5 cubic yards or less** and **$7.00 for each additional cubic yard**. **Tax is added** to all charges. Ask how you'll be billed when you call.
3. **Set it out on time.** Put your items at the **curb in front of your home by 6 a.m.** on your scheduled pickup day.
4. **Give it space.** Keep items **at least 5 feet** from your garbage and recycling bins, mailbox, fence, wall, water meter, telephone box, parked car, low-hanging trees or anything else in the way. Don't block the sidewalk or traffic.

([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Bed bugs or soiled mattresses:** We couldn't find an official City rule about bagging mattresses. Ask Environmental Services when you book.

**Dropping it off yourself:** See the Citizen Collection Station rules in the furniture section below.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Special pickup:** The City's fee-based special pickup accepts **furniture, carpet, doors, lumber, pallets, barbecues (fuel and charcoal removed), lawnmowers (fuel and oil removed) and brush**, with the same fee and set-out rules as mattresses above. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Not accepted at the special pickup:** anything with a title (cars, motorcycles, trailers, boats), commercial or industrial material, compressed gas cylinders, recyclables, dirt, rocks and concrete, tires, hazardous materials and used electronics.

**Citizen Collection Stations:** El Paso residents can drop off **trash, bulky items, household hazardous waste and recyclables** at any of the City's five stations. ([City of El Paso: Collection Stations](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/collection-stations))

- **Request a pass first** using the City's [Citizen Collection Station pass request](https://elpasoesd.net/passes/pass_request.htm).
- **Bring a current water bill and a valid ID** with an address that matches the water bill. This applies to everyone, including active-duty military and veterans.
- **Limit of 4 visits a month.** Only **single-axle trailers up to 8 feet** long are allowed.
- **No construction or demolition material**, and no commercial businesses or contractors.
- **Hours:** **8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Tuesday through Saturday.** Stations may close or change hours in wind, rain or snow, so call (915) 212-6000 to check. Closed New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve.

**Station locations:**

| Northeast | 9171 Stahala Dr, El Paso, TX 79924 |
| Central | 2492 Harrison Ave, El Paso, TX 79930 |
| Westside | 121 Atlantic Rd, El Paso, TX 79922 |
| Mission Valley | 1034 Pendale Rd, El Paso, TX 79907 |
| Eastside | 3500-3516 Confederate Dr, El Paso, TX 79936 |

([City of El Paso: Collection Stations](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/collection-stations))

**Still usable?** The City runs a free **Country Store** inside the Citizen Collection Station at **3510 Confederate Dr**, where residents can drop off and pick up gently used items such as **appliances and furniture**. A water bill with a matching ID is needed to enter, and residents can take up to 5 items a month. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Taking it to the landfill:** The **Greater El Paso Landfill** is at **2600 Darrington Rd, Clint, TX 79928**, open **7 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Saturday** (closed Sunday). Haulers need a City hauler permit to deliver loads. We couldn't find landfill fees for residents on the City's page, so call (915) 212-6000 before you go. ([City of El Paso: Environmental Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Special pickup:** The City lists **appliances** as accepted items for its fee-based special pickup, but they **must be empty**. The same fee and set-out rules apply as for mattresses. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Freon:** The City's pages don't say how fridges, freezers or air conditioners that still hold freon are handled, or whether they cost extra. **Call Environmental Services at (915) 212-6000 before you book** or drop off a freon appliance. Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself.

**Drop-off:** Citizen Collection Stations take bulky items from El Paso residents with a pass, water bill and matching ID. We couldn't confirm on the City's page whether every station takes freon appliances, so call first. ([City of El Paso: Collection Stations](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/collection-stations))

**Still working?** Gently used appliances can go to the City's free Country Store at 3510 Confederate Dr (see the furniture section above).

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Don't put electronics in your blue bin.** The City says e-waste belongs at a Citizen Collection Station. ([City of El Paso: Recycle Right El Paso](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/recycle)) Used electronics are also **not accepted** at the special bulky pickup. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Where to take them:** The City accepts used electronics at its **Citizen Collection Stations** and at the **Greater El Paso Landfill**. It takes:

- Televisions and cameras
- Radios and portable music players
- Cell phones, telephones and fax machines
- VCR and DVD players
- Computers and accessories such as monitors, keyboards, scanners, printers, photocopiers and webcams
- Other consumer electronics

**Not accepted:** commercial electronics and anything that's leaking. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Manufacturer programs:** Texas law requires TV and computer makers to offer recycling to consumers. The state's Electronics Recycling page links to the Texas Recycles Computers program (free computer recycling) and the Texas Recycles TVs program, where you can find your brand's options. Delete any private information from hard drives first. ([TCEQ: Electronics Recycling](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/p2/recycle/electronics))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Citizen Collection Stations** take **household hazardous waste** from El Paso residents with a pass, a current water bill and a matching ID. You can bring **up to 5 gallons of paint and motor oil a month**. ([City of El Paso: Collection Stations](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/collection-stations)) The City's page doesn't publish a full list of accepted chemicals, so call (915) 212-6000 if you're unsure about an item.

**Don't take these to the landfill.** The Greater El Paso Landfill **prohibits** automotive products (gasoline, antifreeze, motor oil, used oil filters and lead-acid batteries), liquid waste, regulated hazardous or radioactive waste, regulated asbestos, PCB items such as transformers and ballasts, and Class 1 industrial waste. ([City of El Paso: Environmental Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services))

**Hazardous items aren't allowed** in the City's special bulky pickup either. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Free paint and reusable products:** The City gives away **free reused paint** at all Citizen Collection Stations, in 5-gallon containers, first come, first served. Colors are mixed, so you can't choose a color. You must be an El Paso resident and fill out the City's paint request form, with a limit of 5 gallons per visit. Stations also offer **usable household products** dropped off by others, such as cleaners, wood stain, paint thinner, antifreeze and adhesives, up to 10 products per person with ID and a signature. ([City of El Paso: Community Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services/community-services))

**Latex paint:** We couldn't find an official City rule on drying out latex paint for the trash. Ask Environmental Services or bring it to a Collection Station within the monthly limit.

Questions? Call El Paso Environmental Services at **(915) 212-6000** or dial **311**. ([City of El Paso: Environmental Services](https://www.elpasotexas.gov/environmental-services))

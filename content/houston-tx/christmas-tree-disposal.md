# Christmas Tree Recycling in Houston, TX (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** The City of Houston recycles natural Christmas trees at drop-off sites after the holidays, but it had not posted 2026-27 dates or sites when we checked on November 25, 2026. Last season, bare trees were taken from December 26, 2025, through January 23, 2026, at 20 locations. If you'd rather not haul the tree yourself, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 25, 2026, against official City of Houston Solid Waste Management pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of Houston Holiday Tree Recycling {#tree-recycling}

- **2026-27 dates: not published** (checked November 25, 2026). The latest official notice is the December 22, 2025, release for last season. ([City of Houston: Tree Recycling Release, December 22, 2025](https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/press-12222025.html))
- **Last season (2025-26):** drop-off ran from Friday, December 26, 2025, through Friday, January 23, 2026, at 20 recycling locations. Trees were turned into mulch and compost for parks, community gardens and other green spaces.
- **Prep:** remove all lights, ornaments, tinsel, wire, nails and stands. Flocked, painted or artificial trees can't be recycled.
- **The catch:** the release names drop-off sites only, with no curbside tree program, so you'll need to drive the tree to a site once the City posts this season's list.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Christmas Eve (Thursday, December 24, 2026) and Christmas Day (Friday, December 25, 2026) are on the City's 2026 holiday schedule, and New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, 2027) is on the 2027 schedule. The City had not posted how routes shift that week when we checked; use the HTX Collects app or 311 for your address. Heavy trash (bulk and tree waste) is collected in December for eligible City curbside addresses, on your assigned week. ## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When does Houston Christmas tree recycling start?

The City hadn't posted 2026-27 dates as of November 25, 2026. Last season, drop-off started Friday, December 26, 2025, and ran through Friday, January 23, 2026.

### Where can I drop off my Christmas tree in Houston?

Last season, at 20 locations, including the Central, Kirkpatrick, Sommermeyer, N. Main, Southwest and Sunbeam neighborhood depositories, Westpark and Kingwood. Check the City's site for this season's list.

### Does Houston pick up Christmas trees at the curb?

The City's tree recycling release lists drop-off sites only. Call 311 to ask about your address.

### Can I recycle a flocked or artificial tree in Houston?

No. The City says flocked, painted and artificial trees can't be recycled.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Houston?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

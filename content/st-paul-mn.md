# Junk Disposal in St. Paul, MN: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in **St. Paul** (officially the City of Saint Paul), item by item. If you live in a home with **1 to 4 units**, the City's coordinated garbage service includes **12 bulky item pickups per garbage cart per year**, and since July 1, 2026, you no longer need to schedule them. Mattresses are picked up **free and unlimited** through the City's recycling partner. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Questions? Call Saint Paul Public Works at **651-266-6101**.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Free, unlimited mattress recycling pickup (book with Second Chance Recycling) |
| Couch / furniture | Bulky item pickup on your collection day (12 a year per garbage cart included) |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Bulky item pickup, emptied of food and liquids |
| TV / computer | Bulky item pickup (Ramsey County's hazardous waste site doesn't take electronics) |
| Paint / chemicals | Ramsey County Environmental Center in Roseville, or a paint drop-off store |
| Christmas tree | City tree collection January 2-15, or a Ramsey County yard waste site |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Book a free pickup through the City's **mattress recycling program** with Second Chance Recycling. There's no limit, it's open to all residential properties, and it doesn't use up any of your 12 bulky items. ([City of Saint Paul: Mattress Recycling](https://www.stpaul.gov/mattressrecycling))

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Mattress Recycling Pickup

1. **Book it.** Homes with 1 to 4 units schedule directly with [Second Chance Recycling](https://www.secondchancerecyclingmn.com/curbside-collection-program/), or email citycurbside@emerge-mn.org or call 612-787-3770 with scheduling questions.
2. **What's accepted:** mattresses, futon mattresses, mattress toppers, box springs and metal bed frames. Mechanical beds, headboards and footboards aren't part of the recycling program.
3. **Label it.** Attach a sign saying the mattress is for the recycling program.
4. **Set it out by 6 a.m.** on the front boulevard or along the alley, visible and easy to reach. On your regular garbage day, keep it away from your carts so it isn't picked up as garbage.
5. **Apartments (5+ units):** property owners or managers book free mattress collection through the City's rental-property partner; tenants should ask their owner or manager.

**Bed bugs:** We did not find a City rule for wrapping bed-bug or soiled mattresses. Ask Public Works at 651-266-6101 before you set one out.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Couches, tables, chairs, dressers and other furniture** go out as **bulky items** on your regular collection day. No appointment is needed: put the item next to (not leaning against) your garbage cart by **6 a.m.**, label it "bulky," and empty it of food, liquids, gas and batteries. The driver tags it and a separate truck collects it within **two business days**. ([City of Saint Paul: Bulky Item Collection](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/garbage-and-recycling/residential-collection/bulky-item-collection))

- **How many:** homes with 1 to 4 units (or properties that opted into City garbage service) get **12 bulky items per garbage cart per calendar year**, with no monthly limit. Extra items cost **$30 each plus tax** on your garbage bill.
- **What counts as one:** about 1 cubic yard (3 x 3 x 3 feet). A sleeper couch counts as 2, each sectional piece counts as 1, and 4 kitchen chairs count as 1.
- **Every-other-week garbage:** set items out on your regular pickup week, not your off week.
- **Watch the 10-foot rule:** anything within 10 feet of your garbage cart may be collected and counted toward your total.
- **Not accepted as bulky items:** construction debris and tires.

**Apartments and condos:** the City doesn't coordinate garbage for buildings with **5 or more units**, so tenants can't use City bulky pickup. Your building's private hauler may offer it; ask your landlord or property manager first.

### Outside City Limits?

This guide covers the **City of Saint Paul** only. Neighboring cities run their own garbage and bulky item programs, so check your own city's website. Ramsey County's free yard waste sites are open to all **Ramsey County residents** (bring proof of residency), and some are also open to Washington County residents. ([Ramsey County: Yard Waste](https://www.ramseycountymn.gov/residents/recycling-waste/collection-sites/yard-waste)) Across the river, Minneapolis has different rules: see our [Minneapolis guide](/minneapolis-mn/). Apartment, condo and HOA communities with a **private hauler** should ask their property manager or HOA.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, central air units, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, water heaters and microwaves** all count as **bulky items** and go out the same way as furniture. Remove all food from fridges, and drain water heaters and water softeners first. ([Bulky Item Collection](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/garbage-and-recycling/residential-collection/bulky-item-collection))

**Freon / refrigerants:** Never cut the lines or release refrigerant yourself; the US EPA says the final disposer in the disposal chain is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**TVs (flat-screen, CRT, console and rear-projection), computers, printers, DVD and VHS players and gaming consoles** are **bulky items** in Saint Paul, so they go out next to your garbage cart and count toward your 12 a year. ([Bulky Item Collection](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/garbage-and-recycling/residential-collection/bulky-item-collection)) Ramsey County's household hazardous waste site does **not** take electronics. ([City of Saint Paul: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/recycling-and-waste-reduction/household-hazardous-waste)) Wipe personal data before you let a device go.

**Holiday lights and cords:** from November 30, 2026, to January 18, 2027, you can drop off string lights and electrical cords at any Saint Paul Public Library, Saint Paul Regional Water Services (1900 Rice St.) or the Public Works Solid Waste & Recycling facility (60 W. Sycamore). Never put them in your recycling cart. ([City of Saint Paul: Holiday & Seasonal Disposal](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/garbage-and-recycling/holiday-seasonal-disposal))

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Household hazardous waste** (paint, stains, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, aerosols, fluorescent bulbs, rechargeable batteries, used oil, gasoline and propane tanks) goes to the **Ramsey County Environmental Center**, 1700 Kent St., Roseville. It's open **Tuesday to Friday, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.**, and **Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.**; closed Sunday, Monday, County holidays and Christmas Eve. Ramsey County calls its household hazardous waste sites free. Empty containers (except propane tanks) can go in the trash. ([Ramsey County: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.ramseycountymn.gov/residents/recycling-waste/collection-sites/household-hazardous-waste))

**Paint:** the City also points residents to hardware stores that take leftover latex and oil-based paint; use the paint drop-off locator on the City's page to find one. ([City of Saint Paul: Household Hazardous Waste](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/recycling-and-waste-reduction/household-hazardous-waste))

**Rule of thumb:** if a label says poison, danger, warning or caution and there's product left inside, it can't go in your garbage cart.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Saint Paul collects **one live holiday tree per household** (one per unit in 1-4 unit homes) from **January 2 to 15** each year. Take off all decorations and the stand and lay the tree next to your garbage cart on your regular collection day; it's picked up by the end of the next business day. Don't stand it upright in the snow, lean it against the cart or block your carts. Artificial trees, and live trees over 6 feet and 20 pounds, aren't included: schedule a bulky item pickup for those. ([City of Saint Paul: Holiday & Seasonal Disposal](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/garbage-and-recycling/holiday-seasonal-disposal))

**Drop-off:** You can also take a stripped tree to a **Ramsey County yard waste site** that takes trees and branches for free: Frank and Sims (1150 Sims Ave.), Midway (1943 Pierce Butler Route), Arden Hills (1881 Hudson Rd.) or White Bear Township (5900 Sherwood Rd.). Battle Creek and Summit Hill don't take wood waste. Winter hours (December to March) are **Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.**, and **Sunday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.**; sites are closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day, and closed December 1-4, 2026, before winter hours start December 5. Artificial trees and wreaths aren't accepted. ([Ramsey County: Yard Waste](https://www.ramseycountymn.gov/residents/recycling-waste/collection-sites/yard-waste))

**Wreaths and garland:** bag them and put them in your garbage cart; holiday greenery isn't accepted as yard waste.

**Holiday collection:** garbage and recycling run one day late in any week with a holiday on or before your collection day. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026, so Friday collection moves to Saturday, December 26; New Year's Day is Friday, January 1, 2027, so Friday collection moves to Saturday, January 2. ([2026 Collection Calendar (PDF)](https://www.stpaul.gov/sites/default/files/public-works/2026-collection-calendar.pdf); [2027 Collection Calendar (PDF)](https://www.stpaul.gov/sites/default/files/public-works/2027-collection-calendar.pdf))

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Does St. Paul pick up bulky items for free?

Yes, up to a point. Homes with 1 to 4 units get 12 bulky item pickups per garbage cart each year as part of City garbage service. After that, each extra item costs $30 plus tax.

### Do I have to schedule bulky item pickup in St. Paul?

Not anymore. Since July 1, 2026, you just label the item "bulky" and set it next to your garbage cart by 6 a.m. on your collection day. Mattresses are different: book those with Second Chance Recycling.

### How do I get rid of a mattress in St. Paul?

Schedule it with Second Chance Recycling, the City's mattress partner. It's free, there's no yearly cap, and it's separate from your bulky item allowance.

### Where do I take paint and chemicals in St. Paul?

To the Ramsey County Environmental Center, 1700 Kent St., Roseville, Tuesday to Saturday. Many hardware stores also take leftover paint.

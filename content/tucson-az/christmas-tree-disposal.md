# Christmas Tree Recycling in Tucson, AZ (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** Take your bare tree to one of 10 City of Tucson TreeCycle sites between Saturday, December 26, 2026, and Monday, January 18, 2027. The City will not collect Christmas trees from curbs or alleys, so if you can't get the tree to a site, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 19, 2026, against official City of Tucson Environmental Services pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: City of Tucson TreeCycle Program {#treecycle}

- **2026-27 dates: published.** TreeCycle runs from Saturday, December 26, 2026, through Monday, January 18, 2027, at sites across Tucson and Oro Valley. ([City of Tucson: TreeCycle](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services/Recycling-Services/TreeCycle))
- **Prep:** remove everything from the tree, including the stand, lights, ornaments, wire and tinsel. The City grinds the trees for compost.
- **Not accepted:** other green waste, and commercial tree-lot overages (except at Los Reales Sustainability Campus at posted landfill rates).
- **Tip from the City:** "Tree Pool" and bring your neighbor's tree too, so there are fewer trips.
- **The catch:** there is no curbside or alley pickup for Christmas trees in Tucson. You have to drive the tree to a site yourself.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026: there is no collection that day, and Friday customers are collected one day late (Saturday, December 26). Monday to Thursday routes don't change. Los Reales Sustainability Campus is closed on Christmas Day. ([City of Tucson: Holiday Collection Schedule](https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Environmental-Services/Residential-Services/Holiday-Collection-Schedule)) The New Year's Day 2027 schedule was not posted as of November 19, 2026. Apartment, commercial and outside-city-limits customers should check with their own hauler, which may follow a different holiday schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Does Tucson pick up Christmas trees at the curb?

No. The City of Tucson says it will not collect Christmas trees from curbs and alleys. Take the tree to a TreeCycle site instead.

### When is Tucson TreeCycle 2026?

From Saturday, December 26, 2026, through Monday, January 18, 2027.

### Where can I drop off my Christmas tree in Tucson?

At any of the 10 TreeCycle sites, including Joaquin Murrieta Park, Randolph Golf Course, Todd M. Harris Sports Complex, Tucson Rodeo Grounds and Los Reales Sustainability Campus.

### Is Tucson trash pickup different during Christmas week 2026?

Only Friday routes change. Friday, December 25, 2026, collections happen on Saturday, December 26.

### Who can haul away my Christmas tree in Tucson?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.

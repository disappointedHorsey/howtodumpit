# Junk Disposal in Louisville, KY: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Louisville, item by item. Every option links to the official Louisville Metro page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or contact Metro311 before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Large Item Pickup by appointment (Urban Services District) or free drop-off at the Waste Reduction Center (up to 4 large items per day) |
| Couch / furniture | Large Item Pickup (up to 4 items per appointment, once per week) or Waste Reduction Center |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Not allowed in Large Item Pickup; take up to 4 items to the Waste Reduction Center for free |
| TV / computer | Not allowed in Large Item Pickup; Waste Reduction Center takes up to 3 electronic items per day free from residents |
| Paint / chemicals | Oil-based or lead paint and other household chemicals to Haz Bin (free for residents); dried-out latex paint can go in the garbage |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** If you live in the **Urban Services District**, book a **Large Item Pickup** appointment for up to **four** large items (mattresses and box springs are on the accepted list). Or haul it yourself to the **Waste Reduction Center**, which takes up to **4 large household items per day free** from Louisville/Jefferson County residents.

<!--LISTINGS-->

### Step by Step: Large Item Pickup

1. Check whether the mattress is still usable. The city suggests donating, giving away or selling usable items first, and names Habitat ReStore, Goodwill and St. Vincent de Paul as places to check.
2. [Schedule an appointment](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/junk-and-bulk-trash-disposal) for up to **4 large items**. You can book up to **once per week**, and an email address is required.
3. You'll get a **pending** email with a tentative date (the city says typically within 5 days), then a **confirmation** email two days before your appointment listing the approved items.
4. Set out only the approved items, in the same spot as your garbage, **after 4 p.m. the day before** and **by 6 a.m. on the day** of your appointment. Keep them clear of vehicles, sidewalks and streets.

### What Not to Do

- **Don't** set out bags or boxes of small items; they won't be collected.
- **Don't** break items into smaller pieces. A multi-piece item like a bed frame or sectional still counts as one item.
- **Don't** set out items without an appointment or from another property. The city takes reports of unscheduled set-outs through Metro311 under "Trash Complaints and Violations."

### Outside the Urban Services District?

Large Item Pickup is only for the Urban Services District. If you live elsewhere in Jefferson County, the city says to contact your waste hauler about large-item collection. The Waste Reduction Center is open to Louisville/Jefferson County residents either way.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

Couches, chairs, tables, buffets, beds, dressers, floor lamps, cribs, playhouses and carpet rolls are on Louisville's accepted **Large Item Pickup** list. The same rules apply as for mattresses: book an appointment, up to **4 items**, once per week, set out after 4 p.m. the day before and by 6 a.m. on the day. Metal items such as washers, dryers, stoves, hot water tanks, bicycles and bed frames are also accepted (but nothing containing Freon). Up to 4 tires per household are accepted if they're set apart from other items. ([Junk and Bulk Trash Disposal](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/junk-and-bulk-trash-disposal))

Construction and demolition debris (lumber, drywall, flooring) is **not** allowed in garbage or Large Item Pickup. Take it to the Waste Reduction Center, where vehicle-load fees apply (for example, **$60** for a pickup truck, van, box truck or trailer up to 10 feet, per the city's price list). The center does not take concrete, bricks, shingles, rocks, mulch or dirt. ([Waste Reduction Center](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/waste-reduction-center))

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

Refrigerators, air conditioners and other appliances that contain (or once contained) **Freon** are **not** accepted in Large Item Pickup. Residents can take **up to 4** of them to the **Waste Reduction Center** at 636 Meriwether Avenue (entrance on Bland Street) **for free**. Fees apply to loads over the four-item limit. ([Junk and Bulk Trash Disposal](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/junk-and-bulk-trash-disposal))

Non-Freon appliances such as washers, dryers, stoves and hot water tanks can go out on a Large Item Pickup appointment as metal items.

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

Electronics (TVs, computers, microwaves and similar) are **not** accepted in Large Item Pickup. Recycle them at the **Waste Reduction Center**, which takes **up to 3 electronic items per day free** from residents. Business loads over three items are **$0.60 per pound** and nonprofit loads are **$0.30 per pound**, by appointment. ([Waste Reduction Center](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/waste-reduction-center))

Hours: Tuesday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. and 1 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. (closed for lunch); Saturday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Trucks with trailers must arrive at least 15 minutes before closing. Payment is debit or credit card only. Check the city page for holiday closings, which include Derby Day and Thanksgiving week. Wipe personal data from phones and computers before you drop them off.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**Haz Bin** at **7501 Grade Lane** takes household hazardous materials **free** from Louisville/Jefferson County residents, **Tuesday to Saturday, 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.** (holiday closings are on the city page). Accepted examples include oil-based and lead paint, spray paint, stains and varnishes, solvents and mineral spirits, automotive fluids, gasoline, batteries, fluorescent and LED bulbs, pesticides and other lawn chemicals, pool chemicals, propane tanks (grill and camping size), mercury thermometers, smoke detectors, Freon, and asbestos floor tiles under 25 lbs. **Not** accepted: business waste, containers over 5 gallons or 40 pounds, more than 25 lbs of asbestos, ammunition, expired medication and **latex paint**. Don't put chemicals in trash bags, and don't use the on-site dumpsters. ([Haz Bin](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/hazardous-materials-disposal-haz-bin))

**Latex paint:** The city says there is no longer a latex paint recycling program. To get rid of used or opened latex paint, let small amounts dry out with the lid off in a well-ventilated area away from kids and pets, then put the dried can in a tightly sealed plastic bag in your garbage. The metal lid can be recycled. Habitat ReStore at 4044 Taylorsville Road accepts **new, unopened** paint with a complete label only. ([Haz Bin: Paint disposal](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/public-works/services/hazardous-materials-disposal-haz-bin))

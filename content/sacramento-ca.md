# Junk Disposal in Sacramento, CA: Mattresses, Couches, Furniture, Appliances, TVs & Electronics, Paint & Hazardous Waste

Here's how to legally get rid of bulky junk in Sacramento, California, item by item. If you're a City of Sacramento residential trash customer, you get **two free Household Junk Pickups** a year (February through October), **two more free Appliance and E-Waste Pickups** that run all year, and a **free dump coupon** mailed each June. **Paint and household chemicals** go free to the City's household hazardous waste facility at 8491 Fruitridge Road. Every option links to the official page we checked. Where we couldn't verify something, we say so. Check the linked page or call 311 (**916-264-5011**) before you set anything out.

## Quick Answer by Item

| Item | Best Verified Option |
|---|---|
| Mattress | Free Household Junk Pickup (February through October), your dump coupon, or free drop-off at a Sacramento County facility |
| Couch / furniture | Free Household Junk Pickup (2 a year); a paid special pickup in November, December and January |
| Fridge / AC (freon) | Free Appliance and E-Waste Pickup, up to 2 appliances per appointment, all year |
| TV / computer | Free Appliance and E-Waste Pickup (up to 4 items), or the City's HHW facility; never in your garbage or recycling container |
| Paint / chemicals | Free at the City HHW facility, 8491 Fruitridge Road (Tuesday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.), or a PaintCare retailer |
| Christmas tree | Cut to fit the organics cart; last season's street Claw pickup ended February 1, 2026 |

## Mattresses and Box Springs {#mattresses}

**Mattress short answer:** Mattresses are on the accepted list for the City's **Household Junk Pickup**. City residential customers get two free appointments a year, from February through October. Request one through 311 online, the Sac311 app, or by calling 3-1-1 or 916-264-5011. ([City of Sacramento: Household Junk Pickup](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/service_requests/household_junk_pickup))

<!--LISTINGS-->

**November through January:** Free junk appointments stop in mid-October for leaf season and start again in February. Until then, see the paid special service pickup in the furniture section below, use your dump coupon, or take the mattress to a County drop-off site.

**County drop-off:** Sacramento County lists mattresses and box springs as free residential drop-off items at Kiefer Landfill and the North Area Recovery Station, as long as the load holds only free items. ([Sacramento County: Free Residential Drop Off Items](https://wmr.saccounty.gov/us/en/residential-services/free-drop-off-locations/free-drop-off-items.html))

**Bed bugs:** We couldn't find an official City of Sacramento rule about wrapping or bagging a mattress with bed bugs. Ask 311 when you book your pickup.

## Couches and Furniture {#furniture}

**Household Junk Pickup:** Each City residential customer can book two free appointments a year, from February through October. Tenants of single-family homes and duplexes can book too. Accepted items include furniture, mattresses, carpet, toys, pipe up to 3 feet, wood and lumber up to 6 feet (fencing taken apart), up to 4 tires off the rim, and yard waste kept apart from the junk. ([City of Sacramento: Household Junk Pickup](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/service_requests/household_junk_pickup))

1. **Book it.** Use 311 online, the Sac311 app, or call 3-1-1 or 916-264-5011. Name the items you'll set out. Appliances and e-waste need their own appointment.
2. **Set it out on time.** Put items on the street no earlier than 24 hours before your appointment and by 6 a.m. on the day. Early set-outs may be fined.
3. **Place it right.** Keep the pile on the public street (not the curb, an alley or a driveway), 6 feet from parked vehicles, without blocking the sidewalk or street. The pile can't be bigger than 4 by 4 by 9 feet. Put loose items in cardboard boxes.
4. **Cancel in time.** Cancel or reschedule by 5 p.m. two business days before. If you don't, the appointment still counts as one of your free pickups.

**Not accepted:** hazardous waste, electronic waste, paint, gas canisters, explosives, construction debris such as dirt, rock and concrete, tires on rims, and treated wood waste such as fence posts and decking.

**Paid pickups:** If you've used both free appointments, or you need a pickup during leaf season (November, December or January), call City Utility Billing at 916-808-5454. The City says a special service pickup has a **minimum fee of $39.48**.

**Dump coupon:** City Recycling and Solid Waste customers are mailed a free dump coupon with the yearly Service Guide each June. It's good for one load of up to five cubic yards at the Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station (SRTS), 8491 Fruitridge Road (open Monday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.), or at the North Area Recovery Station, 4450 Roseville Road. Extra material is charged at SRTS posted rates. If you didn't get a coupon, ask 311 for one. ([City of Sacramento: Garbage](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/garbage_collection))

**Free Dump Day:** The City also holds Free Dump Days for residents with an address inside City limits. The City's page still shows the June 6, 2026 event and says a fall date will be posted, so check the [City of Sacramento: Free Dump Day](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/garbage_collection/free-dump-day) page for the next date.

### Outside City Limits?

The City's pickups are only for City of Sacramento residential customers. Inside the city, **tenants of buildings with five or more units** that aren't served by City collection must ask their landlord, property manager or commercial hauler to arrange a junk or appliance pickup. If you live in **unincorporated Sacramento County**, the County includes one bulky waste pickup of up to five cubic yards every 12 months in its curbside service; book it through County Customer Service at 916-875-5555, the online form or the SacGreenTeam app. ([Sacramento County: Bulky Waste Pickup](https://wmr.saccounty.gov/us/en/residential-services/bulky-item-collection.html)) Neighboring cities such as Elk Grove, Citrus Heights and Rancho Cordova are separate cities with their own rules, so check your own city's page. If you live in an HOA or gated community, check with your HOA or property manager first.

## Appliances (Fridges, Freezers, AC: Freon) {#appliances}

**Appliance and E-Waste Pickup:** City residential customers get two free appointments a year, separate from the junk pickups, and this program runs all year. Up to **two appliances** per appointment are collected at no charge. Accepted appliances include refrigerators and freezers (no food or food residue), air conditioners, dehumidifiers, ovens, stoves, dishwashers, washers, dryers, water heaters, furnaces, trash compactors and ceiling fans with the blades removed. Commercial appliances aren't accepted. ([City of Sacramento: Appliance and E-Waste Pickup](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/service_requests/appliance_pickup))

- **Booking:** 311 online, the Sac311 app, or 3-1-1 / 916-264-5011. Same set-out timing as junk pickups: no earlier than 24 hours before, out by 6 a.m.
- **Placement:** Put appliances in the street where they're visible and easy to reach. The City says to ignore the placement instructions in the paper Service Guide.
- **Minimum:** Each pickup must be at least 10 pounds.

**County drop-off:** Sacramento County takes up to two appliances a day from residents for free at Kiefer Landfill and the North Area Recovery Station, including refrigerators, microwaves, stoves, washers, dryers and dishwashers. ([Sacramento County: Free Residential Drop Off Items](https://wmr.saccounty.gov/us/en/residential-services/free-drop-off-locations/free-drop-off-items.html))

**Free Dump Day:** Refrigerators, air conditioners and other appliances with freon or compressed gas aren't accepted.

**Freon:** Never cut the lines or let the refrigerant out yourself. The US EPA says the final disposer of an appliance is responsible for recovering its refrigerant. ([US EPA: Appliance Disposal](https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal)) California also certifies appliance recyclers to remove hazardous materials. ([California DTSC: Certified Appliance Recycler Program](https://dtsc.ca.gov/certified-appliance-recycler-car-program/))

## TVs, Computers and Electronics (E-Waste) {#electronics}

**Never in your containers:** Batteries and e-waste aren't allowed in your garbage or recycling containers. ([City of Sacramento: Batteries and E-Waste](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Householdhazardouswaste/batteriesandewaste))

**Appliance and E-Waste Pickup:** Each appointment takes up to **four e-waste items**, such as TVs (CRT, LCD, LED, plasma and projection), monitors, desktop and laptop computers, printers, game consoles, stereo equipment, DVD players, cable boxes, vacuums and small kitchen appliances. Several small devices, like phones, can go in one box and count as one item. Take out any removable batteries. Not accepted: household batteries, fluorescent bulbs, smoke detectors, solar panels, items with built-in lithium batteries (such as hoverboards), and CDs or tapes. ([City of Sacramento: Appliance and E-Waste Pickup](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/service_requests/appliance_pickup))

**Drop-off:** You can also bring e-waste to the City HHW facility at 8491 Fruitridge Road. The City says fees may apply for small and medium e-waste such as cell phones, printers and stereos. Sacramento County lists TVs, computers and other electronics as free residential drop-off items at Kiefer Landfill and the North Area Recovery Station.

## Paint and Household Hazardous Waste {#hazardous}

**City HHW facility:** Drop off household hazardous waste for free at the City's facility inside the Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station. No charge for residential customers who follow the guidelines. ([City of Sacramento: Household Hazardous Waste Facilities](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Householdhazardouswaste/HHWfacilities))

| Site | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| City of Sacramento HHW Facility (at SRTS) | 8491 Fruitridge Road, Sacramento | Tuesday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Sacramento County North Area Recovery Station HHW | 4450 Roseville Road, North Highlands | Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.; County Customer Service 916-875-5555 |

- **Limit:** State law bars transporting more than 15 gallons or 125 pounds of hazardous materials per trip. Containers must be leak-proof and labeled.
- **Accepted:** most products labeled Danger, Caution, Warning, Flammable or Poison, including paint (oil-based or latex), thinners, cleaners, pesticides, pool chemicals, batteries, fluorescent tubes, motor oil, antifreeze and propane cylinders.
- **Not accepted:** asbestos, ammunition, explosives, compressed gas cylinders other than propane, medical waste other than sharps, radioactive materials, and business waste (businesses must call for an appointment).
- **Reuse Store:** Mostly full products in their original containers are given away free at the Reuse Store next to the HHW drop-off area.

The City also lists the City of Elk Grove Special Waste Collection Facility, 9255 Disposal Lane, Elk Grove. The City's web page and its printed Service Guide list different days and phone numbers for it, so check with the City of Elk Grove before you go.

**Paint:** You can take leftover paint to the City HHW facility, donate usable paint to the Reuse Store, or drop it off free at a PaintCare retailer such as Kelly-Moore, Dunn-Edwards or Sherwin-Williams. ([City of Sacramento: Paint Disposal](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Householdhazardouswaste/Paint)) We couldn't find an official City rule that lets you dry out latex paint and put it in the garbage, so take all paint to the HHW facility or a PaintCare site.

**Never in your containers:** Paint, batteries, chemicals, e-waste, oil, fluorescent bulbs and sharps are illegal to put in your garbage container. ([City of Sacramento: Garbage](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/public-works/recycling-solid-waste/Collectionservices/garbage_collection))

Questions? Call **311** (916-264-5011), City Utility Billing at **916-808-5454** for paid pickups, or Sacramento County Customer Service at **916-875-5555**.

## Christmas Trees {#christmas-trees}

**Christmas tree short answer:** The City of Sacramento says you can break down a holiday tree so it fits in your organics container on your regular weekly organics day. A seasonal "Claw" street pickup and free drop-off events also run after the holidays; 2026-27 Claw and event dates were not published as of November 10, 2026.

Flocked trees are accepted, but any tinsel, plastic, metal or other decorations must be removed from all trees. Keep trees and leaf piles out of storm drains, and clear of bike lanes where possible. Improper disposal of holiday trees on City streets is considered illegal dumping. ([Sacramento City Express: Free Ways to Get Rid of Your Holiday Tree](https://sacramentocityexpress.com/2025/12/22/here-are-free-ways-to-get-rid-of-your-holiday-tree/))

**Last season (2025-26):** The Department of Public Works leaf-season service let many residents place trees in the organics container or pile them in the street for pickup by "the Claw," up to five trees, on the collection calendar (the schedule can change with weather and pile size). The last day trees or yard waste could be placed in the street was February 1, 2026. Free drop-off events for up to five trees were listed at SMUD Corporation Yard, 6100 Folsom Boulevard (Saturday, January 3, 2026, 8 a.m.-3:30 p.m.); North Area Recovery Station, 4450 Roseville Road (January 3-4); Kiefer Landfill, 12701 Kiefer Boulevard (January 3-4); Elder Creek Recovery and Transfer, 8642 Elder Creek Road (January 3); and Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station, 8491 Fruitridge Road (January 3, and also December 26, 2025-January 8, 2026, Monday-Saturday 8 a.m.-5 p.m.).

If the tree will not fit in the cart and seasonal pickup has not started, a junk removal company listed on this page may offer Christmas tree pickup. HowToDumpIt does not haul anything. Other cities are in our [Christmas Tree Disposal](/christmas-tree-disposal/) guide.

# Christmas Tree Pickup in Jacksonville, FL (2026-27)

**Quick answer:** The City of Jacksonville collects natural Christmas trees with weekly yard waste. Strip off the stand, hangers and decorations and set the tree out by 6 a.m. on your yard waste day, outside your City cart. There is no separate holiday window, and the City's yard waste page lists no tree drop-off sites. If the tree is too big to handle, call one of the local junk removal companies below.

*Last verified November 16, 2026, against official City of Jacksonville pages, linked in each section below.*

## Free: Weekly Yard Waste Collection {#yard-waste}

- **What:** the City's Yard Waste page lists "Natural Christmas wreaths and trees (remove hangers, stands and all decorations)" as yard waste. ([City of Jacksonville: Yard Waste](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/yard-waste))
- **When:** weekly, on your yard waste day, out by 6 a.m. If garbage is collected the same day, keep the tree apart from the garbage.
- **Not in a cart:** don't put yard waste in any City-issued cart.
- **Size and weight:** the yard waste standards say no single item may weigh more than 40 pounds, and limbs and logs must be no more than 6 inches across. The page gives no separate size rule for Christmas trees, so cut a large tree down or ask the City first.
- **Questions:** call (904) 630-CITY (2489) or use MyJax.
- **The catch:** a big, heavy tree may need cutting before the crew will take it.

## Christmas Week 2026 {#christmas-week}

The City says waste collection changes only on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026, so Friday customers will be served on Saturday, December 26, 2026. Every other day runs as normal. ([City of Jacksonville: Holiday Schedules](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-administrative-services/solid-waste/holiday-schedules))

## What the City Doesn't Say {#not-covered}

The official Yard Waste page does not mention artificial or flocked trees, and we found no City Christmas tree drop-off program. Ask the City on (904) 630-CITY (2489) before you set either kind of tree out. Tree waste left by a tree surgeon or similar company working on your property is that company's job to remove, not the City's.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### When is Christmas tree pickup in Jacksonville?

Trees go out on your usual weekly yard waste day. The City has no special tree collection week.

### Does Jacksonville pick up trees during Christmas week 2026?

Yes. Only Friday routes move that week: Friday, December 25, 2026, routes are served on Saturday, December 26.

### Can I put my Christmas tree in the City cart?

No. The City doesn't allow yard waste in its carts, so set the tree beside the curb instead.

### Is there a Christmas tree drop-off in Jacksonville?

We didn't find one on official City pages as of November 16, 2026.

### Who can haul away a big Christmas tree in Jacksonville?

A local junk removal company can. Call one listed on this page and ask whether they take trees and what they charge. HowToDumpIt is an information guide and doesn't haul anything.
